  2. 2일차: VinWonders – Vinpearl Safari

    Morning at the open-range Safari zoo, afternoon at the VinWonders theme park through to the evening show.

    • VinWonders – Vinpearl Safari 650k–950k 09:00–19:00 Gành Dầu, Phú Quốc 온라인 티켓 찾기

  3. 3일차: South island – Hon Thom cable car

    Check out, head south to An Thoi for the sea cable car and Aquatopia water park, watch sunset at Sunset Town, then fly out (pick an evening flight).

    • Hon Thom cable car + Aquatopia 600k–750k 09:00–17:00 Ga An Thới, Phú Quốc 온라인 티켓 찾기
    • Sunset Town – Kiss of the Sea show 250k–500k ~8pm (varies) Sunset Town, An Thới 온라인 티켓 찾기
