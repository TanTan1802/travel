When is the best time to visit Vietnam?

The three regions have very different climates – here is a month-by-month guide to the best places to go.

업데이트: 2026.9

목차
  1. Three regions, three climates
  2. Where to go, month by month
  3. Tet and public holidays
  4. What to pack

Three regions, three climates

  • The North has four seasons: cold winters (Dec – Feb, below 5°C in the mountains), hot and rainy summers (Jun – Aug). The best months are Mar – May and Sep – Nov.
  • Central Vietnam is sunny from Feb – Aug; Sep – Dec is the rainy and typhoon season, with flooding in Hue, Hoi An and Quang Binh.
  • The South is warm all year: dry season Dec – Apr, rainy season May – Nov, usually with short afternoon showers.
  • The Central Highlands (Da Lat, Buon Ma Thuot) are cool; the dry season Nov – Apr is best.

Tet and public holidays

Lunar New Year (Tet – late January to mid-February) is the busiest time of year: flights and buses cost much more and need booking 1–2 months ahead; many eateries and shops close for the first 3–5 days.

The 30 April – 1 May and 2 September holidays are also packed at beaches near Hanoi and Saigon. Shift your dates by a few days for better prices and fewer crowds.

What to pack

  • Northern winter and mountains: a warm jacket, scarf and socks.
  • Central and South: sunscreen, a hat, light clothes and a compact raincoat.
  • Trekking in Sa Pa, Ha Giang or Pu Luong: shoes with good grip and insect repellent.
  • Temples and pagodas: clothes that cover shoulders and knees.
