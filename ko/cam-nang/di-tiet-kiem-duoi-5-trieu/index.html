What does 5 million VND get you?

The table adds two parts: 3 days and 2 nights on the ground at budget level (room, food, sights, local transport) and a budget return trip by the suggested transport from Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City. It uses the same formula as the cost section on each destination page, so the numbers always match the site.

Destinations close to your home city are usually much cheaper, because a bus or train replaces the flight.

How to keep costs down

  • Take long legs by sleeper bus or overnight train: you travel and save a night's room at the same time.
  • Book flights early and avoid weekends and public holidays; budget fares usually include cabin baggage only.
  • Stay in homestays, guesthouses or hostel dorms – many have a shared kitchen and rent out scooters.
  • Eat at local eateries and night markets; ask the price first where the menu has none.
  • Travel in the low season: rooms and tours are cheaper, but check the destination's weather first (see When to visit).
  • Travel in a group of 2 – 4 to split rooms, car hire and boat hire.

Plan around your own budget

The trip planner calculates costs for the number of days, travellers and spending level you choose; the home page has a 3-day budget filter to narrow destinations quickly.
