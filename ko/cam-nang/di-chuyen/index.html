Getting around Vietnam: planes, trains, buses or scooters?

How to travel between and within cities, with tips for booking cheaper tickets.

업데이트: 2026.9

목차
  1. Flights – fastest for long distances
  2. The Reunification Express – slow but scenic
  3. Buses, sleeper buses and limousine vans
  4. Getting around town

Flights – fastest for long distances

Main domestic airlines are Vietnam Airlines, Vietjet Air, Bamboo Airways and Vietravel Airlines. Hanoi – Saigon takes about 2 hours. Fares are lowest 3–6 weeks ahead, avoiding weekends and holidays.

Budget fares usually include only 7 kg of cabin baggage – adding checked bags when booking is cheaper than at the airport.

The Reunification Express – slow but scenic

The Hanoi – Saigon line is over 1,700 km and takes about 30–35 hours, stopping at Ninh Binh, Dong Hoi, Hue, Da Nang, Nha Trang and Phan Thiet. The Hue – Da Nang stretch over Hai Van pass is one of the most beautiful rail journeys around.

Choose a 4-berth air-conditioned sleeper for overnight legs. Buy official tickets at dsvn.vn or at stations.

Buses, sleeper buses and limousine vans

The cheapest and most common way to reach Sa Pa, Ha Giang, Ninh Binh, Da Lat, Mui Ne… 9–11-seat limousine vans are more comfortable and often pick up from hotels. Book via Vexere or directly with well-reviewed operators.

Getting around town

  • Ride-hailing apps Grab, Be and Xanh SM (electric): upfront prices, no haggling.
  • Hanoi and HCMC have metro/urban rail lines and cheap buses.
  • Renting a scooter: you need a licence valid in Vietnam, always wear a helmet and check whether your travel insurance covers scooter accidents.
  • Crossing the street: walk slowly and steadily – traffic flows around you; never suddenly run or step back.
