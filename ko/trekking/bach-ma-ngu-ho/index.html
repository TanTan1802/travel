출발
Bach Ma National Park gate (Phu Loc)
  • Five Lakes trail: five clear pools linked by small falls
  • Hai Vong Dai – views over Cau Hai lagoon and the sea
  • Primary forest and old French villas on the mountain

일별 일정

  1. Bach Ma summit and the Five Lakes

    Ride the park vehicle up near the summit and walk to Hai Vong Dai; descend to the Five Lakes trail (about 1.5 – 3 km), swim in the last pools and walk back to the car park. Add the Rhododendron waterfall trail if you have time.

도착: Bach Ma National Park gate

비용과 절차

  • National park entry about 60,000 – 65,000 VND per adult (indicative)
  • Shuttle to the summit hired per trip; price depends on group size

안전

  • There are forest leeches – wear long socks and use repellent
  • In the September – December storm season the summit road may close

트레킹 공통 안전 수칙

준비물

  • Grippy shoes (hiking boots or deep-tread trainers) and thick socks
  • A light raincoat, hat and sunscreen
  • 1.5 – 2 litres of water and snacks
  • Personal medicine, plasters and insect repellent
