출발
A Hmong village at the foot of the mountain (Mu Cang Chai)
  • A 2,913 m summit above the Mu Cang Chai terraces
  • Old forest, grassy clearings and morning clouds
  • Pair it with the rice harvest in September – October

일별 일정

  1. 1일차: Village – old forest – hut

    Moderate climbing through maize fields at first; after lunch the path gets steeper and denser in the forest, reaching a hut near the top by afternoon.

  2. 2일차: Lung Cung summit – descent

    Summit for sunrise and descend the same way to the village.

비용과 절차

  • Hmong porters and guides – book ahead via a homestay or operator

안전

  • Avoid July – August: heavy rain, landslides and muddy forest trails
  • Easy to get lost in the dense forest – stay with your guide
