Da Lat – Nha Trang in 5 days: highlands then sea · ダラット → ニャチャン · 4泊5日

  1. 1 日目: Central Da Lat

    Stroll Xuan Huong lake, ride the old train from Da Lat station, street food at the night market.

    • Xuan Huong lake – Lam Vien square 無料 終日営業 Trần Quốc Toản, Đà Lạt
    • Da Lat station – Trai Mat train 150k Trains 7:45am – 4pm 1 Quang Trung, Đà Lạt
    • Da Lat night market 無料 17:00–23:00 Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai, Đà Lạt
    + ダラットのスポットを追加（13）
    • Linh Phuoc Pagoda 無料 07:00–17:00 120 Tự Phước, Trại Mát
    • Cau Dat tea hills 50k 06:00–17:00 Xuân Trường, Đà Lạt
    • Strawberry farm 50k–100k 07:00–17:00 Thái Phiên / Vạn Thành, Đà Lạt
    • Hydrangea garden 30k 07:00–18:00 Trại Mát, Đà Lạt
    • Valley of Love 250k 07:00–17:00 7 Mai Anh Đào, Đà Lạt
    • Langbiang peak – jeep 200k–250k 07:00–17:00 Lạc Dương, Lâm Đồng
    • Cu Lan village 50k 07:00–17:00 Lạc Dương, Lâm Đồng
    • Central Highlands gong show 150k–250k 19:30–21:30 Khu du lịch Langbiang, Lạc Dương
    • Bao Dai Palace III 40k 07:00–17:00 Triệu Việt Vương, Đà Lạt
    • Chicken Church 無料 終日営業 Trần Phú, Đà Lạt
    • Crazy House 120k 08:30–19:00 3 Huỳnh Thúc Kháng, Đà Lạt
    • Van Thanh flower village 無料 終日営業 Vạn Thành, Đà Lạt
    • Da Lat persimmon garden 30k–50k 07:00–17:00 Trại Mát / Xuân Thọ, Đà Lạt

  2. 2 日目: Pine forest – Tuyen Lam lake – waterfall

    Robin cable car to Truc Lam monastery and Tuyen Lam lake, then the Datanla falls alpine coaster.

    • Robin cable car – Truc Lam Zen Monastery 100k–130k 07:30–17:00 Đèo Prenn, Đà Lạt
    • Tuyen Lam lake 無料 終日営業 Tuyền Lâm, Đà Lạt
    • Datanla falls – alpine coaster 50k–250k 07:00–17:00 Đèo Prenn, Đà Lạt

  3. ダラット → ニャチャン · バス / 車 ~2 時間 30 分 · 120k–300k

    3 日目: Down the pass to Nha Trang

    Morning coach down the Khanh Le pass to Nha Trang, then Po Nagar towers, a swim on Tran Phu beach and the night market.

    • Po Nagar Cham Towers 30k 06:00–17:30 2 Tháng 4, Vĩnh Phước
    • Tran Phu beach 無料 終日営業 Đường Trần Phú, Nha Trang
    • Nha Trang night market 無料 17:00–23:00 Trần Phú – Lý Tự Trọng, Nha Trang
    + ニャチャンのスポットを追加（6）
    • Long Son Pagoda 無料 07:00–17:00 22 Tháng 10, Phương Sơn
    • Hon Tam – Bai Tranh 無料 09:00–16:00 Vịnh Nha Trang
    • Binh Ba island – Nom beach 100k–150k 07:00–16:00 Cảng Ba Ngòi, Cam Ranh
    • Doc Let beach 0k–30k 終日営業 Ninh Hải, Ninh Hòa
    • Tram Trung hot springs 250k–400k 07:00–18:00 Ninh Phụng, Ninh Hòa オンラインでチケットを探す
    • Skylight rooftop bar 150k–300k 17:00–24:00 38 Trần Phú, Nha Trang オンラインでチケットを探す

  4. 4 日目: Hon Tre – VinWonders

    Cable car to the island, theme park and water park, then the evening water show.

    • Cable car + VinWonders Nha Trang 800k–950k 08:30–20:00 Ga cáp treo Vĩnh Nguyên, Nha Trang オンラインでチケットを探す
    • VinWonders water park 無料 09:00–18:00 Đảo Hòn Tre
    • Tata water music show 無料 ~7pm VinWonders, đảo Hòn Tre

  5. 5 日目: Hon Mun – mud bath

    Boat to Hon Mun for snorkelling, a mineral mud bath in the afternoon, then Cam Ranh airport.

    • Hon Mun island tour – snorkelling 250k–450k 08:00–16:00 Cảng Cầu Đá, Nha Trang オンラインでチケットを探す
    • I-Resort mud bath 300k–450k 07:00–19:00 Tổ 19, Vĩnh Ngọc オンラインでチケットを探す

ヒント

  • The Khanh Le pass is very winding – take motion-sickness tablets and avoid it in heavy rain.
  • Fly into Da Lat (Lien Khuong) and out of Nha Trang (Cam Ranh) to avoid backtracking.
