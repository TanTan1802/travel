Ninh Binh in a day: Trang An – Mua Cave – Hoa Lu · チャンアン – ニンビン · 日帰り

  1. Trang An – Hoa Lu – Mua Cave

    Morning boat ride through Trang An (about 3 hours), lunch of mountain goat and crispy rice, the Dinh and Le temples, then climb Mua Cave for sunset.

    • Trang An boat trip 250k 07:00–16:00 Trường Yên, Hoa Lư
    • Hoa Lu ancient capital 20k 07:00–18:00 Trường Yên, Hoa Lư
    • Mua Cave viewpoint 100k 06:00–19:00 Khê Hạ, Ninh Xuân, Hoa Lư
    + チャンアン – ニンビンのスポットを追加（9）
    • Bai Dinh Pagoda 60k 06:00–18:00 Gia Sinh, Gia Viễn
    • Tam Coc – Bich Dong 120k–200k 07:00–17:00 Văn Lâm, Ninh Hải, Hoa Lư
    • Van Long wetland 90k 07:00–17:00 Gia Vân, Gia Viễn
    • Thung Nham bird park 100k 07:00–18:00 Ninh Hải, Hoa Lư
    • Phat Diem stone cathedral 無料 08:00–17:00 Thị trấn Phát Diệm, Kim Sơn
    • Kim Son sedge-weaving village 無料 07:00–17:00 Kim Sơn, Ninh Bình
    • Tam Coc night street 無料 18:00–23:00 Đường Tam Cốc, Ninh Hải
    • Cuc Phuong National Park 60k 07:00–17:00 Cúc Phương, Nho Quan
    • Prehistoric Man Cave 無料 07:00–17:00 VQG Cúc Phương, Nho Quan
