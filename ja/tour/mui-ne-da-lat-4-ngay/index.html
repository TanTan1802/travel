概要

Red dunes, White Sand Dunes at sunrise and Fairy Stream in Mui Ne; then up to cool Da Lat for Tuyen Lam lake and Datanla falls.

カスタマイズ：行かない場所のチェックを外し、「スポットを追加」で追加 – 費用はすぐに再計算され、選択はこの端末に保存されます。

Mui Ne – Da Lat in 4 days: sand dunes then pine forests · ムイネー → ダラット · 3泊4日

  1. 1 日目: Mui Ne – red dunes

    Check in, walk Fairy Stream, sunset on the red dunes and the fishing village in the evening.

    • Fairy Stream 15k 06:00–18:00 Huỳnh Thúc Kháng, Mũi Né
    • Red Sand Dunes 無料 終日営業 Võ Nguyên Giáp, Mũi Né
    • Mui Ne fishing village 無料 Best 5–8am Huỳnh Thúc Kháng, Mũi Né
    + ムイネーのスポットを追加（8）
    • Kitesurfing lesson 1.2M–2M 08:00–17:00 Nguyễn Đình Chiểu, Hàm Tiến オンラインでチケットを探す
    • Ke Ga lighthouse 50k–100k 07:00–17:00 Tân Thành, Hàm Thuận Nam
    • Tien Thanh beach – Ong Dia rocks 無料 終日営業 Tiến Thành, Phan Thiết
    • Hon Rom beach – SUP 150k–250k 06:00–17:00 Hòn Rơm, Mũi Né
    • Phan Thiet fish-sauce village 無料 07:00–17:00 Phú Hài / Mũi Né
    • Doi Duong beach 無料 終日営業 Nguyễn Tất Thành, Phan Thiết
    • Ta Cu cable car – Reclining Buddha 250k 07:00–17:00 Thuận Quý, Hàm Thuận Nam
    • Ke Ga beach – Pink dunes 無料 終日営業 Tân Thành, Hàm Thuận Nam

  2. 2 日目: White dunes – Cham towers

    Jeep to the White Sand Dunes for sunrise, then the Poshanu Cham towers and the wine castle.

    • White Sand Dunes (Bau Trang) 20k 05:00–18:00 Hòa Thắng, Bắc Bình
    • Po Shanu Cham Towers 15k 07:00–17:00 Đồi Bà Nài, Phú Hài
    • RD Wine Castle 100k 07:30–17:00 Nguyễn Thông, Phú Hài

  3. ムイネー → ダラット · バス / 車 ~3 時間 · 140k–340k

    3 日目: Up to Da Lat

    Drive up the passes to Da Lat, stroll Xuan Huong lake, then the night market.

    • Xuan Huong lake – Lam Vien square 無料 終日営業 Trần Quốc Toản, Đà Lạt
    • Da Lat night market 無料 17:00–23:00 Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai, Đà Lạt
    + ダラットのスポットを追加（14）
    • Da Lat station – Trai Mat train 150k Trains 7:45am – 4pm 1 Quang Trung, Đà Lạt
    • Linh Phuoc Pagoda 無料 07:00–17:00 120 Tự Phước, Trại Mát
    • Cau Dat tea hills 50k 06:00–17:00 Xuân Trường, Đà Lạt
    • Strawberry farm 50k–100k 07:00–17:00 Thái Phiên / Vạn Thành, Đà Lạt
    • Hydrangea garden 30k 07:00–18:00 Trại Mát, Đà Lạt
    • Valley of Love 250k 07:00–17:00 7 Mai Anh Đào, Đà Lạt
    • Langbiang peak – jeep 200k–250k 07:00–17:00 Lạc Dương, Lâm Đồng
    • Cu Lan village 50k 07:00–17:00 Lạc Dương, Lâm Đồng
    • Central Highlands gong show 150k–250k 19:30–21:30 Khu du lịch Langbiang, Lạc Dương
    • Bao Dai Palace III 40k 07:00–17:00 Triệu Việt Vương, Đà Lạt
    • Chicken Church 無料 終日営業 Trần Phú, Đà Lạt
    • Crazy House 120k 08:30–19:00 3 Huỳnh Thúc Kháng, Đà Lạt
    • Van Thanh flower village 無料 終日営業 Vạn Thành, Đà Lạt
    • Da Lat persimmon garden 30k–50k 07:00–17:00 Trại Mát / Xuân Thọ, Đà Lạt

  4. 4 日目: Tuyen Lam lake – Datanla falls

    Robin cable car to Truc Lam, Tuyen Lam lake and the Datanla coaster, then fly home from Lien Khuong.

    • Robin cable car – Truc Lam Zen Monastery 100k–130k 07:30–17:00 Đèo Prenn, Đà Lạt
    • Tuyen Lam lake 無料 終日営業 Tuyền Lâm, Đà Lạt
    • Datanla falls – alpine coaster 50k–250k 07:00–17:00 Đèo Prenn, Đà Lạt
