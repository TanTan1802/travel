Da Nang – Hoi An family trip, 4 days · ダナン · 3泊4日

  1. 1 日目: My Khe beach – Han River

    Check in, swim at My Khe, then a Han River cruise and the Dragon Bridge at night.

    • My Khe beach 無料 終日営業 Võ Nguyên Giáp, Sơn Trà
    • Han river cruise 150k–200k 18:00–22:00 Bến du thuyền Bạch Đằng, Hải Châu
    • Dragon Bridge 無料 Fire show 9pm Sat & Sun Nguyễn Văn Linh, Đà Nẵng
    + ダナンのスポットを追加（7）
    • Non Nuoc stone-carving village 無料 07:00–18:00 Huyền Trân Công Chúa, Ngũ Hành Sơn
    • Son Tra night market 無料 17:00–23:30 Mai Hắc Đế, Sơn Trà
    • Hai Van pass 無料 終日営業 QL1A, Liên Chiểu
    • Lang Co bay 無料 終日営業 Lăng Cô, Phú Lộc, Huế
    • Tra Que vegetable village 無料 07:00–17:00 Cẩm Hà, Hội An
    • Cu Lao Cham speedboat & snorkelling 650k–800k Boats leave ~8am Cảng Cửa Đại, Hội An オンラインでチケットを探す
    • Bai Chong beach – Bai Lang village 無料 終日営業 Cù Lao Chàm

  2. 2 日目: Ba Na Hills – Golden Bridge

    Go early to beat the crowds: cable car up to the Golden Bridge, the French Village and the indoor play zone.

    • Ba Na Hills – Golden Bridge 950k 07:00–22:00 Hòa Ninh, Hòa Vang オンラインでチケットを探す
    • French Village – Le Jardin D'Amour 無料 08:00–21:00 Đỉnh Bà Nà

  3. 3 日目: Hoi An

    Basket boats in the Bay Mau coconut forest in the morning, then the old town and lanterns on the Hoai River at dusk.

    • Bay Mau coconut forest – basket boat 100k–150k 07:00–17:30 Cẩm Thanh, Hội An
    • Hoi An Old Town ticket – Japanese Bridge 120k 07:00–21:30 Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai, Hội An
    • Lanterns on the Hoai river 20k–50k 17:30–22:00 Bến sông Hoài, Hội An

  4. 4 日目: Marble Mountains – Son Tra

    Climb the Marble Mountains, visit Linh Ung pagoda over the bay, check out and fly home.

    • Marble Mountains 40k 07:00–17:30 81 Huyền Trân Công Chúa, Ngũ Hành Sơn
    • Linh Ung Pagoda, Son Tra 無料 06:00–21:30 Bãi Bụt, Sơn Trà
