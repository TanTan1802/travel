Hanoi – Ninh Binh – Ha Long in 4 days

ハノイ → チャンアン – ニンビン → ハロン湾 · Multi-stop · 3泊4日 · 3.4M–6.4M / 人

更新：11/2026

概要

Hanoi old quarter, Trang An boats and Mua Cave, then a day on Ha Long Bay – the classic northern loop.

ルート
ハノイ (1 日) → チャンアン – ニンビン (1 日) → ハロン湾 (2 日)
スタイル
Multi-stop
日数
3泊4日
1 人あたりの費用目安
3.4M–6.4M
宿泊
Hotel

日ごとの行程

  1. 1 日目: Hanoi

    Temple of Literature, Hoan Kiem lake and a water puppet show at night.

    • Temple of Literature 70k 08:00–17:00 58 Quốc Tử Giám, Đống Đa
    • Hoan Kiem Lake – The Huc bridge 無料 終日営業 Phố Đinh Tiên Hoàng, Hoàn Kiếm
    • Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre 150k–200k Shows 3pm – 8pm daily 57B Đinh Tiên Hoàng, Hoàn Kiếm

  2. ハノイ → チャンアン – ニンビン · バス / 車 ~1 時間 30 分 · 120k–210k

    2 日目: Ninh Binh

    About 2 hours by road to Ninh Binh, the Trang An boat ride, then climb Mua Cave for sunset.

    • Trang An boat trip 250k 07:00–16:00 Trường Yên, Hoa Lư
    • Mua Cave viewpoint 100k 06:00–19:00 Khê Hạ, Ninh Xuân, Hoa Lư

  3. チャンアン – ニンビン → ハロン湾 · バス / 車 ~3 時間 · 170k–420k

    3 日目: Ha Long Bay

    Drive to Ha Long and take a bay boat to Sung Sot cave and Ti Top island.

    • Ha Long Bay entrance ticket (routes 1–2) 290k 07:00–17:00 Cảng tàu khách quốc tế Tuần Châu, Hạ Long
    • Sung Sot Cave 無料 07:30–17:00 Đảo Bồ Hòn, vịnh Hạ Long
    • Ti Top Island 無料 07:30–17:00 Đảo Ti Tốp, vịnh Hạ Long

  4. 4 日目: Cable car – back to Hanoi

    Queen cable car up Ba Deo hill, then back to Hanoi at midday (about 2.5 hours on the expressway).

    • Queen Cable Car – Ba Deo hill 350k 09:00–22:00 Ga Hạ Long Park, Bãi Cháy
