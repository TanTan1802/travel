    • Con Son islet 50k–150k 07:00–17:00 Cồn Sơn, Bình Thủy

    Boat to the floating market at 5 – 6 am, a noodle workshop, lunch in the Phong Dien orchards.

    • Cai Rang floating market 150k–250k 5–9am Bến Ninh Kiều / cầu Cái Răng

    • Phong Dien fruit orchards 30k–60k 07:00–18:00 Phong Điền, Cần Thơ

    • Chau Doc market 無料 05:00–20:00 Bạch Đằng, Châu Đốc
    • Cam mountain cable car – Maitreya Buddha 200k–250k 06:00–18:00 An Hảo, Tịnh Biên
    • Chau Giang Cham village – Mubarak mosque 無料 07:00–17:00 Châu Phong, Tân Châu
