概要

リーソン島を訪れる理由

鮮やかな緑のニンニク畑、切り立った玄武岩の崖、小島の周りの水晶のように澄んだ海がある火山島です。リーソンには、島々を守った兵士をしのぶ厳かな儀式「ホアンサ兵士慰霊祭」が受け継がれています。

祭りと季節の見どころ

  • Fishing villages' Cau Ngu (whale worship) festival 2月 – 6月 · 旧暦

    Central-coast fishing villages honour the whale god for a safe season at sea: boat processions, ba trao rowing songs and basket-boat races.

    Each village has its own date – ask your host or the commune office to get it right.

  • Ly Son Feast for the Hoang Sa soldiers 4月 – 5月 · 旧暦

    In mid-3rd lunar month: a ceremony honouring the historic Hoang Sa flotilla, with paper boats released to the sea – a national intangible heritage.

    Ferries fill up during the festival – book ahead and watch for cancellations in rough seas.

  • Summer peak season 6月 – 8月

    School holidays make beaches their busiest of the year.

    Travel midweek and book early for better prices.

  • Central Vietnam storm season 9月 – 11月

    Heavy rain, possible floods and typhoons; Hoi An and Hue may flood.

    Watch forecasts and book flexible tickets/rooms; island boats may be suspended.

旧暦の祭りは毎年日付が変わります。出発前に公式の日程をご確認ください。

リーソン島への行き方

Fly to Chu Lai (VCL) or take the train to Quang Ngai, drive ~40 min to Sa Ky port, then a ~30-min speedboat to the island.
