概要

チャウドック – サム山を訪れる理由

ハウ川沿いのチャウドックは、毎年何百万人もの参拝者が訪れる霊験あらたかなバーチュアスー廟があるサム山遺跡群で知られています。増水期にはチャースーのカユプテ林が緑の浮草に覆われ、養魚いかだの村やロンスエンの水上マーケットがにぎわいます。

祭りと季節の見どころ

  • Ba Chua Xu Festival at Sam Mountain 5月 – 6月 · 旧暦

    The 23rd–27th of the fourth lunar month – one of the biggest festivals in the south.

    Very crowded – stay in Chau Doc, go early and watch your belongings.

  • Mekong orchard fruit season 5月 – 8月

    Durian, rambutan, mangosteen and mango are all in season – many orchards in Phong Dien and Cai Rang let you eat straight from the trees.

    Ask the entry price (usually covers eating on site; take-away is extra) and bring mosquito repellent.

  • Mekong flood season 9月 – 11月

    Rising water brings linh fish, sesbania flowers and lush Tra Su forest.

    Try linh fish hotpot – only available this season.

  • Bay Nui bull racing 9月 – 10月 · 旧暦

    During the Khmer Sen Dolta festival: pairs of bulls dragging harrows race across muddy fields in Tri Ton and Tinh Bien – a wild, unique spectacle.

    Stay behind the safety barriers and expect mud – wear old clothes and protect your camera.

旧暦の祭りは毎年日付が変わります。出発前に公式の日程をご確認ください。
