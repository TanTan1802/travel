概要

ハザンを訪れる理由

ユネスコ世界ジオパークのドンヴァン・カルスト高原はロードトリップの楽園です。マーピーレン峠は深い峡谷の上を縫うように続き、ニョークエ川はエメラルドに輝き、ルンクー国旗塔は国の最北端を示し、秋にはそばの花が丘をピンクに染めます。

祭りと季節の見どころ

  • H'Mong Gau Tao festival 1月 – 2月 · 旧暦

    In early spring: a ceremonial pole, khen pipes, pao ball throwing, top spinning and horse racing – the biggest blessing festival of the H'Mong.

    Ask your homestay host which village holds it and when; ask before taking close-up photos of people.

  • Highland market days 毎月

    Rotating markets: Dong Van and Meo Vac (Ha Giang), Bac Ha and Can Cau (near Sa Pa) – colourful costumes, livestock trading, brocade and thang co stew.

    Most run on Sunday mornings (Can Cau: Saturday) and wind down by noon – plan to arrive by 7–8 am.

  • Peach and plum blossom on the stone plateau 2月 – 3月

    Pink peach and white plum blossom against stone walls and rammed-earth houses in Lung Cu, Sung La, Pho Cao and Lao Xa.

    It's still cold with drizzle – pack warm layers; ride a motorbike only if you're used to mountain passes.

  • Khau Vai Love Market 4月 – 5月 · 旧暦

    On the 26th–27th of the third lunar month in Meo Vac – a unique highland gathering.

    Book Meo Vac homestays early; mountain roads get busy.

  • Golden rice terraces 05/09 – 15/10

    Terraces turn gold – best from late September to early October.

    Weekends are crowded and homestays fill up fast.

  • Buckwheat flower season 10月 – 11月

    Pink-purple buckwheat flowers cover the hills around Dong Van and Sung La.

    Weekends peak – go midweek if you can.

旧暦の祭りは毎年日付が変わります。出発前に公式の日程をご確認ください。

ハザンへの行き方

Sleeper bus Hanoi – Ha Giang ~6–7 h (often overnight). Ride the Dong Van – Ma Pi Leng loop on a scooter or hire an "easy rider" driver.
