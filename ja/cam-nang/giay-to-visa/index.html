Visas and documents to bring

E-visas, visa exemptions, passports and the documents you need for domestic flights and hotels.

更新：2026/9

目次
  1. International visitors
  2. Domestic travellers
  3. Also worth preparing

International visitors

  • E-visas are available to citizens of all countries, valid for up to 90 days with single or multiple entry. Apply only on the official portal evisa.gov.vn to avoid look-alike sites charging extra fees.
  • Citizens of some countries (for example many European countries, Japan and South Korea) get 45 days visa-free; ASEAN countries have their own exemptions. The list changes over time – check before you travel.
  • Your passport should be valid for at least 6 more months. Hotels and homestays will record your passport for residence registration – this is normal.

Domestic travellers

  • Domestic flights: bring your chip-based citizen ID card or use a level-2 VNeID digital ID; children need a birth certificate.
  • Check-in: hotels need your ID card for guest registration.
  • Some border and island areas may check ID at checkpoints – always carry the original.

Also worth preparing

  • Travel insurance covering medical care and accidents.
  • Photos of your passport/ID saved on your phone and email.
  • Your hotel's phone number and address in Vietnamese to show drivers.

この情報は目安であり変更される場合があります。旅行前に公式情報をご確認ください。
