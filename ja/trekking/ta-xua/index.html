ガイド
必須
出発
Ta Xua commune (Bac Yen)
  • The dinosaur's back – a narrow rocky ridge with drops on both sides
  • Cloud-hunting season from October to April
  • Mossy forest and bamboo high up

日ごとの行程

  1. 1 日目: Ta Xua village – Two Wild Apple Trees – hut

    The first stage to the Two Wild Apple Trees is about 3.5 km (around 1.5 hours); climb on through forest to an overnight hut.

  2. 2 日目: 2,865 m summit – the dinosaur's back – descent

    Summit at dawn for the clouds, cross the dinosaur's back (about 3 km of narrow, steep rock) and descend to the village.

終点: Ta Xua commune

費用と手続き

  • Hmong porters and guides from the village – book ahead via a homestay or operator

安全

  • Do not cross the dinosaur's back in rain, strong wind or thick fog
  • Keep both hands free for the rock – use a two-strap pack and stow poles on narrow sections

トレッキング共通の安全ルール

持ち物

  • Grippy shoes (hiking boots or deep-tread trainers) and thick socks
  • A light raincoat, hat and sunscreen
  • 1.5 – 2 litres of water and snacks
  • Personal medicine, plasters and insect repellent
  • A 25 – 35 litre pack with a hip belt and a waterproof liner
  • A base layer and a warm jacket for the night
  • A head torch, spare batteries and a power bank
  • Trekking poles – they save your knees on descents
  • Dry socks and clothes in a plastic bag
  • A waterproof shell, gloves and a beanie – above 2,500 m it can drop below 5°C
  • Broken-in high-ankle boots (never new ones)
  • A sleeping bag or liner if sleeping in huts
  • Electrolyte tablets and energy food

Ta Xua – the dinosaur's backのトレッキングは難しいですか？
Hard: Summits of 2,800 – 3,100 m, long relentless climbs and narrow rocky sections – you need good fitness and an overnight trek behind you.

はい – 地元ガイドまたはツアー会社と一緒に行く必要があります。
