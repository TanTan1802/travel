Overview

Why visit Mui Ne?

Mui Ne is famous for red and white sand dunes that change shape with the wind, the Fairy Stream flowing between red clay cliffs and a lively fishing village full of basket boats. It is also Southeast Asia's kitesurfing capital.

Highlights not to miss

  • Flying sand dunes
  • White sand dunes
  • Fairy Stream
  • Mui Ne fishing village

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Jeep over the dunes

Watch sunrise over the White Dunes by jeep.

Kitesurfing

Strong, steady winds all year – ideal for kitesurfing.

Sand sledding

Slide down the Flying Dunes on a board.

Walk the Fairy Stream

Walk barefoot between striking red and white cliffs.

Travel tips

  • Visit the dunes at sunrise or sunset to avoid hot sand.
  • Bring sunglasses and a face scarf when it is windy.

Getting to Mui Ne

From Saigon: ~3.5 h on the expressway by bus or limousine, or the train to Phan Thiet Station (~4 h) then a 20-min taxi.
