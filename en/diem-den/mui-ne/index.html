Overview

Why visit Mui Ne?

Mui Ne is famous for red and white sand dunes that change shape with the wind, the Fairy Stream flowing between red clay cliffs and a lively fishing village full of basket boats. It is also Southeast Asia's kitesurfing capital.

Highlights not to miss

  • Flying sand dunes
  • White sand dunes
  • Fairy Stream
  • Mui Ne fishing village

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Jeep over the dunes

Watch sunrise over the White Dunes by jeep.

Kitesurfing

Strong, steady winds all year – ideal for kitesurfing.

Sand sledding

Slide down the Flying Dunes on a board.

Walk the Fairy Stream

Walk barefoot between striking red and white cliffs.

Travel tips

  • Visit the dunes at sunrise or sunset to avoid hot sand.
  • Bring sunglasses and a face scarf when it is windy.
Tour itineraries

Mui Ne tour itineraries

3 days 2 nights · Sand dunes – Fairy Stream → Beach and sports → Ke Ga cape

Day 1: Sand dunes – Fairy Stream

  1. Morning

    Jeep to watch sunrise over the White Dunes.

  2. Afternoon

    Walk the Fairy Stream and visit Mui Ne fishing village.

  3. Evening

    Sunset at the Flying Dunes and seaside seafood.

Day 2: Beach and sports

  1. Morning

    Learn kitesurfing or swim at your resort.

  2. Afternoon

    Poshanu Cham towers and the wine castle.

  3. Evening

    Bánh căn and mackerel salad.

Day 3: Ke Ga cape

  1. Morning

    Ke Ga lighthouse – one of Vietnam's oldest lighthouses.

  2. Afternoon

    Swim at Tien Thanh beach and explore Ong Dia rocks.

  3. Evening

    Back in Phan Thiet for lẩu thả hotpot.

End of tour: check out, shop for local specialties and head home.
