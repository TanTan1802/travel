Overview

Why visit Pu Luong?

Pu Luong Nature Reserve has primeval forest, sweeping rice terraces and Thai and Muong villages that keep their traditional stilt houses. Perfect for trekking, homestays among the paddies and falling asleep to creaking bamboo water wheels.

Highlights not to miss

  • Don village rice terraces
  • Pho Doan water wheels
  • Hieu waterfall
  • Kho Muong village

Festivals & seasonal highlights

  • Water-pouring season 10/05 – 15/06

    Flooded terraces mirror the sky as planting begins.

    Go early morning or late afternoon for the best light.

  • Golden rice terraces 05/09 – 15/10

    Terraces turn gold – best from late September to early October.

    Weekends are crowded and homestays fill up fast.

Lunar-calendar festivals move each year – check the official dates before you go.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Trek Don – Kho Muong

Walk through terraces, bamboo forest and Bang cave.

Swim at Hieu waterfall

Multi-tiered falls with clear pools in the forest.

Cycle past water wheels

Giant bamboo water wheels at Pho Doan and Hieu village.

Stilt-house homestay

Stream sounds and family dinners with a Thai host.

Travel tips

  • Village roads are narrow and steep – hire a driver or ask your homestay for a pick-up.
  • The rice is golden in late May – early June and in Sep – Oct.

Getting to Pu Luong

From Hanoi ~4 h (170 km) via Mai Chau: bus to Ba Thuoc and a homestay pick-up, or a private car/limousine.
