Overview

Why visit Hanoi?

The thousand-year-old capital blends the charm of the Old Quarter's 36 streets, French colonial architecture and a modern pace of life. Strolling around Hoan Kiem Lake, sipping egg coffee or enjoying a bowl of heritage phở is the best way to feel the soul of Hanoi.

Highlights not to miss

  • Hoan Kiem Lake
  • Temple of Literature
  • Old Quarter
  • Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum

Festivals & seasonal highlights

  • Perfume Pagoda Festival February – April · lunar calendar

    From the 6th of the first lunar month to the end of the third – huge crowds on Yen stream.

    Avoid weekends in the first lunar month; set off by 6am.

  • Mid-Autumn Festival September – October · lunar calendar

    15th of the 8th lunar month: lantern-filled Hang Ma street and Hoi An, lion dances.

    Evenings are packed – park further out and walk in.

Lunar-calendar festivals move each year – check the official dates before you go.

Getting to Hanoi

Noi Bai Airport (HAN) is ~30 km from the Old Quarter: bus 86 (~45 min) or taxi/Grab ~250–350k VND. Hanoi Station is central.
