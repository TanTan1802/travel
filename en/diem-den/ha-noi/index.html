Overview

Why visit Hanoi?

The thousand-year-old capital blends the charm of the Old Quarter's 36 streets, French colonial architecture and a modern pace of life. Strolling around Hoan Kiem Lake, sipping egg coffee or enjoying a bowl of heritage phở is the best way to feel the soul of Hanoi.

Highlights not to miss

  • Temple of Literature
  • Old Quarter
  • Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Walking street

The lively Hoan Kiem walking street on weekend evenings.

Cyclo ride

See the 36 streets of the Old Quarter from a slow cyclo.

Water puppetry

A unique folk art at Thang Long Theatre.

Night food tour

Street food around Ta Hien and Dong Xuan.

Travel tips

  • Cross the street slowly and steadily so motorbikes can avoid you.
  • Many historic sites require modest clothing.
Tour itineraries

Hanoi tour itineraries

3 days 2 nights · Thousand-year Hanoi → Old Quarter and West Lake → Craft villages

Day 1: Thousand-year Hanoi

  1. Morning

    Visit Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, the One Pillar Pagoda and the Temple of Literature.

  2. Afternoon

    Walk around Hoan Kiem Lake and Ngoc Son Temple, sip egg coffee with a lake view.

  3. Evening

    Watch water puppetry and join a food tour on Ta Hien street.

Day 2: Old Quarter and West Lake

  1. Morning

    Heritage phở, a cyclo ride through the 36 streets and Dong Xuan market.

  2. Afternoon

    Hoa Lo Prison, Train Street and the Opera House.

  3. Evening

    Sunset at West Lake, shrimp cakes and the weekend walking street.

Day 3: Craft villages

  1. Morning

    Bat Trang ceramic village – make your own pottery.

  2. Afternoon

    Vietnam Museum of Ethnology with stilt houses of many ethnic groups.

  3. Evening

    Chả cá Lã Vọng and live music at an Old Quarter café.

End of tour: check out, shop for local specialties and head home.
