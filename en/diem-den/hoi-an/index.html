Overview

Why visit Hoi An Ancient Town?

Once one of Southeast Asia's busiest ports in the 16th–17th centuries, Hoi An still keeps its rows of yellow-walled, moss-roofed houses intact. At night, thousands of colourful lanterns light up the old town and the Hoai river.

Highlights not to miss

  • Japanese Covered Bridge
  • Hoai River
  • Fujian Assembly Hall
  • Bay Mau coconut forest

Festivals & seasonal highlights

  • Full-moon night in the old town Monthly · lunar calendar

    Every 14th lunar evening the old town switches to lantern light only.

    Check the lunar calendar to catch the full-moon night.

  • Da Nang International Fireworks Festival June – July

    International teams put on shows over the Han River on weekend nights.

    Buy stand tickets or grab a riverside spot early; rooms get pricey.

  • Mid-Autumn Festival September – October · lunar calendar

    15th of the 8th lunar month: lantern-filled Hang Ma street and Hoi An, lion dances.

    Evenings are packed – park further out and walk in.

  • Central Vietnam storm season September – November

    Heavy rain, possible floods and typhoons; Hoi An and Hue may flood.

    Watch forecasts and book flexible tickets/rooms; island boats may be suspended.

Lunar-calendar festivals move each year – check the official dates before you go.

Travel tips

  • Motorbikes are banned in the old town in the evening – walk or cycle.
  • On each full-moon night the old town switches off electric lights and glows with lanterns only.
