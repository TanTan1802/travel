Overview

Why visit Phong Nha – Ke Bang?

Phong Nha – Ke Bang National Park has Asia's oldest karst cave system, including Son Doong – the largest cave on the planet. Paradise Cave with its magnificent stalactites and Phong Nha Cave with its underground river are not to be missed.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Boat into Phong Nha Cave

Sail the Son river into an underground river.

Dark Cave adventure

Zipline, mud bath and swimming inside the cave.

Trek to En Cave

Camp inside the world's third-largest cave.

Cycle Bong Lai valley

Explore the green valleys around Phong Nha.

Travel tips

  • Son Doong tours must be booked months ahead and require good fitness.
  • Many caves close in the flood season (September – November).
