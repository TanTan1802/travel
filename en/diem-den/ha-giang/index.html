Overview

Why visit Ha Giang?

The Dong Van Karst Plateau – a UNESCO Global Geopark – is a paradise for road trippers. Ma Pi Leng pass winds above deep gorges, the Nho Que river glows turquoise, Lung Cu flag tower marks the country's northern tip and buckwheat flowers paint the hills pink in autumn.

Highlights not to miss

  • Ma Pi Leng pass
  • Nho Que river
  • Lung Cu flag tower
  • Dong Van old quarter

Festivals & seasonal highlights

  • Khau Vai Love Market April – May · lunar calendar

    On the 26th–27th of the third lunar month in Meo Vac – a unique highland gathering.

    Book Meo Vac homestays early; mountain roads get busy.

  • Golden rice terraces 05/09 – 15/10

    Terraces turn gold – best from late September to early October.

    Weekends are crowded and homestays fill up fast.

  • Buckwheat flower season October – November

    Pink-purple buckwheat flowers cover the hills around Dong Van and Sung La.

    Weekends peak – go midweek if you can.

Lunar-calendar festivals move each year – check the official dates before you go.

Dong Van market

Travel tips

  • Only ride yourself if you have mountain-pass experience; otherwise hire an "easy rider".
  • Fill up whenever you see a petrol station – they are few and far between.

Getting to Ha Giang

Sleeper bus Hanoi – Ha Giang ~6–7 h (often overnight). Ride the Dong Van – Ma Pi Leng loop on a scooter or hire an "easy rider" driver.
