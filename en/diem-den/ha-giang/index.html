Overview

Why visit Ha Giang?

The Dong Van Karst Plateau – a UNESCO Global Geopark – is a paradise for road trippers. Ma Pi Leng pass winds above deep gorges, the Nho Que river glows turquoise, Lung Cu flag tower marks the country's northern tip and buckwheat flowers paint the hills pink in autumn.

Highlights not to miss

  • Ma Pi Leng pass
  • Nho Que river
  • Lung Cu flag tower
  • Dong Van old quarter

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Motorbike 350 km of the country's most beautiful passes.

Nho Que river boat

Sail through Tu San, Southeast Asia's deepest canyon.

Lung Cu flag tower

Stand at the sacred northernmost point of Vietnam.

Dong Van market

A Sunday morning market full of highland life.

Travel tips

  • Only ride yourself if you have mountain-pass experience; otherwise hire an "easy rider".
  • Fill up whenever you see a petrol station – they are few and far between.

Getting to Ha Giang

Sleeper bus Hanoi – Ha Giang ~6–7 h (often overnight). Ride the Dong Van – Ma Pi Leng loop on a scooter or hire an "easy rider" driver.

Ha Giang tour itineraries

Ha Giang tour itineraries

Việt Travel · Ha Giang · Best time: October – November (buckwheat flowers)

3 days 2 nights · Ha Giang – Quan Ba – Yen Minh → Dong Van – Lung Cu → Ma Pi Leng – Nho Que

Day 1: Ha Giang – Quan Ba – Yen Minh

  1. Morning

    Rent a motorbike or hire an easy rider, check in at the Km0 marker.

  2. Afternoon

    Quan Ba Heaven's Gate, the Fairy Twin Mountains and Yen Minh pine forest.

  3. Evening

    Stay in Yen Minh, try thắng cố or black chicken hotpot.

Day 2: Dong Van – Lung Cu

  1. Morning

    The Vuong family mansion and Tham Ma slope.

  2. Afternoon

    Climb Lung Cu flag tower – Vietnam's northernmost point.

  3. Evening

    Wander Dong Van old quarter and its cafés.

Day 3: Ma Pi Leng – Nho Que

  1. Morning

    Cross Ma Pi Leng pass and admire Tu San canyon.

  2. Afternoon

    Take a boat on the Nho Que river, then head to Meo Vac.

  3. Evening

    Join the love market (if in season) and stay in Meo Vac.

End of tour: check out, shop for local specialties and head home.
