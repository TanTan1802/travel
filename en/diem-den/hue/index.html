Overview

Why visit Hue Imperial City?

Capital of 13 Nguyen emperors, Hue preserves a vast complex of royal monuments, solemn tombs and a slow, contemplative way of life. Listening to Hue folk songs on the Perfume River and tasting royal cuisine are experiences you can only have here.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Visit the Imperial City

Explore over 100 royal buildings.

Hue folk music

Listen to traditional songs on a dragon boat.

Cycle to the tombs

Tu Duc, Minh Mang and Khai Dinh tombs among pine forests.

Floating lanterns

Release a wish lantern on the Perfume River at night.

Travel tips

  • Hue's rainy season lasts from September to December.
  • Buy a combo ticket to save when visiting several monuments.
Tour itineraries

Hue Imperial City tour itineraries

3 days 2 nights · Hue Imperial City → Royal tombs and food → Tam Giang lagoon – Thuan An beach

Day 1: Hue Imperial City

  1. Morning

    Bún bò Huế for breakfast, then the Imperial City – Ngo Mon Gate – Forbidden Purple City.

  2. Afternoon

    Thien Mu Pagoda and Minh Mang Tomb.

  3. Evening

    Listen to Hue folk songs on a dragon boat and release lanterns on the Perfume River.

Day 2: Royal tombs and food

  1. Morning

    Khai Dinh Tomb and Tu Duc Tomb.

  2. Afternoon

    Thuy Xuan incense village and Dong Ba market.

  3. Evening

    Food tour of bánh bèo, bánh nậm and Hue sweet soups on Han Thuyen street.

Day 3: Tam Giang lagoon – Thuan An beach

  1. Morning

    Chuon lagoon on Tam Giang, with lagoon seafood.

  2. Afternoon

    Phuoc Tich ancient village and Thuan An beach.

  3. Evening

    Sunset over Tam Giang lagoon.

End of tour: check out, shop for local specialties and head home.
