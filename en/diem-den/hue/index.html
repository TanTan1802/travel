Overview

Why visit Hue Imperial City?

Capital of 13 Nguyen emperors, Hue preserves a vast complex of royal monuments, solemn tombs and a slow, contemplative way of life. Listening to Hue folk songs on the Perfume River and tasting royal cuisine are experiences you can only have here.

Festivals & seasonal highlights

  • Hue Festival April – June

    Royal arts and culture events – the main edition is usually held in even years.

    Check the official schedule; central hotels sell out early.

  • Central Vietnam storm season September – November

    Heavy rain, possible floods and typhoons; Hoi An and Hue may flood.

    Watch forecasts and book flexible tickets/rooms; island boats may be suspended.

Lunar-calendar festivals move each year – check the official dates before you go.

Getting to Hue Imperial City

Fly to Phu Bai (HUI, ~15 km from the centre) or take the train to Hue Station. From Da Nang/Hoi An it is ~2–2.5 h by road via the Hai Van tunnel.
