Overview

Why visit Ha Tien?

Home of the "ten views of Ha Tien": limestone hills, caves, Dong Ho lagoon and Mui Nai beach. Ha Tien is also the gateway to the Hai Tac (Pirate) islands, Phu Quoc and Cambodia – a meeting point of Vietnamese, Chinese and Khmer cultures.

Highlights not to miss

  • Mui Nai beach
  • Thach Dong cave
  • Dong Ho lagoon
  • Hai Tac islands

Festivals & seasonal highlights

  • Rough-sea season June – September

    Monsoon winds bring rough seas; island tours and ferries may be cancelled.

    Keep a spare day in case boats are cancelled.

  • Dry season – peak time on Phu Quoc December – February

    Calm seas and clear skies – the most beautiful and busiest time.

    Book resorts, island tours and the cable car ahead.

Lunar-calendar festivals move each year – check the official dates before you go.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Sunset at Mui Nai

Swim, eat seafood and watch the sun set over the Gulf of Thailand.

Thach Dong & Mac Cuu tombs

A cave on a limestone hill and the tombs of the Mac family who founded Ha Tien.

Hai Tac islands

A ~1.5 h speedboat to unspoilt islands with coral reefs.

Ha Tien night market

Street food by Dong Ho lagoon.

Travel tips

  • Combine Ha Tien with Phu Quoc (fast ferry ~1.5 h) in one trip.
  • In the rainy season (May – Oct) seas can be rough and island boats may be cancelled.

Getting to Ha Tien

Bus Saigon – Ha Tien ~7–8 h; ~2 h from Rach Gia. Fast ferries link Phu Quoc in ~1.5 h; fly to Phu Quoc (PQC) or Rach Gia (VKG).
