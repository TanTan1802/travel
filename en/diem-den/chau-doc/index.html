Overview

Why visit Chau Doc – Sam Mountain?

Chau Doc sits on the Hau river and is known for the Sam Mountain complex, home to the sacred Ba Chua Xu temple visited by millions of pilgrims every year. In the flood season, Tra Su cajuput forest is carpeted with green duckweed, and fish-raft villages and Long Xuyen floating market come alive.

Highlights not to miss

  • Ba Chua Xu temple
  • Sam Mountain
  • Tra Su cajuput forest
  • Chau Giang Cham village

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Boat through Tra Su

Glide over green duckweed among flocks of birds.

Ba Chua Xu temple

The festival in the 4th lunar month is especially lively.

Cam Mountain cable car

Up to the "roof of the Mekong Delta" and its giant Maitreya statue.

Fish-raft village

Visit raft houses and the Cham weaving village.

Travel tips

  • The festival season (1st – 4th lunar months) is very crowded – book early.
  • Buy fermented fish from reputable stalls at the market.
Itinerary

2-day itinerary for Chau Doc – Sam Mountain

  1. Morning

    Arrive in Chau Doc, visit Ba Chua Xu temple and Thoai Ngoc Hau tomb.

  2. Afternoon

    Climb Sam Mountain for border views and visit Tay An Pagoda.

  3. Evening

    Fermented fish hotpot and Chau Doc market for fish sauce gifts.
