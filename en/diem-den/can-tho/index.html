Overview

Why visit Can Tho – Mekong Delta?

The capital of the Mekong Delta offers unique river experiences: Cai Rang floating market opening at dawn, orchards heavy with fruit, canals shaded by coconut palms and the famously gentle, welcoming people of the Southwest.

Highlights not to miss

  • Cai Rang floating market
  • Ninh Kieu wharf
  • Binh Thuy ancient house
  • Phong Dien orchards

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Floating market at dawn

Breakfast on a boat amid the lively trading.

Fruit orchards

Pick rambutan, durian and mangosteen in the gardens.

Đờn ca tài tử music

Traditional Southern music by the river.

Cycle the islets

Explore village life on Tan Loc and Son islets.

Travel tips

  • Leave for the floating market at 5 am to see it at its busiest.
  • Fruit season peaks from May to August.
Tour itineraries

Can Tho – Mekong Delta tour itineraries

3 days 2 nights · Ninh Kieu – ancient house → Cai Rang floating market → Son islet – Bang Lang stork garden

Day 1: Ninh Kieu – ancient house

  1. Morning

    Arrive in Can Tho, visit Binh Thuy ancient house and Ong Pagoda.

  2. Afternoon

    Phong Dien fruit orchards and Mekong bánh xèo.

  3. Evening

    Stroll Ninh Kieu wharf and take a cruise with đờn ca tài tử music.

Day 2: Cai Rang floating market

  1. Morning

    Boat to Cai Rang floating market at 5 am and eat hủ tiếu on a boat.

  2. Afternoon

    A traditional noodle workshop and My Khanh tourist village.

  3. Evening

    Tay Do night market and fermented fish hotpot.

Day 3: Son islet – Bang Lang stork garden

  1. Morning

    Son islet – "flying" snakehead fish and folk cake making.

  2. Afternoon

    Bang Lang stork garden with thousands of storks returning home.

  3. Evening

    Orchard homestay.

End of tour: check out, shop for local specialties and head home.
