Overview

Why visit Da Lat?

With a cool climate all year, whispering pine forests, poetic lakes and French villas, Da Lat is Vietnam's most romantic destination. Chase clouds in the morning, stroll around Xuan Huong Lake in the afternoon and wander the night market with hot soy milk in the evening.

Highlights not to miss

  • Xuan Huong Lake
  • Da Lat railway station
  • Valley of Love
  • Cau Dat tea hills

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Clouds at Cau Dat

Watch a sea of clouds over the tea hills at 5 am.

Trai Mat train

A vintage train from Da Lat station past gardens and flower fields.

Flowers & strawberries

Pick strawberries and stroll hydrangea gardens.

Da Lat night market

Snacks, knitwear and candied fruit.

Travel tips

  • Bring a jacket – nights in Da Lat can drop to 12–15°C.
  • Weekends and holidays are busy, book accommodation early.
Itinerary

3-day itinerary for Da Lat

  1. Morning

    Walk around Xuan Huong Lake, eat bánh căn and visit Lam Vien square.

  2. Afternoon

    Da Lat station, the Trai Mat train and Linh Phuoc Pagoda.

  3. Evening

    Da Lat night market with grilled rice paper and soy milk.
