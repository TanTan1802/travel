Overview

Why visit Da Lat?

With a cool climate all year, whispering pine forests, poetic lakes and French villas, Da Lat is Vietnam's most romantic destination. Chase clouds in the morning, stroll around Xuan Huong Lake in the afternoon and wander the night market with hot soy milk in the evening.

Highlights not to miss

Festivals & seasonal highlights

  • Cherry blossom season January – February

    Pink cherry blossoms around Xuan Huong Lake and Cau Dat tea hills.

    Often overlaps with Tết – book early.

  • Purple jacaranda in Da Lat March – May

    Jacarandas bloom along Yersin, Tran Phu and Hung Vuong streets and around Xuan Huong Lake – sunny dry-season weather.

    Best on clear mornings; bring a jacket, as mornings and evenings are still cold.

  • Wild sunflower season November

    Wild sunflowers line the Highlands roads, Prenn pass and Langbiang.

    Sunny and cool – ideal for a motorbike ride.

  • Pink grass season in Da Lat November – December

    Grassy hills at Trai Mat, Langbiang and Tuyen Lam Lake turn soft pink as the dry season begins.

    Shoot at sunrise or sunset; many hills are private land – ask first and don't trample the grass.

  • Da Lat Flower Festival December

    Usually every other December: flower parades and displays around Xuan Huong Lake.

    Coincides with year-end crowds – book early.

  • Christmas season 20/12 – 25/12

    Notre-Dame and Tan Dinh churches in Saigon, St Joseph's Cathedral in Hanoi and Da Lat's Rooster Church put up nativity scenes and star lights; the streets are buzzing on the night of the 24th.

    Traffic jams around the big churches on the 24th – walk or go early, and keep quiet during mass.

Lunar-calendar festivals move each year – check the official dates before you go.

Getting to Da Lat

Lien Khuong Airport (DLI) is ~30 km from town. Sleeper bus or limousine from Saigon ~7 h, from Nha Trang ~3.5 h.
