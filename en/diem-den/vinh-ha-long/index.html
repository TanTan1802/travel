Overview

Why visit Ha Long Bay?

Nearly 2,000 limestone islands rise from emerald water, creating a living ink-wash painting. Twice recognised by UNESCO as a World Natural Heritage Site, Ha Long is where you can spend the night on a cruise, kayak through mysterious caves and watch the sunset settle over the bay.

Highlights not to miss

  • Sung Sot Cave
  • Ti Top Island
  • Cua Van fishing village
  • Lan Ha Bay

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Overnight cruise

A 2-day, 1-night trip with sunrise in the middle of the bay.

Kayaking

Paddle through Luon Cave into hidden lagoons.

Climb Ti Top peak

Panoramic bay views after more than 400 stone steps.

Seaplane

See the bay from the sky on a 25-minute flight.

Travel tips

  • Book your cruise at least a week ahead in peak season.
  • Bring sunscreen and shoes with good grip for climbing.

Getting to Ha Long Bay

From Hanoi take a limousine van or express bus, ~2.5 h (180 km); or fly to Van Don (VDO) then drive ~1 h.
