Overview

Why visit Ho Chi Minh City?

Saigon is Vietnam's largest city, buzzing day and night. From the French-era Notre-Dame Cathedral and Central Post Office to soaring skyscrapers, from bustling Ben Thanh Market to endless food alleys.

Highlights not to miss

  • Notre-Dame Cathedral
  • Ben Thanh Market
  • Independence Palace
  • Nguyen Hue walking street

Notre-Dame, the Post Office, Independence Palace, War Remnants Museum.

Saigon River cruise

Travel tips

  • Afternoon showers are common in the rainy season (May – November).
  • Use a ride-hailing app to get around easily.

Getting to Ho Chi Minh City

Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN) is ~7 km from District 1: bus 109 or taxi/Grab ~100–150k VND. Saigon Station is in District 3.

Flights to SGN Train (Sài Gòn station) Bus / limousine tickets
