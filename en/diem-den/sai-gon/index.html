Overview

Why visit Ho Chi Minh City?

Saigon is Vietnam's largest city, buzzing day and night. From the French-era Notre-Dame Cathedral and Central Post Office to soaring skyscrapers, from bustling Ben Thanh Market to endless food alleys.

Highlights not to miss

  • Notre-Dame Cathedral
  • Ben Thanh Market
  • Independence Palace
  • Nguyen Hue walking street

Saigon bánh mì

Hủ tiếu

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Heritage city tour

Notre-Dame, the Post Office, Independence Palace, War Remnants Museum.

Motorbike food tour

Ride through the night tasting food in Districts 4 and 5.

Saigon River cruise

Dinner while the city lights up.

Cu Chi Tunnels

A historic 250 km tunnel network.

Travel tips

  • Afternoon showers are common in the rainy season (May – November).
  • Use a ride-hailing app to get around easily.
Itinerary

2-day itinerary for Ho Chi Minh City

  1. Morning

    Independence Palace, Notre-Dame Cathedral and the Central Post Office.

  2. Afternoon

    War Remnants Museum and Ben Thanh Market.

  3. Evening

    Nguyen Hue walking street and city views from Landmark 81 or Bitexco.

  1. Morning

    Cu Chi Tunnels (half day).

  2. Afternoon

    Cho Lon and Thien Hau Temple in District 5.

  3. Evening

    Motorbike food tour in District 4: snails, broken rice and bánh mì.
