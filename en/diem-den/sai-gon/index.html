Overview

Why visit Ho Chi Minh City?

Saigon is Vietnam's largest city, buzzing day and night. From the French-era Notre-Dame Cathedral and Central Post Office to soaring skyscrapers, from bustling Ben Thanh Market to endless food alleys.

Festivals & seasonal highlights

  • Nguyen Hue Tet flower street January – February · lunar calendar

    For about a week around Tết, Nguyen Hue walking street becomes a flower avenue with the zodiac mascot of the year and dazzling lights at night.

    Go early in the morning (6–8 am) for photos without crowds; evenings are packed.

  • Southern rainy season May – October

    Short afternoon downpours that pass quickly; some Saigon streets flood in heavy rain and high tides; seas around the southern islands get rough.

    Plan outdoor activities in the morning and always carry a raincoat; boats to Nam Du and Phu Quoc may be cancelled in rough seas.

  • Nghinh Ong whale festival at Can Gio and Thang Tam September – October · lunar calendar

    Mid-8th lunar month: hundreds of decorated boats escort the whale god at sea, with hat boi opera and lion dances – the biggest fishermen's festival in the south.

    The road to Can Gio jams on the main day – leave early; ask the organisers beforehand if you want to join a boat.

  • New Year's Eve countdown 31/12

    On 31 December: open-air concerts and midnight fireworks at Hoan Kiem Lake, Nguyen Hue walking street and along the Han River.

    Arrive before 9 pm for a good spot; park well outside the centre and walk in, as many streets close to traffic.

  • Christmas season 20/12 – 25/12

    Notre-Dame and Tan Dinh churches in Saigon, St Joseph's Cathedral in Hanoi and Da Lat's Rooster Church put up nativity scenes and star lights; the streets are buzzing on the night of the 24th.

    Traffic jams around the big churches on the 24th – walk or go early, and keep quiet during mass.

Lunar-calendar festivals move each year – check the official dates before you go.

Getting to Ho Chi Minh City

Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN) is ~7 km from District 1: bus 109 or taxi/Grab ~100–150k VND. Saigon Station is in District 3.
