Overview

Why visit Vung Tau?

Only about 2 hours from Ho Chi Minh City, Vung Tau is a favourite weekend escape with Back Beach, Front Beach, the Christ the King statue on Small Mountain and an old lighthouse. The city is famous for crispy bánh khọt and fresh seafood.

Highlights not to miss

  • Christ the King statue
  • Vung Tau lighthouse
  • Hon Ba island
  • White Palace

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Climb to Christ the King

Over 800 steps to the top of Small Mountain for city views.

Swim at Back Beach

A long beach with gentle waves and many services.

Walk to Hon Ba

Walk out to Hon Ba shrine at low tide.

Ho May cable car

A hilltop park on Big Mountain with many rides.

Travel tips

  • Avoid public holidays – the road from Saigon gets very congested.
  • Check the tide table before walking out to Hon Ba.

Day 1: Small Mountain – Back Beach

  2. Afternoon

    Swim at Back Beach and walk to Hon Ba at low tide.

  3. Evening

    Bánh khọt, stingray hotpot and a stroll along Front Beach.

Day 2: Lighthouse – White Palace

  1. Morning

    Sunrise at Vung Tau lighthouse.

  2. Afternoon

    Visit the White Palace and the Ho May cable car.

  3. Evening

    Seafood at Xom Luoi market.

Day 3: Long Hai – Ho Tram

  1. Morning

    Dinh Co temple and Long Hai beach.

  2. Afternoon

    Swim at Ho Tram and soak in Binh Chau hot springs.

    Stay at a Ho Tram resort.
