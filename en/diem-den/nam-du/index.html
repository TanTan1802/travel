Overview

Why visit Nam Du Islands?

About 2.5 hours by fast boat from Rach Gia, the Nam Du archipelago has more than 20 islands with clear-water beaches such as Cay Men and Ngu, a fishing village on Cu Tron island and a coastal road along the hills. Nam Du is still quiet and unspoilt – ideal for boat trips, fishing, snorkelling and cheap fresh seafood.

Highlights not to miss

  • Cay Men beach
  • Cu Tron island
  • Nam Du lighthouse
  • Hon Mau

Festivals & seasonal highlights

  • Southern rainy season May – October

    Short afternoon downpours that pass quickly; some Saigon streets flood in heavy rain and high tides; seas around the southern islands get rough.

    Plan outdoor activities in the morning and always carry a raincoat; boats to Nam Du and Phu Quoc may be cancelled in rough seas.

Lunar-calendar festivals move each year – check the official dates before you go.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Swim at Cay Men beach

A small turquoise cove among coconut palms on Hon Lon.

Island-hopping by boat

Hon Mau, Hon Dau, Hon Ngang – fishing and snorkelling.

Nam Du lighthouse

A lighthouse on the highest peak with views over the archipelago.

Ride around Hon Lon

The coastal road past fishing villages, Ngu beach and rocky coves.

Travel tips

  • Rach Gia – Nam Du boats stop in rough seas (June – September) – check timetables and the forecast first.
  • There are few ATMs and the power may be cut at times – bring cash and a power bank.

Getting to Nam Du Islands

Fast boat from Rach Gia port, about 2.5 h (1 – 2 sailings a day). To Rach Gia: fly (VKG), or a bus from Saigon in about 6 h or from Can Tho in about 3 h.
