Overview

Why visit Ba Be Lake?

Ba Be Lake lies inside Ba Be National Park and is the largest natural freshwater lake in Vietnam. Its three arms – Pe Lam, Pe Lu and Pe Leng – form a stretch of water about 8 km long between limestone peaks and primary forest. Take a boat up the Nang River through Puong cave to Dau Dang waterfall, paddle a kayak in the morning mist, then stay in Tay stilt houses at Pac Ngoi or Bo Lu – the area is a Ramsar site and an ASEAN Heritage Park.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Boat trip on Ba Be Lake

Pass Ba Goa islet, Ao Tien pond and An Ma temple between the three arms of the lake.

Puong cave

Boats glide through the mountain along the Nang River under roosting bats.

Dau Dang waterfall

A multi-step forest waterfall that can only be reached by boat.

Tay village homestay

Sleep in a stilt house at Pac Ngoi or Bo Lu and listen to Then singing.

Travel tips

  • Share one boat (up to about 12 people) to split the cost, and carry cash – there are very few ATMs around the lake.
  • Dau Dang waterfall is reachable only by boat; in the rainy season (July – August) the Nang River runs high, so check with the boatman first.

Getting to Ba Be Lake

From Hanoi a bus or limousine van takes about 5 h (about 230 km) via Thai Nguyen, around 200,000 – 300,000 VND; many operators stop at the lake boat pier or Cho Ra town. The nearest airport is Noi Bai (HAN).
