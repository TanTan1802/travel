Overview

Why visit Da Nang?

Da Nang combines sea, mountains and a modern city. My Khe beach has been named one of the most attractive beaches on the planet, the Golden Bridge at Ba Na Hills is world-famous and the Dragon Bridge breathes fire every weekend.

Highlights not to miss

  • Golden Bridge – Ba Na Hills
  • My Khe beach
  • Marble Mountains
  • Dragon Bridge

Festivals & seasonal highlights

  • Summer peak season June – August

    School holidays make beaches their busiest of the year.

    Travel midweek and book early for better prices.

  • Da Nang International Fireworks Festival June – July

    International teams put on shows over the Han River on weekend nights.

    Buy stand tickets or grab a riverside spot early; rooms get pricey.

  • Central Vietnam storm season September – November

    Heavy rain, possible floods and typhoons; Hoi An and Hue may flood.

    Watch forecasts and book flexible tickets/rooms; island boats may be suspended.

Lunar-calendar festivals move each year – check the official dates before you go.

Getting to Da Nang

Da Nang Airport (DAD) is just ~3 km from the centre, taxi/Grab ~80–120k VND. Da Nang Station is on the North–South line.

Flights to DAD Train (Đà Nẵng station) Bus / limousine tickets
