Overview

Why visit Phu Quoc?

Vietnam's largest island has fine white sand, jade-clear water and brilliant sunsets on its west coast. Phu Quoc is also famous for fish sauce, pepper, pearls and the world's longest sea-crossing cable car.

Highlights not to miss

  • Sao Beach
  • Hon Thom
  • Grand World

Festivals & seasonal highlights

  • Summer peak season June – August

    School holidays make beaches their busiest of the year.

    Travel midweek and book early for better prices.

  • Rough-sea season June – September

    Monsoon winds bring rough seas; island tours and ferries may be cancelled.

    Keep a spare day in case boats are cancelled.

  • Dry season – peak time on Phu Quoc December – February

    Calm seas and clear skies – the most beautiful and busiest time.

    Book resorts, island tours and the cable car ahead.

Lunar-calendar festivals move each year – check the official dates before you go.

Travel tips

  • In the rainy season (May – October) the west coast is rough – choose Sao Beach on the east.
  • Fish sauce is generally not allowed as liquid in regular airline luggage.

Getting to Phu Quoc

Fly to Phu Quoc (PQC) – ~1 h from Saigon, ~2 h from Hanoi. Or take a fast ferry from Rach Gia / Ha Tien (~1.5–2.5 h).
