Overview

Why visit Ly Son Island?

A volcanic island of bright-green garlic fields, sheer basalt cliffs and crystal-clear water around Little Island. Ly Son also keeps the Khao le the linh Hoang Sa ceremony – a solemn memory of the soldiers who guarded the islands.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Sunset at To Vo arch

A natural stone arch framing the sun sinking into the sea.

Climb Thoi Loi peak

An old volcanic crater with views over the island and garlic fields.

Visit Little Island

Swim and snorkel at Hang beach in glassy water.

Hang pagoda & Am Linh Tu

A cave pagoda and the shrine to the Hoang Sa flotilla.

Travel tips

  • Speedboats from Sa Ky port can be suspended in rough seas – check the weather and keep a spare day.
  • Rent a scooter on the island to get around.

Getting to Ly Son Island

Fly to Chu Lai (VCL) or take the train to Quang Ngai, drive ~40 min to Sa Ky port, then a ~30-min speedboat to the island.

Ly Son Island tour itineraries

Ly Son Island tour itineraries

Việt Travel · Quang Ngai · Best time: March – August

3 days 2 nights · To the big island → Thoi Loi peak → Little Island

Day 1: To the big island

  1. Morning

    Speedboat from Sa Ky port (~30 min), check in and rent a scooter.

  2. Afternoon

    Hang pagoda and To Vo arch – wait for sunset.

  3. Evening

    Garlic salad and rock snails at the Ly Son night market.

Day 2: Thoi Loi peak

  1. Morning

    Climb Thoi Loi for sunrise over the old crater.

  2. Afternoon

    Am Linh Tu and the Hoang Sa – Bac Hai flotilla exhibition house.

  3. Evening

    Fresh seafood and sea urchin porridge.

Day 3: Little Island

  1. Morning

    Boat to Little Island and swim at Hang beach.

  2. Afternoon

    Snorkel the corals and walk around the islet.

  3. Evening

    Back on the big island, stroll the garlic fields at dusk.

End of tour: check out, shop for local specialties and head home.
