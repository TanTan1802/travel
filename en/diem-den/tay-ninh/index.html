Overview

Why visit Ba Den Mountain – Tay Ninh?

At 986 m, Ba Den (Black Virgin) Mountain is the "roof of southern Vietnam", with sacred Ba pagodas, modern cable cars and a bronze statue of the Tay Bo Da Son Buddha on its summit. About 100 km from Saigon, Tay Ninh also has the striking Cao Dai Holy See, the huge Dau Tieng lake and dew-dried rice paper.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Cable car to the summit

Views over the plains and Dau Tieng lake from nearly 1,000 m.

Climb the stone steps to Ba pagoda

About 1.5 h on foot through forest to the pagoda and Tiger cave.

Noon ceremony at the Holy See

Watch the white-robed Cao Dai worship at 12 pm.

Sunset at Dau Tieng lake

Vietnam's largest man-made lake, loveliest at dusk.

Travel tips

  • The first lunar month is the busy Ba Mountain spring festival – weekdays are calmer.
  • The Holy See holds ceremonies four times a day (6 am, noon, 6 pm, midnight) – dress modestly and keep quiet.

    Long Hoa market for shrimp salt and rice paper.

Day 3: Dau Tieng lake

  1. Morning

    Drive to the vast Dau Tieng lake.

  2. Afternoon

    A boat on the lake and straw-grilled snakehead fish.

    Sunset from the Dau Tieng dam.
