Overview

Why visit Tay Ninh – Ba Den Mountain?

About 100 km from Ho Chi Minh City, Tay Ninh is home to 986-metre Ba Den Mountain – the highest peak in southern Vietnam – with cable cars to the summit, a sacred pagoda complex and the Tay Bo Da Son Buddha statue above a sea of clouds. The Cao Dai Holy See, with its striking architecture and solemn services, is a one-of-a-kind experience, along with local dew-dried rice paper and shrimp salt.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Cable car to Ba Den summit

Ride to the roof of the south for clouds and the Tay Bo Da Son statue.

Attend a Cao Dai service

Watch the solemn ceremony from the visitors' balcony.

Hike Ba Den Mountain

A 2–3 hour trail past the main pagoda and cave pagoda.

Sunset at Dau Tieng Lake

A vast lake at its best in the late afternoon.

Travel tips

  • At the Holy See dress modestly, keep quiet and don't photograph worshippers up close during services.
  • Ba Den is packed at Tet and the first full moon of the lunar year – buy cable car tickets ahead and go very early.

Getting to Tay Ninh – Ba Den Mountain

From Saigon: bus or limousine van about 2–2.5 h (~100 km) on Highway 22, many departures daily; or hire a car for a day trip.
