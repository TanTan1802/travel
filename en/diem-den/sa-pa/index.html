Overview

Why visit Sa Pa?

At 1,600 metres above sea level, Sa Pa charms visitors with its cool climate all year round, rice terraces curving like stairways to the sky and the rich culture of the H'Mong and Red Dao people. It is also the gateway to Fansipan – "the Roof of Indochina".

Highlights not to miss

  • Fansipan peak
  • Cat Cat village
  • Muong Hoa valley
  • O Quy Ho pass

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Fansipan cable car

Reach the 3,143 m summit in just 15 minutes.

Village trekking

Walk through Lao Chai and Ta Van, stay with a Dao family.

Highland markets

The colourful Sa Pa love market and Bac Ha market.

Cloud hunting

Watch a sea of clouds on O Quy Ho pass at sunrise.

Travel tips

  • Nights can drop below 10°C – bring warm clothes.
  • Hire a local guide when trekking.

Getting to Sa Pa

Sleeper bus or limousine from Hanoi ~5.5 h via the expressway, or the night train to Lao Cai then 35 km by road.

Tour itineraries

Sa Pa tour itineraries

Sa Pa tour itineraries

Việt Travel · Lao Cai · Best time: September – November (rice harvest)

3 days 2 nights · A town in the mist → Conquer Fansipan → Trek Muong Hoa

Day 1: A town in the mist

  1. Morning

    Arrive in Sa Pa by sleeper bus, check in and have chicken phở for breakfast.

  2. Afternoon

    Walk down to Cat Cat village, see Tien Sa waterfall and H'Mong houses.

  3. Evening

    Visit the Stone Church and night market, enjoy grilled snacks and apple wine.

Day 2: Conquer Fansipan

  1. Morning

    Take the cable car to the 3,143 m Fansipan summit, hunt clouds and visit the spiritual complex.

  2. Afternoon

    See O Quy Ho pass, Silver Waterfall and Heaven's Gate.

  3. Evening

    Try salmon hotpot and a Red Dao herbal bath.

Day 3: Trek Muong Hoa

  1. Morning

    Trek Lao Chai – Ta Van through the rice terraces.

  2. Afternoon

    Lunch at a homestay and shop for brocade in Ta Van.

  3. Evening

    Stay with a Dao family, enjoy bamboo rice and local pork.

End of tour: check out, shop for local specialties and head home.
