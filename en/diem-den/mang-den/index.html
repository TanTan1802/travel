Overview

Why visit Mang Den?

A plateau town nearly 1,200 m up that stays cool all year, surrounded by pine forest, lakes and waterfalls. Mang Den is still unspoilt, with its Our Lady of Mang Den statue, the Mo Nam village of Kon Pring, Dak Ke lake and wild cherry blossoms in early spring. It is only about 1.5 hours from Kon Tum city.

Highlights not to miss

  • Our Lady of Mang Den statue
  • Dak Ke lake
  • Pa Sy waterfall
  • Kon Pring village

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Paddleboard on Dak Ke lake

A still lake among pines, best on misty mornings.

Pa Sy waterfall

Falls in old forest with a suspension bridge and streamside trail.

Kon Pring village

Communal houses, stilt homes and Mo Nam basket weaving.

Kon Tum wooden church

Stop on the way at this century-old wooden church.

Travel tips

  • Nights can drop to 10 – 15°C – bring warm layers.
  • In the rainy season (May – Oct) mountain roads are slippery and foggy – drive slowly.

Getting to Mang Den

Fly to Pleiku (PXU) and drive ~3 h via Kon Tum city; or take a sleeper bus from Da Nang or Quang Ngai to Kon Tum, then ~1.5 h more.

Mang Den tour itineraries

3 days 2 nights · Kon Tum – up to Mang Den → Lakes and falls → Kon Pring and Our Lady of Mang Den

Day 1: Kon Tum – up to Mang Den

  1. Morning

    Fly to Pleiku and drive to Kon Tum for the wooden church and Kon Klor communal house.

  2. Afternoon

    Cross the pass to Mang Den (~1.5 h) and check in among the pines.

  3. Evening

    Grilled chicken, bamboo rice and jar wine.

Day 2: Lakes and falls

  1. Morning

    Chase the morning mist and paddleboard on Dak Ke lake.

  2. Afternoon

    Pa Sy waterfall with its suspension bridge and streamside trail.

  3. Evening

    Sturgeon hotpot in the cool evening air.

Day 3: Kon Pring and Our Lady of Mang Den

  1. Morning

    The Our Lady of Mang Den statue on a pine hill.

  2. Afternoon

    The Mo Nam village of Kon Pring and its basket weavers.

  3. Evening

    Mang Den market and coffee by the fire.

End of tour: check out, shop for local specialties and head home.
