Overview

Why visit Mang Den?

A plateau town nearly 1,200 m up that stays cool all year, surrounded by pine forest, lakes and waterfalls. Mang Den is still unspoilt, with its Our Lady of Mang Den statue, the Mo Nam village of Kon Pring, Dak Ke lake and wild cherry blossoms in early spring. It is only about 1.5 hours from Kon Tum (the former provincial capital).

Highlights not to miss

  • Our Lady of Mang Den statue
  • Dak Ke lake
  • Pa Sy waterfall
  • Kon Pring village

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Paddleboard on Dak Ke lake

A still lake among pines, best on misty mornings.

Pa Sy waterfall

Falls in old forest with a suspension bridge and streamside trail.

Kon Pring village

Communal houses, stilt homes and Mo Nam basket weaving.

Kon Tum wooden church

Stop on the way at this century-old wooden church.

Travel tips

  • Nights can drop to 10 – 15°C – bring warm layers.
  • In the rainy season (May – Oct) mountain roads are slippery and foggy – drive slowly.

Getting to Mang Den

Fly to Pleiku (PXU) and drive ~3 h via Kon Tum; or take a sleeper bus from Da Nang or Quang Ngai to Kon Tum, then ~1.5 h more.
