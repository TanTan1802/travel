Overview

Why visit Mu Cang Chai?

The Mu Cang Chai terraces are a national scenic monument carved into the H'Mong mountainsides. In May–June the flooded fields shine like mirrors; in September–October the ripe rice turns the whole valley gold. Khau Pha pass – one of the "four great passes" – is a paragliding paradise.

Highlights not to miss

  • Khau Pha pass
  • La Pan Tan

Photograph Mam Xoi hill

H'Mong homestay

Travel tips

  • The "Flying over the golden season" paragliding festival usually takes place in late September.
  • The pass is often foggy – take a bus or hire an experienced driver.

3 days 2 nights · Nghia Lo – Tu Le → Khau Pha pass – Mam Xoi → Che Cu Nha – De Xu Phinh

Day 1: Nghia Lo – Tu Le

  1. Morning

    Night bus from Hanoi, breakfast on Tu Le sticky rice.

  2. Afternoon

    Soak in Tu Le hot springs and admire the rice fields.

  3. Evening

    Stay at a Thai homestay with bamboo rice and hill chicken.

Day 2: Khau Pha pass – Mam Xoi

  1. Morning

    Cross Khau Pha pass and watch paragliders (harvest season).

  2. Afternoon

    Photograph Mam Xoi hill and the La Pan Tan terraces.

  3. Evening

    Stay at an H'Mong homestay and taste smoked buffalo.

Day 3: Che Cu Nha – De Xu Phinh

  1. Morning

    Chase clouds and admire the Che Cu Nha terraces.

  2. Afternoon

    De Xu Phinh village and its horseshoe-shaped terraces.

  3. Evening

    Folk music and corn wine with the H'Mong.

End of tour: check out, shop for local specialties and head home.
