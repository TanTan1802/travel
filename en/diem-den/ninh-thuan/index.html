Overview

Why visit Vinh Hy – Ninh Thuan?

Vietnam's sunniest, driest corner is home to crystal-clear Vinh Hy bay inside Nui Chua National Park, the moss-covered rocks of Hang Rai, the Nam Cuong sand dunes and vineyards heavy with grapes. It is also the heartland of Cham culture, with the 700-year-old Po Klong Garai towers and Bau Truc pottery village.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Glass-bottom boat on Vinh Hy bay

See the coral, snorkel and eat seafood on a floating raft.

Sunrise at Hang Rai

Ancient coral rocks covered in green moss, best at low tide.

Nam Cuong sand dunes

Vast golden dunes beside a Cham village – best in late afternoon light.

Po Klong Garai & Bau Truc pottery

A 13th-century Cham temple and a village that shapes pots by hand without a wheel.

Travel tips

  • The rainy season (Sep – Dec) brings rough seas – visit in the dry months.
  • Harsh sun and strong wind – bring sunscreen, sunglasses and a scarf for the dunes.

Getting to Vinh Hy – Ninh Thuan

Fly to Cam Ranh (CXR), then ~1 h (60 km) by road to Phan Rang; Reunification Express trains stop at Thap Cham; buses from Saigon take ~6 h.

Vinh Hy – Ninh Thuan tour itineraries

Việt Travel · Khanh Hoa (formerly Ninh Thuan) · Best time: January – August

3 days 2 nights · Phan Rang and the Cham towers → Vinh Hy bay → Hang Rai and the dunes

Day 1: Phan Rang and the Cham towers

  1. Morning

    Fly to Cam Ranh, drive ~1 h to Phan Rang and breakfast on bánh căn.

  2. Afternoon

    Po Klong Garai towers on Trau hill and Bau Truc village, where pots are shaped by hand.

  3. Evening

    Stroll Ninh Chu beach; grilled goat and anchovy salad for dinner.

Day 2: Vinh Hy bay

  1. Morning

    Take the coastal road through Nui Chua National Park to Vinh Hy bay.

  2. Afternoon

    Glass-bottom boat over the coral, snorkelling and seafood on a raft.

  3. Evening

    Visit Thai An vineyards to taste grape wine and syrup.

Day 3: Hang Rai and the dunes

  1. Morning

    Sunrise on the Hang Rai rocks at low tide.

  2. Afternoon

    Nam Cuong sand dunes beside a Cham village, with a dune-buggy ride.

  3. Evening

    Sunset at Nai lagoon with grilled snails and fish.

End of tour: check out, shop for local specialties and head home.
