Overview

Why visit Phu Quy Island?

About 120 km south-east of Phan Thiet, Phu Quy island (Cu Lao Thu) has crystal-clear water, coral reefs around Hon Tranh, the Ganh Hang rock shelves and wind turbines turning above the sea. Island life is still slow and peaceful, with the Van An Thanh whale temple, Linh Son pagoda on Cam mountain looking out to sea and fresh, inexpensive seafood – ideal for 3 – 4 days exploring by motorbike.

Highlights not to miss

  • Linh Son pagoda
  • Ganh Hang rocks
  • Hon Tranh islet
  • Van An Thanh whale temple

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Snorkel at Hon Tranh

Take a boat to the little Hon Tranh islet to snorkel over coral and swim in clear water.

Linh Son pagoda on Cam mountain

The island's oldest pagoda, with a Guanyin statue facing the sea and views over the fishing village.

Sunset at Ganh Hang

Layered rock shelves and sea caves right at the water – best in the late afternoon.

Ride around the island

About 30 km of coastal road past the wind farm, fishing villages and whale temples.

Travel tips

  • The Phan Thiet – Phu Quy fast boat takes about 2.5 – 3 hours and often stops in rough seas (north-east monsoon, October – February) – book ahead, bring seasickness tablets and keep a spare day.
  • Rent a motorbike to get around; ATMs are few – bring enough cash, especially for the Hon Tranh trip.

Getting to Phu Quy Island

Fast boat from Phan Thiet port, about 2.5 – 3 h (usually 1 – 2 sailings a day, cancelled in rough seas). To Phan Thiet: train from Saigon in about 4 h, or a bus on the expressway in about 3 h.
