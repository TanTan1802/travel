Overview

Why visit Cat Ba – Lan Ha Bay?

Cat Ba Island is a world biosphere reserve with a primeval national park, clear Cat Co beaches and Lan Ha Bay with over 400 limestone islets. Quieter than Ha Long, Lan Ha is ideal for kayaking and swimming on small beaches reachable only by boat.

Highlights not to miss

  • Lan Ha Bay
  • Cat Co beaches
  • Cat Ba National Park
  • Cannon Fort

Activities

National park trekking

Swim at Cat Co

Three Cat Co beaches linked by a cliffside walkway.

Cannon Fort

Watch the sunset from the top of the fort.

Travel tips

  • Combo bus + speedboat tickets from Hanoi via Got pier take about 3–4 hours.
  • Summer weekends are very busy – book rooms and ferry tickets early.
Tour itineraries

Cat Ba – Lan Ha Bay tour itineraries

3 days 2 nights · Cat Ba island → Lan Ha Bay → National park

Day 1: Cat Ba island

  1. Morning

    Travel Hanoi – Hai Phong, take the speedboat to the island and check in.

  2. Afternoon

    Swim at Cat Co 1, 2 and 3 and walk the cliffside path.

  3. Evening

    Sunset at Cannon Fort and seafood at the fishing pier.

Day 2: Lan Ha Bay

  1. Morning

    Sail to Lan Ha Bay and kayak through the Light and Dark caves.

  2. Afternoon

    Swim at Ba Trai Dao beach and visit Cai Beo fishing village.

  3. Evening

    Stroll the town's walking street and try crab noodle soup.

Day 3: National park

  1. Morning

    Trek in Cat Ba National Park and climb Ngu Lam peak.

  2. Afternoon

    Visit Hospital Cave and Trung Trang cave.

  3. Evening

    Dinner on a floating raft with night views of the bay.

End of tour: check out, shop for local specialties and head home.
