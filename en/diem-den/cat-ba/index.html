Overview

Why visit Cat Ba – Lan Ha Bay?

Cat Ba Island is a world biosphere reserve with a primeval national park, clear Cat Co beaches and Lan Ha Bay with over 400 limestone islets. Quieter than Ha Long, Lan Ha is ideal for kayaking and swimming on small beaches reachable only by boat.

Highlights not to miss

  • Lan Ha Bay
  • Cat Co beaches
  • Cat Ba National Park
  • Cannon Fort

Festivals & seasonal highlights

  • Summer peak season June – August

    School holidays make beaches their busiest of the year.

    Travel midweek and book early for better prices.

  • Rough-sea season June – September

    Monsoon winds bring rough seas; island tours and ferries may be cancelled.

    Keep a spare day in case boats are cancelled.

Lunar-calendar festivals move each year – check the official dates before you go.
