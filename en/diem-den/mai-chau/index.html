Overview

Why visit Mai Chau Valley?

Mai Chau valley is about 140 km from Hanoi, where Thai villages such as Lac and Pom Coong sit among rice fields ringed by limestone mountains. Visitors come to cycle through the paddies, sleep in stilt houses, watch xoe dancing and eat bamboo rice and stream fish. It makes a great weekend trip or a stop on the way to Moc Chau and Pu Luong.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Cycle Lac – Pom Coong

Small lanes through rice fields, stopping to watch brocade weaving.

Thung Khe pass

Stop for a panoramic view over the valley.

Chieu cave

Climb over 1,000 stone steps to the cave above the Mai Chau fields.

Xoe dancing by the campfire

Thai folk performances and tube wine in the evening.

Travel tips

  • Stay at least one night in a stilt house to feel the rhythm of village life.
  • Brocade bought directly from homes in Lac village is usually cheaper and nicer than at souvenir stalls.

Getting to Mai Chau Valley

Bus or limousine from Hanoi ~3.5 h (140 km) via the Thang Long boulevard and Hoa Binh, frequent departures from My Dinh station.
