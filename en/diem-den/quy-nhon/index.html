Overview

Why visit Quy Nhon?

Quy Nhon has a long, clear coastline, unspoilt beaches such as Ky Co and Eo Gio and ancient Cham towers. The city keeps a slow pace of life, fresh and affordable seafood and is the home of traditional Binh Dinh martial arts.

Highlights not to miss

  • Ky Co
  • Eo Gio
  • Twin Towers
  • Ghenh Rang Tien Sa

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Speedboat to Ky Co

A beach dubbed "the Maldives of Vietnam".

Snorkel at Bai Dua

A coral reef close to shore at Hon Kho.

Cham towers

The Twin Towers and Banh It towers, over 800 years old.

Martial arts show

Quang Trung Museum and Tay Son martial arts performances.

Travel tips

  • Rent a motorbike for the beautiful coastal road to Eo Gio and Ky Co.
  • The rainy season runs from October to December with rough seas.

Getting to Quy Nhon

Phu Cat Airport (UIH) is ~30 km from the city; Dieu Tri Station ~10 km. ~5–6 h by road from Da Nang or Nha Trang.

Flights to UIH Train (Diêu Trì station) Bus / limousine tickets
