Overview

Why visit Co To Island?

The Co To archipelago is about 1.5 hours by fast boat from Cai Rong port (Van Don), known for the fine white sand of Hong Van and Van Chay beaches, casuarina forests and a winding coastal road. It has a lighthouse overlooking the whole island, the wave-sculpted rocks of Cau My and wild Co To Con islet – at low tide you can walk across the sandbar between the two.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Sunrise at Cau My rocks

Wave-carved rocks in every shape as the sun comes up.

Climb Co To lighthouse

Walk to Co To Con

Cross the sandbar at low tide and camp overnight.

Travel tips

  • Summer weekends are packed – book boats and rooms ahead; boats stop during storms.
  • There are few ATMs on the island – bring enough cash.

Getting to Co To Island

From Hanoi, a ~3.5 h limousine to Cai Rong port (Van Don), then a ~1.5 h fast boat to Co To; or fly to Van Don airport (VDO).

Day 1: Out to the island

  1. Morning

    A ~3.5 h limousine from Hanoi to Cai Rong port (Van Don).

  2. Afternoon

    A ~1.5 h fast boat to Co To; check in and rent a bicycle or e-cart.

  3. Evening

    Wander the town and have a seafront seafood dinner.

Day 2: Hong Van and the lighthouse

  1. Morning

    Sunrise at the Cau My rocks.

  2. Afternoon

    Swim at Hong Van beach and cycle the coastal road through casuarina forest.

  3. Evening

    Sunset over the island from Co To lighthouse.

Day 3: Co To Con

  1. Morning

    Walk the sandbar to Co To Con at low tide.

  2. Afternoon

    Swim on wild beaches, fish and camp.

  3. Evening

    Grilled squid and peanut worms by the campfire.

End of tour: check out, shop for local specialties and head home.
