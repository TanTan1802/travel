Overview

Why visit Binh Lieu?

Binh Lieu is a mountainous border district of Quang Ninh, famous for its winding grassy ridge road – the "dinosaur spine" – along the border markers, white reed flowers in October – November and Khe Van waterfall. It is home to the Tay, Dao Thanh Phan and San Chi peoples, with market days, the Soong Co festival and handmade arrowroot glass noodles.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

The dinosaur spine

Walk the border patrol road winding over the grassy hills.

Border marker 1300

Climb the stone steps to the marker for views over both sides.

Khe Van waterfall

A multi-tiered forest waterfall – swim in the stream in summer.

Binh Lieu market

Buy arrowroot noodles and meet Dao Thanh Phan women in traditional dress.

Travel tips

  • This is a border area: carry ID or a passport, do not fly drones and never cross the markers.
  • In reed season (October – November) the bare hills are sunny – bring a hat, water and grippy shoes.

Getting to Binh Lieu

From Hanoi a bus takes about 5 h via Ha Long – Tien Yen (about 250 km); from Ha Long about 3 h. The nearest airport is Van Don (VDO).
