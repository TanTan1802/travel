Overview

Why visit Nha Trang?

Nha Trang Bay is a member of the Club of the Most Beautiful Bays in the World, with long sandy beaches, clear water and rich coral reefs. Go diving, soak in mineral mud baths or explore the ancient Po Nagar Cham towers.

Highlights not to miss

  • Po Nagar towers
  • Hon Mun island
  • VinWonders
  • Binh Ba island

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

Snorkelling

Hon Mun marine reserve with crystal-clear water.

Four-island tour

Hon Mun, Hon Tam, Hon Mot and Bai Tranh in one day.

Mineral mud bath

Relax at Thap Ba or I-Resort.

VinWonders

An amusement park on Hon Tre island.

Travel tips

  • A short rainy season runs from October to December.
  • Buy seafood at Xom Moi market and have a restaurant cook it to save money.
Itinerary

3-day itinerary for Nha Trang

  1. Morning

    Po Nagar towers and Long Son Pagoda.

  2. Afternoon

    Relax in a hot mineral mud bath.

  3. Evening

    Stroll Tran Phu beach and eat seafood at Xom Moi market.

  1. Morning

    Snorkel the Hon Mun coral reef.

  2. Afternoon

    Hon Tam, Bai Tranh and a floating party at sea.

  3. Evening

    Fish cake noodle soup and Ninh Hoa grilled pork rolls.

  1. Morning

    Take the sea-crossing cable car to Hon Tre.

  2. Afternoon

    Water park and rides at VinWonders.

  3. Evening

    Watch the Tata musical fountain show, then return to the mainland.
