Overview

Why visit Nha Trang?

Nha Trang Bay is a member of the Club of the Most Beautiful Bays in the World, with long sandy beaches, clear water and rich coral reefs. Go diving, soak in mineral mud baths or explore the ancient Po Nagar Cham towers.

Highlights not to miss

  • Po Nagar towers
  • Hon Mun island
  • VinWonders
  • Binh Ba island

Festivals & seasonal highlights

  • Summer peak season June – August

    School holidays make beaches their busiest of the year.

    Travel midweek and book early for better prices.

  • Central Vietnam storm season September – November

    Heavy rain, possible floods and typhoons; Hoi An and Hue may flood.

    Watch forecasts and book flexible tickets/rooms; island boats may be suspended.

Lunar-calendar festivals move each year – check the official dates before you go.

Getting to Nha Trang

Cam Ranh Airport (CXR) is ~35 km from town: shuttle bus or taxi ~40 min. Nha Trang Station is right in the centre.
