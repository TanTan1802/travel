Overview

Why visit Phu Yen – Ganh Da Dia?

Phu Yen is wild and poetic, home to Ganh Da Dia – thousands of hexagonal basalt columns packed together like a honeycomb, formed by volcanic eruptions. Mui Dien greets the earliest sunrise on mainland Vietnam, while Xuan Dai Bay and O Loan lagoon are peaceful fishing-village scenes.

  • Combine Phu Yen with Quy Nhon in one trip.
Tour itineraries

Phu Yen – Ganh Da Dia tour itineraries

3 days 2 nights · Mui Dien – Bai Mon → Ganh Da Dia – Xuan Dai → Hon Yen – Ganh Ong

Day 1: Mui Dien – Bai Mon

  1. Morning

    Catch the earliest mainland sunrise at Mui Dien and climb Dai Lanh lighthouse.

  2. Afternoon

    Swim at Bai Mon and visit O Loan lagoon.

  3. Evening

    Tuna eyes and Nhan Tower by night.

Day 2: Ganh Da Dia – Xuan Dai

  1. Morning

    Explore Ganh Da Dia and Mang Lang church.

  2. Afternoon

    Boat on Xuan Dai Bay and Do Do village (from the film "Mat Biec").

  3. Evening

    Blood cockles from O Loan lagoon.

Day 3: Hon Yen – Ganh Ong

  1. Morning

    Hon Yen – coral reefs revealed at low tide.

  2. Afternoon

    Ganh Ong and Bai Xep – locations of "Yellow Flowers on the Green Grass".

  3. Evening

    Back to Tuy Hoa for chive noodle soup.

End of tour: check out, shop for local specialties and head home.
