Overview

Why visit Ca Mau Cape?

Ca Mau Cape is the southernmost point of mainland Vietnam, home to the national GPS 0001 marker, a ship-shaped monument facing the East Sea and mangrove forests that push tens of metres further into the sea every year. A trip to "Dat Mui" means boat rides along mangrove-shaded canals, Ca Mau crab and salted crab, and a visit to the U Minh Ha melaleuca forest.

Activities

Unforgettable experiences

GPS 0001 marker & ship monument

Photos at the southernmost point, where the sun rises and sets over the sea.

Sea-watch tower

Views over the mangroves and the East Sea from above.

Boat through the mangroves

Wind along the canals and watch locals catch crabs and set shrimp traps.

U Minh Ha melaleuca forest

Paddle through the forest and learn about wild-honey hunting.

Travel tips

  • From Ca Mau city you can reach Dat Mui by car or speedboat – check boat times and road conditions first.
  • Bring insect repellent and long sleeves for the mangrove and melaleuca forests.

Getting to Ca Mau Cape

Fly to Ca Mau (CAH) or Can Tho (VCA); buses from Saigon take ~8 h. From Ca Mau city to Dat Mui by car or speedboat.
