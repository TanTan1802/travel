Da Lat in 3 days: lakes, tea hills and waterfalls

Overview

Cool highland air: Xuan Huong lake, a cable car over pine forest to Tuyen Lam lake, the Datanla falls coaster and cloud-hunting at Cau Dat tea hills.

Day-by-day plan

  1. Day 1: Central Da Lat

    Stroll Xuan Huong lake, ride the old train from Da Lat station, then street food at the night market.

    • Xuan Huong lake – Lam Vien square Free Open all day Trần Quốc Toản, Đà Lạt
    • Da Lat station – Trai Mat train 150k Trains 7:45am – 4pm 1 Quang Trung, Đà Lạt
    • Da Lat night market Free 17:00–23:00 Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai, Đà Lạt

  2. Day 2: Pine forest – Tuyen Lam lake – waterfall

    Robin cable car down to Truc Lam monastery and Tuyen Lam lake, then the Datanla falls alpine coaster.

    • Robin cable car – Truc Lam Zen Monastery 100k–130k 07:30–17:00 Đèo Prenn, Đà Lạt
    • Tuyen Lam lake Free Open all day Tuyền Lâm, Đà Lạt
    • Datanla falls – alpine coaster 50k–250k 07:00–17:00 Đèo Prenn, Đà Lạt

  3. Day 3: Cau Dat tea hills

    Rise early for the clouds over Cau Dat tea hills, pick strawberries, then head home.

    • Cau Dat tea hills 50k 06:00–17:00 Xuân Trường, Đà Lạt
    • Strawberry farm 50k–100k 07:00–17:00 Thái Phiên / Vạn Thành, Đà Lạt
