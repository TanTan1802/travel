The Ha Giang loop in 4 days: Quan Ba – Dong Van – Ma Pi Leng · Ha Giang · 4 days, 3 nights

  1. Day 1: Ha Giang – Quan Ba – Yen Minh

    Photo at the Km0 marker, up to Quan Ba gate and the twin mountains, through the Yen Minh pine forest.

    • Ha Giang Km0 marker Free Open all day Trước UBND tỉnh, TP Hà Giang
    • Quan Ba Heaven Gate – Fairy Bosom hills Free Open all day Tam Sơn, Quản Bạ
    • Yen Minh pine forest Free Open all day QL4C, Yên Minh
    + Add places in Ha Giang (2)
    • Hoang Su Phi rice terraces Free Open all day Hoàng Su Phì, Hà Giang
    • Nam Hong tea hills Free Open all day Nậm Hồng, Hoàng Su Phì

  2. Day 2: Vuong palace – Lung Cu – Dong Van

    The Vuong palace, Tham Ma slope, Lung Cu flag tower at the northern tip, then Dong Van old quarter at night.

    • Vuong family mansion 20k 07:00–17:30 Sà Phìn, Đồng Văn
    • Tham Ma slope Free Open all day QL4C, Phố Cáo
    • Lung Cu flag tower 25k 07:00–17:30 Lũng Cú, Đồng Văn
    • Dong Van old quarter Free Open all day Thị trấn Đồng Văn

  3. Day 3: Ma Pi Leng – Nho Que river

    Over Ma Pi Leng pass, a boat through Tu San canyon on the Nho Que, then the Meo Vac – Du Gia road.

    • Ma Pi Leng pass – Tu San canyon Free Open all day QL4C, Đồng Văn – Mèo Vạc
    • Nho Que river boat 150k–200k 07:00–17:00 Bến thuyền Tà Làng, Mèo Vạc
    • Meo Vac – Du Gia road Free Open all day Mèo Vạc – Yên Minh

  4. Day 4: Du Gia – back to Ha Giang

    Swim at Du Gia waterfall in the morning, then back to Ha Giang town.

    • Du Gia waterfall Free Open all day Du Già, Yên Minh

Tips

  • Steep passes and hairpins: if you are not used to mountain riding, hire a local easy rider.
  • Border area – carry ID or a passport; buckwheat flowers bloom in Oct – Nov.
