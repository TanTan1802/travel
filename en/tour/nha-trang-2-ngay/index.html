Overview

Day one on Hon Tre island with the cable car and VinWonders; day two snorkelling at Hon Mun and a mineral mud bath.

Day-by-day plan

  1. Day 1: Hon Tre – VinWonders

    Cable car across the sea, theme park and water park all day, the Tata water show at night, then back to the mainland.

    • Cable car + VinWonders Nha Trang 800k–950k 08:30–20:00 Ga cáp treo Vĩnh Nguyên, Nha Trang
    • VinWonders water park Free 09:00–18:00 Đảo Hòn Tre
    • Tata water music show Free ~7pm VinWonders, đảo Hòn Tre

  2. Day 2: Hon Mun – mud bath

    Boat to the Hon Mun marine reserve for snorkelling, then an afternoon hot mineral mud bath.

    • Hon Mun island tour – snorkelling 250k–450k 08:00–16:00 Cảng Cầu Đá, Nha Trang
    • I-Resort mud bath 300k–450k 07:00–19:00 Tổ 19, Vĩnh Ngọc
