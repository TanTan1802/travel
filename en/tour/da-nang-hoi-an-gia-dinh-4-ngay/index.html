Da Nang – Hoi An family trip, 4 days · Da Nang · 4 days, 3 nights

  1. Day 1: My Khe beach – Han River

    Check in, swim at My Khe, then a Han River cruise and the Dragon Bridge at night.

    • My Khe beach Free Open all day Võ Nguyên Giáp, Sơn Trà
    • Han river cruise 150k–200k 18:00–22:00 Bến du thuyền Bạch Đằng, Hải Châu
    • Dragon Bridge Free Fire show 9pm Sat & Sun Nguyễn Văn Linh, Đà Nẵng
    + Add places in Da Nang (7)
    • Non Nuoc stone-carving village Free 07:00–18:00 Huyền Trân Công Chúa, Ngũ Hành Sơn
    • Son Tra night market Free 17:00–23:30 Mai Hắc Đế, Sơn Trà
    • Hai Van pass Free Open all day QL1A, Liên Chiểu
    • Lang Co bay Free Open all day Lăng Cô, Phú Lộc, Huế
    • Tra Que vegetable village Free 07:00–17:00 Cẩm Hà, Hội An
    • Cu Lao Cham speedboat & snorkelling 650k–800k Boats leave ~8am Cảng Cửa Đại, Hội An Find tickets online
    • Bai Chong beach – Bai Lang village Free Open all day Cù Lao Chàm

  2. Day 2: Ba Na Hills – Golden Bridge

    Go early to beat the crowds: cable car up to the Golden Bridge, the French Village and the indoor play zone.

    • Ba Na Hills – Golden Bridge 950k 07:00–22:00 Hòa Ninh, Hòa Vang Find tickets online
    • French Village – Le Jardin D'Amour Free 08:00–21:00 Đỉnh Bà Nà

  3. Day 3: Hoi An

    Basket boats in the Bay Mau coconut forest in the morning, then the old town and lanterns on the Hoai River at dusk.

    • Bay Mau coconut forest – basket boat 100k–150k 07:00–17:30 Cẩm Thanh, Hội An
    • Hoi An Old Town ticket – Japanese Bridge 120k 07:00–21:30 Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai, Hội An
    • Lanterns on the Hoai river 20k–50k 17:30–22:00 Bến sông Hoài, Hội An

  4. Day 4: Marble Mountains – Son Tra

    Climb the Marble Mountains, visit Linh Ung pagoda over the bay, check out and fly home.

    • Marble Mountains 40k 07:00–17:30 81 Huyền Trân Công Chúa, Ngũ Hành Sơn
    • Linh Ung Pagoda, Son Tra Free 06:00–21:30 Bãi Bụt, Sơn Trà
