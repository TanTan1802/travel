Vietnam heritage: 12 destinations to visit

Citadels, old towns, sanctuaries and natural wonders recognised by UNESCO – where Vietnam's history and landscapes are best preserved.

Beaches & islandsMountains & forestsHeritageCitiesCavesNorthern VietnamCentral VietnamSouthern Vietnam
  • Ha Long Bay (Quang Ninh) – A natural wonder of the world in the Gulf of Tonkin. Best time: April – October. Entrance fees
  • Trang An – Ninh Binh (Ninh Binh) – "Ha Long Bay on land" among majestic karst peaks. Best time: May – June (golden rice). Entrance fees
  • Phong Nha – Ke Bang (Quang Tri (formerly Quang Binh)) – The kingdom of caves. Best time: March – August. Entrance fees
  • Hoi An Ancient Town (Da Nang (formerly Quang Nam)) – An ancient trading port glowing with lanterns. Best time: February – May. Entrance fees
  • Hanoi (Capital city) – A thousand years of culture around Hoan Kiem Lake. Best time: September – November (autumn). Entrance fees
  • Hue Imperial City (Hue City) – The Nguyen dynasty citadel beside the dreamy Perfume River. Best time: January – April. Entrance fees
  • Con Dao (Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Ba Ria – Vung Tau)) – Unspoilt nature and moving history. Best time: March – September. Entrance fees
  • Chau Doc – Sam Mountain (An Giang) – A spiritual land and cajuput forests in the flood season. Best time: September – November (flood season). Entrance fees
  • Ninh Thuan – Vinh Hy (Khanh Hoa (formerly Ninh Thuan)) – Sun, wind, vineyards and ancient Cham towers. Best time: January – August. Entrance fees
  • Ha Tien (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – A gentle border town on the Gulf of Thailand. Best time: November – April. Entrance fees
  • Tay Ninh – Ba Den Mountain (Tay Ninh) – The roof of the south and the Cao Dai Holy See. Best time: December – April. Entrance fees
  • Tam Dao (Phu Tho (formerly Vinh Phuc)) – A misty hill town close to Hanoi. Best time: April – September. Entrance fees
