Vietnam beaches & islands: 15 destinations to visit

Over 3,000 km of coastline give Vietnam limestone bays, white-sand beaches and unspoilt islands – from Ha Long Bay in the north to Phu Quoc in the south.

Beaches & islandsMountains & forestsHeritageCitiesCavesNorthern VietnamCentral VietnamSouthern Vietnam
  • Ha Long Bay (Quang Ninh) – A natural wonder of the world in the Gulf of Tonkin. Best time: April – October. Entrance fees
  • Da Nang (Da Nang) – A liveable city on My Khe beach. Best time: March – August. Entrance fees
  • Phu Quoc (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – The pearl island with Vietnam's most beautiful sunsets. Best time: November – April. Entrance fees
  • Cat Ba – Lan Ha Bay (Hai Phong) – A green island beside unspoilt Lan Ha Bay. Best time: April – September. Entrance fees
  • Con Dao (Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Ba Ria – Vung Tau)) – Unspoilt nature and moving history. Best time: March – September. Entrance fees
  • Nha Trang (Khanh Hoa) – The pearl of the East Sea. Best time: January – August. Entrance fees
  • Quy Nhon (Gia Lai (formerly Binh Dinh)) – A peaceful seaside city in the land of martial arts. Best time: March – September. Entrance fees
  • Phu Yen – Ganh Da Dia (Dak Lak (formerly Phu Yen)) – The "land of yellow flowers on green grass" with one-of-a-kind basalt cliffs. Best time: February – August. Entrance fees
  • Ly Son Island (Quang Ngai) – The garlic kingdom in the open sea. Best time: March – August. Entrance fees
  • Co To Island (Quang Ninh) – Unspoilt blue seas at the northeastern frontier. Best time: April – September. Entrance fees
  • Mui Ne (Lam Dong (formerly Binh Thuan)) – Vietnam's "little Sahara". Best time: November – April. Entrance fees
  • Ninh Thuan – Vinh Hy (Khanh Hoa (formerly Ninh Thuan)) – Sun, wind, vineyards and ancient Cham towers. Best time: January – August. Entrance fees
  • Vung Tau (Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Ba Ria – Vung Tau)) – The closest beach city to Saigon. Best time: November – April. Entrance fees
  • Ha Tien (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – A gentle border town on the Gulf of Thailand. Best time: November – April. Entrance fees
  • Ca Mau Cape (Ca Mau) – The southernmost tip, where mangroves reach into the sea. Best time: December – April. Entrance fees
