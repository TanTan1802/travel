Southern Vietnam: 10 destinations to visit

Lively Saigon, the Mekong Delta waterways and southern islands; warm all year, with a dry season from November to April.

Beaches & islandsMountains & forestsHeritageCitiesCavesNorthern VietnamCentral VietnamSouthern Vietnam
  • Phu Quoc (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – The pearl island with Vietnam's most beautiful sunsets. Best time: November – April. Entrance fees
  • Con Dao (Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Ba Ria – Vung Tau)) – Unspoilt nature and moving history. Best time: March – September. Entrance fees
  • Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon) – A vibrant city that never sleeps. Best time: December – April (dry season). Entrance fees
  • Can Tho – Mekong Delta (Can Tho) – Floating markets, fruit orchards and river life. Best time: September – November (flood season). Entrance fees
  • Chau Doc – Sam Mountain (An Giang) – A spiritual land and cajuput forests in the flood season. Best time: September – November (flood season). Entrance fees
  • Vung Tau (Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Ba Ria – Vung Tau)) – The closest beach city to Saigon. Best time: November – April. Entrance fees
  • Ha Tien (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – A gentle border town on the Gulf of Thailand. Best time: November – April. Entrance fees
  • Tay Ninh – Ba Den Mountain (Tay Ninh) – The roof of the south and the Cao Dai Holy See. Best time: December – April. Entrance fees
  • Ca Mau Cape (Ca Mau) – The southernmost tip, where mangroves reach into the sea. Best time: December – April. Entrance fees
  • Nam Du Islands (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – Unspoilt islands in the Gulf of Thailand. Best time: November – April. Entrance fees
