Vietnam mountains & forests: 12 destinations to visit

Rice terraces, high passes, ethnic villages and cool air – Vietnam's mountains suit travellers who love trekking, cloud-hunting and culture.

Beaches & islandsMountains & forestsHeritageCitiesCavesNorthern VietnamCentral VietnamSouthern Vietnam
  • Trang An – Ninh Binh (Ninh Binh) – "Ha Long Bay on land" among majestic karst peaks. Best time: May – June (golden rice). Entrance fees
  • Ha Giang (Ha Giang) – Vietnam's most spectacular mountain passes. Best time: October – November (buckwheat flowers). Entrance fees
  • Phong Nha – Ke Bang (Quang Binh) – The kingdom of caves. Best time: March – August. Entrance fees
  • Sa Pa (Lao Cai) – A town in the mist and its terraced rice fields. Best time: September – November (rice harvest). Entrance fees
  • Ban Gioc Waterfall (Cao Bang) – Southeast Asia's most beautiful border waterfall. Best time: August – October (high water, ripe rice). Entrance fees
  • Mu Cang Chai (Yen Bai) – The most beautiful rice terraces of the Northwest. Best time: September – October (ripe rice), May – June (flooded fields). Entrance fees
  • Cat Ba – Lan Ha Bay (Hai Phong) – A green island beside unspoilt Lan Ha Bay. Best time: April – September. Entrance fees
  • Da Lat (Lam Dong) – The city of a thousand flowers on the Lang Biang plateau. Best time: November – March (flower season). Entrance fees
  • Moc Chau Plateau (Son La) – Emerald tea hills and white mustard blossoms. Best time: October – March. Entrance fees
  • Pu Luong (Thanh Hoa) – Rice terraces, water wheels and peaceful Thai villages. Best time: May – June, September – October. Entrance fees
  • Chau Doc – Sam Mountain (An Giang) – A spiritual land and cajuput forests in the flood season. Best time: September – November (flood season). Entrance fees
  • Buon Ma Thuot (Dak Lak) – Vietnam's coffee capital and the Central Highlands. Best time: November – March. Entrance fees
