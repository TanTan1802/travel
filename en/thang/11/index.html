Where to go in Vietnam in November? 15 best destinations

The north is cool and buckwheat flowers bloom in Ha Giang; central Vietnam is still rainy; the south and Phu Quoc start their dry season.

Northern Vietnam

  • Ha Giang (Tuyen Quang (formerly Ha Giang)) – Vietnam's most spectacular mountain passes. Best time: October – November (buckwheat flowers).
  • Sa Pa (Lao Cai) – A town in the mist and its terraced rice fields. Best time: September – November (rice harvest).
  • Hanoi (Capital city) – A thousand years of culture around Hoan Kiem Lake. Best time: September – November (autumn).
  • Moc Chau Plateau (Son La) – Emerald tea hills and white mustard blossoms. Best time: October – March.
  • Binh Lieu (Quang Ninh) – The "dinosaur spine" and border reed flowers. Best time: March – April, September – November.

Central Vietnam

  • Da Lat (Lam Dong) – The city of a thousand flowers on the Lang Biang plateau. Best time: November – March (flower season).
  • Mui Ne (Lam Dong (formerly Binh Thuan)) – Vietnam's "little Sahara". Best time: November – April.
  • Buon Ma Thuot (Dak Lak) – Vietnam's coffee capital and the Central Highlands. Best time: November – March.
  • Mang Den (Quang Ngai (formerly Kon Tum)) – A "second Da Lat" in the Central Highlands forest. Best time: November – April.

Southern Vietnam

  • Phu Quoc (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – The pearl island with Vietnam's most beautiful sunsets. Best time: November – April.
  • Can Tho – Mekong Delta (Can Tho) – Floating markets, fruit orchards and river life. Best time: September – November (flood season).
  • Chau Doc – Sam Mountain (An Giang) – A spiritual land and cajuput forests in the flood season. Best time: September – November (flood season).
  • Vung Tau (Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Ba Ria – Vung Tau)) – The closest beach city to Saigon. Best time: November – April.
  • Ha Tien (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – A gentle border town on the Gulf of Thailand. Best time: November – April.
  • Nam Du Islands (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – Unspoilt islands in the Gulf of Thailand. Best time: November – April.

Festivals & things to know in November

  • Full-moon night in the old town · Hoi An Ancient Town Monthly · lunar calendar

    Every 14th lunar evening the old town switches to lantern light only.

    Check the lunar calendar to catch the full-moon night.

  • Mekong flood season · Can Tho – Mekong Delta, Chau Doc – Sam Mountain September – November

    Rising water brings linh fish, sesbania flowers and lush Tra Su forest.

    Try linh fish hotpot – only available this season.

  • Central Vietnam storm season · Hue Imperial City, Da Nang, Hoi An Ancient Town, Phong Nha – Ke Bang, Quy Nhon, Phu Yen – Ganh Da Dia, Ly Son Island, Nha Trang September – November

    Heavy rain, possible floods and typhoons; Hoi An and Hue may flood.

    Watch forecasts and book flexible tickets/rooms; island boats may be suspended.

  • Buckwheat flower season · Ha Giang October – November

    Pink-purple buckwheat flowers cover the hills around Dong Van and Sung La.

    Weekends peak – go midweek if you can.

  • White mustard flower season · Moc Chau Plateau November – December

    Fields of white mustard flowers across Chieng Di and Na Ka.

    Some fields charge 20,000–30,000đ for photos.

  • Wild sunflower season · Da Lat, Buon Ma Thuot November

    Wild sunflowers line the Highlands roads, Prenn pass and Langbiang.

    Sunny and cool – ideal for a motorbike ride.
