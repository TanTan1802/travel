Where to go in Vietnam in April? 22 best destinations

One of the most pleasant months nationwide; central beaches enter their best season. The 30 April – 1 May holiday is very busy.

Northern Vietnam

  • Ha Long Bay (Quang Ninh) – A natural wonder of the world in the Gulf of Tonkin. Best time: April – October.
  • Cat Ba – Lan Ha Bay (Hai Phong) – A green island beside unspoilt Lan Ha Bay. Best time: April – September.
  • Co To Island (Quang Ninh) – Unspoilt blue seas at the northeastern frontier. Best time: April – September.
  • Mai Chau Valley (Phu Tho (formerly Hoa Binh)) – Peaceful Thai villages in a valley of rice. Best time: March – May, September – October.

Central Vietnam

  • Phong Nha – Ke Bang (Quang Tri (formerly Quang Binh)) – The kingdom of caves. Best time: March – August.
  • Hoi An Ancient Town (Da Nang (formerly Quang Nam)) – An ancient trading port glowing with lanterns. Best time: February – May.
  • Da Nang (Da Nang) – A liveable city on My Khe beach. Best time: March – August.
  • Hue Imperial City (Hue City) – The Nguyen dynasty citadel beside the dreamy Perfume River. Best time: January – April.
  • Nha Trang (Khanh Hoa) – The pearl of the East Sea. Best time: January – August.
  • Quy Nhon (Gia Lai (formerly Binh Dinh)) – A peaceful seaside city in the land of martial arts. Best time: March – September.
  • Phu Yen – Ganh Da Dia (Dak Lak (formerly Phu Yen)) – The "land of yellow flowers on green grass" with one-of-a-kind basalt cliffs. Best time: February – August.
  • Ly Son Island (Quang Ngai) – The garlic kingdom in the open sea. Best time: March – August.
  • Mui Ne (Lam Dong (formerly Binh Thuan)) – Vietnam's "little Sahara". Best time: November – April.
  • Ninh Thuan – Vinh Hy (Khanh Hoa (formerly Ninh Thuan)) – Sun, wind, vineyards and ancient Cham towers. Best time: January – August.
  • Mang Den (Quang Ngai (formerly Kon Tum)) – A "second Da Lat" in the Central Highlands forest. Best time: November – April.

Southern Vietnam

  • Phu Quoc (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – The pearl island with Vietnam's most beautiful sunsets. Best time: November – April.
  • Con Dao (Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Ba Ria – Vung Tau)) – Unspoilt nature and moving history. Best time: March – September.
  • Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon) – A vibrant city that never sleeps. Best time: December – April (dry season).
  • Vung Tau (Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Ba Ria – Vung Tau)) – The closest beach city to Saigon. Best time: November – April.
  • Ha Tien (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – A gentle border town on the Gulf of Thailand. Best time: November – April.
  • Tay Ninh – Ba Den Mountain (Tay Ninh) – The roof of the south and the Cao Dai Holy See. Best time: December – April.
  • Ca Mau Cape (Ca Mau) – The southernmost tip, where mangroves reach into the sea. Best time: December – April.

Festivals & things to know in April

  • Full-moon night in the old town · Hoi An Ancient Town Monthly · lunar calendar

    Every 14th lunar evening the old town switches to lantern light only.

    Check the lunar calendar to catch the full-moon night.

  • Perfume Pagoda Festival · Hanoi February – April · lunar calendar

    From the 6th of the first lunar month to the end of the third – huge crowds on Yen stream.

    Avoid weekends in the first lunar month; set off by 6am.

  • Reunification Day & Labour Day holiday 28/04 – 03/05

    A long early-summer break; beaches and places near big cities get crowded.

    Book early and avoid road travel on the first and last day of the break.

  • Hue Festival · Hue Imperial City April – June

    Royal arts and culture events – the main edition is usually held in even years.

    Check the official schedule; central hotels sell out early.

  • Khau Vai Love Market · Ha Giang April – May · lunar calendar

    On the 26th–27th of the third lunar month in Meo Vac – a unique highland gathering.

    Book Meo Vac homestays early; mountain roads get busy.
