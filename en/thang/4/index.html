Where to go in Vietnam in April? 26 best destinations

One of the most pleasant months nationwide; central beaches enter their best season. The 30 April – 1 May holiday is very busy.

Northern Vietnam

  • Ha Long Bay (Quang Ninh) – A natural wonder of the world in the Gulf of Tonkin. Best time: April – October.
  • Cat Ba – Lan Ha Bay (Hai Phong) – A green island beside unspoilt Lan Ha Bay. Best time: April – September.
  • Co To Island (Quang Ninh) – Unspoilt blue seas at the northeastern frontier. Best time: April – September.
  • Mai Chau Valley (Phu Tho (formerly Hoa Binh)) – Peaceful Thai villages in a valley of rice. Best time: March – May, September – October.
  • Ba Be Lake (Thai Nguyen (formerly Bac Kan)) – Vietnam's largest natural freshwater lake in the northeastern mountains. Best time: March – June, September – October.
  • Binh Lieu (Quang Ninh) – The "dinosaur spine" and border reed flowers. Best time: March – April, September – November.
  • Tam Dao (Phu Tho (formerly Vinh Phuc)) – A misty hill town close to Hanoi. Best time: April – September.

Central Vietnam

  • Phong Nha – Ke Bang (Quang Tri (formerly Quang Binh)) – The kingdom of caves. Best time: March – August.
  • Hoi An Ancient Town (Da Nang (formerly Quang Nam)) – An ancient trading port glowing with lanterns. Best time: February – May.
  • Da Nang (Da Nang) – A liveable city on My Khe beach. Best time: March – August.
  • Hue Imperial City (Hue City) – The Nguyen dynasty citadel beside the dreamy Perfume River. Best time: January – April.
  • Nha Trang (Khanh Hoa) – The pearl of the East Sea. Best time: January – August.
  • Quy Nhon (Gia Lai (formerly Binh Dinh)) – A peaceful seaside city in the land of martial arts. Best time: March – September.
  • Phu Yen – Ganh Da Dia (Dak Lak (formerly Phu Yen)) – The "land of yellow flowers on green grass" with one-of-a-kind basalt cliffs. Best time: February – August.
  • Ly Son Island (Quang Ngai) – The garlic kingdom in the open sea. Best time: March – August.
  • Mui Ne (Lam Dong (formerly Binh Thuan)) – Vietnam's "little Sahara". Best time: November – April.
  • Ninh Thuan – Vinh Hy (Khanh Hoa (formerly Ninh Thuan)) – Sun, wind, vineyards and ancient Cham towers. Best time: January – August.
  • Mang Den (Quang Ngai (formerly Kon Tum)) – A "second Da Lat" in the Central Highlands forest. Best time: November – April.

Southern Vietnam

  • Phu Quoc (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – The pearl island with Vietnam's most beautiful sunsets. Best time: November – April.
  • Con Dao (Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Ba Ria – Vung Tau)) – Unspoilt nature and moving history. Best time: March – September.
  • Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon) – A vibrant city that never sleeps. Best time: December – April (dry season).
  • Vung Tau (Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Ba Ria – Vung Tau)) – The closest beach city to Saigon. Best time: November – April.
  • Ha Tien (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – A gentle border town on the Gulf of Thailand. Best time: November – April.
  • Tay Ninh – Ba Den Mountain (Tay Ninh) – The roof of the south and the Cao Dai Holy See. Best time: December – April.
  • Ca Mau Cape (Ca Mau) – The southernmost tip, where mangroves reach into the sea. Best time: December – April.
  • Nam Du Islands (An Giang (formerly Kien Giang)) – Unspoilt islands in the Gulf of Thailand. Best time: November – April.

Festivals & things to know in April

  • Full-moon night in the old town · Hoi An Ancient Town Monthly · lunar calendar

    Every 14th lunar evening the old town switches to lantern light only.

    Check the lunar calendar to catch the full-moon night.

  • Highland market days · Ha Giang, Sa Pa Monthly

    Rotating markets: Dong Van and Meo Vac (Ha Giang), Bac Ha and Can Cau (near Sa Pa) – colourful costumes, livestock trading, brocade and thang co stew.

    Most run on Sunday mornings (Can Cau: Saturday) and wind down by noon – plan to arrive by 7–8 am.

  • Perfume Pagoda Festival · Hanoi February – April · lunar calendar

    From the 6th of the first lunar month to the end of the third – huge crowds on Yen stream.

    Avoid weekends in the first lunar month; set off by 6am.

  • Northern 'nồm' humidity · Hanoi, Trang An – Ninh Binh, Ha Long Bay, Tam Dao February – April

    Humidity near 100%: floors and walls sweat, the sky is hazy and laundry won't dry; visibility on the bay and mountains can be poor.

    Bring grippy shoes, pick rooms with a dehumidifier or air-con, and check the forecast before a scenic cruise.

  • Bai Dinh – Trang An pilgrimage season · Trang An – Ninh Binh February – April · lunar calendar

    From the 6th day of the lunar new year to the end of the 3rd lunar month Bai Dinh pagoda holds its festival; Trang An and Hoa Lu festivals bring palanquin processions and folk performances.

    Weekends and the first weeks of spring are packed – go on a weekday, early in the morning; expect queues for Trang An boats.

  • Fishing villages' Cau Ngu (whale worship) festival · Quy Nhon, Phu Yen – Ganh Da Dia, Ly Son Island, Vung Tau February – June · lunar calendar

    Central-coast fishing villages honour the whale god for a safe season at sea: boat processions, ba trao rowing songs and basket-boat races.

    Each village has its own date – ask your host or the commune office to get it right.

  • Cave expedition season · Phong Nha – Ke Bang February – August

    In the dry season, expeditions to Son Doong, Hang En and Tu Lan run, and Phong Nha and Paradise caves are fully open.

    Son Doong and Hang En tours must be booked months ahead with a fitness check; many pause from September for the flood season.

  • Rhododendrons on Fansipan · Sa Pa March – April

    Red, pink and white rhododendrons bloom along the trails and around Fansipan's summit; clear, dry weather makes it great trekking season.

    Trekking requires a local guide; if you take the cable car, go up early before the clouds roll in.

  • Quan The Am Festival at the Marble Mountains · Da Nang March – April · lunar calendar

    On the 17th–19th days of the 2nd lunar month: Bodhisattva processions, lantern displays, martial arts and human chess at the foot of the Marble Mountains.

    Climb Thuy Son early in the morning in grippy shoes – the stone steps are slippery.

  • Purple jacaranda in Da Lat · Da Lat March – May

    Jacarandas bloom along Yersin, Tran Phu and Hung Vuong streets and around Xuan Huong Lake – sunny dry-season weather.

    Best on clear mornings; bring a jacket, as mornings and evenings are still cold.

  • Reunification Day & Labour Day holiday 28/04 – 03/05

    A long early-summer break; beaches and places near big cities get crowded.

    Book early and avoid road travel on the first and last day of the break.

  • Hue Festival · Hue Imperial City April – June

    Royal arts and culture events – the main edition is usually held in even years.

    Check the official schedule; central hotels sell out early.

  • Khau Vai Love Market · Ha Giang April – May · lunar calendar

    On the 26th–27th of the third lunar month in Meo Vac – a unique highland gathering.

    Book Meo Vac homestays early; mountain roads get busy.

  • Hung Kings' Commemoration (10th day of the 3rd lunar month) April · lunar calendar

    A national public holiday; when it falls next to a weekend or the 30 April break it becomes a long weekend and places near Hanoi get crowded.

    Check the official holiday dates each year and book early if it falls next to a weekend.

  • Easter lily season · Hanoi 01/04 – 30/04

    Throughout April, white Easter lilies ride on flower-sellers' bicycles across the Old Quarter – Hanoi's signature sign of the changing season.

    Buy bunches at Quang An flower market or from bicycle vendors – cheaper and fresher than shops.

  • Ly Son Feast for the Hoang Sa soldiers · Ly Son Island April – May · lunar calendar

    In mid-3rd lunar month: a ceremony honouring the historic Hoang Sa flotilla, with paper boats released to the sea – a national intangible heritage.

    Ferries fill up during the festival – book ahead and watch for cancellations in rough seas.

  • Po Nagar Tower Festival · Nha Trang April – May · lunar calendar

    On the 20th–23rd days of the 3rd lunar month: statue bathing and offering dances by Viet and Cham communities at the ancient Cham towers by the Cai River.

    Wear clothes covering shoulders and knees; go in the morning to avoid the harsh sun.
