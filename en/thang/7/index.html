Where to go in Vietnam in July? 12 best destinations

The north is hot with possible storms; central beaches stay sunny; the south has short showers.

Northern Vietnam

  • Ha Long Bay (Quang Ninh) – A natural wonder of the world in the Gulf of Tonkin. Best time: April – October.
  • Cat Ba – Lan Ha Bay (Hai Phong) – A green island beside unspoilt Lan Ha Bay. Best time: April – September.
  • Co To Island (Quang Ninh) – Unspoilt blue seas at the northeastern frontier. Best time: April – September.
  • Tam Dao (Phu Tho (formerly Vinh Phuc)) – A misty hill town close to Hanoi. Best time: April – September.

Central Vietnam

  • Phong Nha – Ke Bang (Quang Tri (formerly Quang Binh)) – The kingdom of caves. Best time: March – August.
  • Da Nang (Da Nang) – A liveable city on My Khe beach. Best time: March – August.
  • Nha Trang (Khanh Hoa) – The pearl of the East Sea. Best time: January – August.
  • Quy Nhon (Gia Lai (formerly Binh Dinh)) – A peaceful seaside city in the land of martial arts. Best time: March – September.
  • Phu Yen – Ganh Da Dia (Dak Lak (formerly Phu Yen)) – The "land of yellow flowers on green grass" with one-of-a-kind basalt cliffs. Best time: February – August.
  • Ly Son Island (Quang Ngai) – The garlic kingdom in the open sea. Best time: March – August.
  • Ninh Thuan – Vinh Hy (Khanh Hoa (formerly Ninh Thuan)) – Sun, wind, vineyards and ancient Cham towers. Best time: January – August.

Southern Vietnam

  • Con Dao (Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Ba Ria – Vung Tau)) – Unspoilt nature and moving history. Best time: March – September.

Festivals & things to know in July

  • Full-moon night in the old town · Hoi An Ancient Town Monthly · lunar calendar

    Every 14th lunar evening the old town switches to lantern light only.

    Check the lunar calendar to catch the full-moon night.

  • Highland market days · Ha Giang, Sa Pa Monthly

    Rotating markets: Dong Van and Meo Vac (Ha Giang), Bac Ha and Can Cau (near Sa Pa) – colourful costumes, livestock trading, brocade and thang co stew.

    Most run on Sunday mornings (Can Cau: Saturday) and wind down by noon – plan to arrive by 7–8 am.

  • Cave expedition season · Phong Nha – Ke Bang February – August

    In the dry season, expeditions to Son Doong, Hang En and Tu Lan run, and Phong Nha and Paradise caves are fully open.

    Son Doong and Hang En tours must be booked months ahead with a fitness check; many pause from September for the flood season.

  • Sea turtle nesting season · Con Dao May – September

    Watch turtles nest and hatchlings released on Bay Canh island.

    Must be booked in advance with Con Dao National Park.

  • Mekong orchard fruit season · Can Tho – Mekong Delta, Chau Doc – Sam Mountain May – August

    Durian, rambutan, mangosteen and mango are all in season – many orchards in Phong Dien and Cai Rang let you eat straight from the trees.

    Ask the entry price (usually covers eating on site; take-away is extra) and bring mosquito repellent.

  • Southern rainy season · Ho Chi Minh City, Can Tho – Mekong Delta, Phu Quoc, Nam Du Islands, Ha Tien, Ca Mau Cape May – October

    Short afternoon downpours that pass quickly; some Saigon streets flood in heavy rain and high tides; seas around the southern islands get rough.

    Plan outdoor activities in the morning and always carry a raincoat; boats to Nam Du and Phu Quoc may be cancelled in rough seas.

  • Summer peak season · Ha Long Bay, Cat Ba – Lan Ha Bay, Da Nang, Nha Trang, Quy Nhon, Phu Yen – Ganh Da Dia, Vung Tau, Phu Quoc, Ly Son Island, Con Dao, Mui Ne, Ninh Thuan – Vinh Hy, Co To Island June – August

    School holidays make beaches their busiest of the year.

    Travel midweek and book early for better prices.

  • Da Nang International Fireworks Festival · Da Nang, Hoi An Ancient Town June – July

    International teams put on shows over the Han River on weekend nights.

    Buy stand tickets or grab a riverside spot early; rooms get pricey.

  • Rough-sea season · Phu Quoc, Con Dao, Ha Tien, Cat Ba – Lan Ha Bay June – September

    Monsoon winds bring rough seas; island tours and ferries may be cancelled.

    Keep a spare day in case boats are cancelled.
