Ta Nang – Phan Dung trek

Lam Dong (formerly Lam Dong and Binh Thuan) · Moderate · 2 days, 1 nights · 28 – 35 km · 1,100 m

Start
Ta Nang commune (former Duc Trong)
  • Rolling grass hills across the former provinces of Lam Dong, Ninh Thuan and Binh Thuan
  • Pine forest, rocky streams and camping on windy hilltops
  • The most popular trek in southern Vietnam

Day-by-day plan

  1. Day 1: Ta Nang – grass hills – campsite

    Walk through pine forest and over grassy hills with lunch on the way, reaching a hilltop campsite in the afternoon; the guides cook and pitch the tents.

  2. Day 2: Grass hills – streams – Phan Dung

    Sunrise on the hills, then on via streams and dry forest down to Phan Dung, where a vehicle takes you back to the highway.

Finish: Phan Dung commune (former Tuy Phong)

Costs & permits

  • Usually done as a package with a tour operator (guides, porters, tents, meals, transport)

Safety

  • Hikers have gone missing and died when going alone – only go with local guides and leave your plan with family
  • Dry-season middays are very sunny with little shade – carry 2 – 3 litres of water
  • In the rainy season streams rise fast – never cross when the water is muddy and fast

General trekking safety rules

What to bring

  • Grippy shoes (hiking boots or deep-tread trainers) and thick socks
  • A light raincoat, hat and sunscreen
  • 1.5 – 2 litres of water and snacks
  • Personal medicine, plasters and insect repellent
  • A 25 – 35 litre pack with a hip belt and a waterproof liner
  • A base layer and a warm jacket for the night
  • A head torch, spare batteries and a power bank
  • Trekking poles – they save your knees on descents
  • Dry socks and clothes in a plastic bag

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Ta Nang – Phan Dung trek?
2 days, 1 nights, 28 – 35 km (the walking part of the usual two-day route; the full trail is about 50 km).
How hard is the Ta Nang – Phan Dung trek?
Moderate: Two days and one night, 6 – 8 hours of walking a day, sleeping in tents or huts – train with walks and stairs for a few weeks first.
Do I need a guide for the Ta Nang – Phan Dung trek?
Yes – you must go with a local guide or an operator.
When is the best time for the Ta Nang – Phan Dung trek?
January, February, March, April, November, December.
