Start
Nhiu Co San village, Sang Ma Sao commune (Bat Xat)

Since May 2026 Bat Xat National Park has banned unorganised climbing and trekking on several peaks, including this route; entering the forest without registering can be fined. Only go with a permitted operator and check first whether the route has reopened.

  • Rice terraces and stone trails of the Dao and Ha Nhi people at the foot of the mountain
  • Sunrise and a sea of clouds from the 2,965 m summit
  • Old-growth forest, bamboo slopes and mossy rocks on the way to the hut

Day-by-day plan

  1. Day 1: Sang Ma Sao – Nhiu Co San village – hut

    Ride about 7 km of dirt road to the village by motorbike, then walk past rice terraces, stone trails and forest to the overnight hut (usually arriving late afternoon).

  2. Day 2: Nhiu Co San summit – descent

    Wake around 4 – 5 am and climb about 2 hours to the 2,965 m summit for sunrise, return to the hut for breakfast, then descend to the village.

Finish: Nhiu Co San village

Costs & permits

  • Porters, local guides, the motorbike ride in and hut accommodation arranged by the operator – ask exactly what is included

Safety

  • Border area: carry ID or a passport
  • Nights at the hut are very cold, close to 0°C in winter – bring a sleeping bag and warm layers
  • Stone trails and forest get slippery in rain and thick fog rolls in – always follow your guide

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Nhiu Co San trek?
2 days, 1 nights, 16 – 21 km (up and down; operators measure it differently, not counting the 7 km motorbike ride to the village).
How hard is the Nhiu Co San trek?
Hard: Summits of 2,800 – 3,100 m, long relentless climbs and narrow rocky sections – you need good fitness and an overnight trek behind you.
Do I need a guide for the Nhiu Co San trek?
Yes – you must go with a local guide or an operator. Since May 2026 Bat Xat National Park has banned unorganised climbing and trekking on several peaks, including this route; entering the forest without registering can be fined. Only go with a permitted operator and check first whether the route has reopened.
When is the best time for the Nhiu Co San trek?
March, April, September, October, November.
