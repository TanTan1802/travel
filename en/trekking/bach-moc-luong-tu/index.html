Bach Moc Luong Tu (Ky Quan San) trek

Start
Sang Ma Sao village (Bat Xat)

Since May 2026 Bat Xat National Park has banned unorganised climbing and trekking on several peaks, including this route; entering the forest without registering can be fined. Only go with a permitted operator and check first whether the route has reopened.

  • The 3,046 m summit – Vietnam’s fourth highest, after Fansipan, Pu Ta Leng and Pu Si Lung
  • Sunset over Salt Mountain, the ridge on the Lao Cai – Lai Chau border
  • Seas of cloud, bamboo forest and rhododendron ridges

Day-by-day plan

  1. Day 1: Sang Ma Sao – hut

    Walk about 14 km through villages, forest and ridges to a hut at around 2,100 m.

  2. Day 2: Bach Moc Luong Tu summit – Salt Mountain

    About 4 km to the 3,046 m summit for the cloud sea; watch the sunset from Salt Mountain in the afternoon, then return to the hut.

  3. Day 3: Descent to Sang Ma Sao

    Follow the same trail about 14 km back down to the village.

Finish: Sang Ma Sao village

Costs & permits

  • Porters, local guides and hut accommodation arranged by the operator – ask exactly what is included

Safety

  • In winter the summit can drop below 0°C with ice and slippery trails – bring hiking boots, gloves and several warm layers
  • Three long days with steep climbs – only for people used to overnight high-mountain treks
  • Border area: carry ID or a passport; thick fog – always follow your guide

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Bach Moc Luong Tu (Ky Quan San) trek?
3 days, 2 nights, 30 – 36 km (up and back down the same trail; operators measure it differently).
How hard is the Bach Moc Luong Tu (Ky Quan San) trek?
Very hard: Three days or more on muddy, slippery forest trails with strong, cold winds – only for experienced high-mountain trekkers.
Do I need a guide for the Bach Moc Luong Tu (Ky Quan San) trek?
Yes – you must go with a local guide or an operator. Since May 2026 Bat Xat National Park has banned unorganised climbing and trekking on several peaks, including this route; entering the forest without registering can be fined. Only go with a permitted operator and check first whether the route has reopened.
When is the best time for the Bach Moc Luong Tu (Ky Quan San) trek?
January, February, March, October, November, December.
