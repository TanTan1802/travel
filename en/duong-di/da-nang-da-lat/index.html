Getting to Da Lat: tips

Lien Khuong Airport (DLI) is ~30 km from town. Sleeper bus or limousine from Saigon ~7 h, from Nha Trang ~3.5 h.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Da Lat from Da Nang?
About 625 km by road.
How long does it take from Da Nang to Da Lat?
Flight ~1 h 10 min; Bus / car ~10 h 30 min.
What is the cheapest way from Da Nang to Da Lat?
