Getting to Ha Giang: tips

Sleeper bus Hanoi – Ha Giang ~6–7 h (often overnight). Ride the Dong Van – Ma Pi Leng loop on a scooter or hire an "easy rider" driver.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Ha Giang from Ho Chi Minh City?
About 1930 km by road.
How long does it take from Ho Chi Minh City to Ha Giang?
Flight ~2 h 20 min; Bus / car ~29 h 30 min.
