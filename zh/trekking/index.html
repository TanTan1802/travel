难度等级

Easy Day hikes on clear trails with short climbs – fine for first-timers and older children. Moderate Two days and one night, 6 – 8 hours of walking a day, sleeping in tents or huts – train with walks and stairs for a few weeks first. Hard Summits of 2,800 – 3,100 m, long relentless climbs and narrow rocky sections – you need good fitness and an overnight trek behind you. Very hard Three days or more on muddy, slippery forest trails with strong, cold winds – only for experienced high-mountain trekkers.

装备清单

按难度列出——难度越高，需在前一级基础上增加装备。

Easy

  • Grippy shoes (hiking boots or deep-tread trainers) and thick socks
  • A light raincoat, hat and sunscreen
  • 1.5 – 2 litres of water and snacks
  • Personal medicine, plasters and insect repellent

Moderate

  • A 25 – 35 litre pack with a hip belt and a waterproof liner
  • A base layer and a warm jacket for the night
  • A head torch, spare batteries and a power bank
  • Trekking poles – they save your knees on descents
  • Dry socks and clothes in a plastic bag

Hard

  • A waterproof shell, gloves and a beanie – above 2,500 m it can drop below 5°C
  • Broken-in high-ankle boots (never new ones)
  • A sleeping bag or liner if sleeping in huts
  • Electrolyte tablets and energy food

Very hard

  • Layered clothing and spares for three wet days
  • A whistle, an emergency blanket and a full first-aid kit

安全守则

  • Go with a local guide or an operator registered with the forest managers; many areas ban unorganised trekking and can fine you.
  • Leave your route and return time with family; there is often no phone signal in the mountains.
  • Check the forecast 1 – 2 days ahead; postpone or turn back in heavy rain, storms or thick fog.
  • Never leave the group or take shortcuts; follow the guide on narrow and slippery sections.
  • Buy travel insurance that covers hiking and trekking.
  • Carry your rubbish down, only light fires where allowed and do not pick flowers or break branches.

如何选择组织方

  • Has a tour licence and is registered with the national park or local authorities.
  • Hires local porters and guides, with roughly one guide per 5 – 8 trekkers.
  • Carries first aid, has an evacuation plan and insures its guests.
  • Is willing to postpone when the weather is bad rather than push on.

距离、费用和规定为多方汇总的参考信息，可能变动——出发前请向森林管理处或组织方确认。
