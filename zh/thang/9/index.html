9月去越南哪里玩？15 个最佳目的地

Golden rice season in Mu Cang Chai and Hoang Su Phi; the 2 September holiday is busy; rains reach central Vietnam late in the month.

越南北部

  • 下龙湾 (广宁) – 北部湾中的世界自然奇观. 最佳时间：4 月 – 10 月。
  • 沙坝 (老街) – 雾中小镇与梯田. 最佳时间：9 月 – 11 月（稻谷收获季）。
  • 板约瀑布 (高平) – 东南亚最美的边境瀑布. 最佳时间：8 月 – 10 月（水量充沛、稻谷成熟）。
  • 木江界 (老街（原安沛）) – 西北最美的梯田. 最佳时间：9 月 – 10 月（稻谷成熟），5 月 – 6 月（灌水期）。
  • 河内 (首都) – 还剑湖畔的千年文化. 最佳时间：9 月 – 11 月（秋季）。
  • 吉婆岛 – 兰夏湾 (海防) – 原始兰夏湾旁的绿色海岛. 最佳时间：4 月 – 9 月。
  • 姑苏岛 (广宁) – 东北边陲未经雕琢的碧海. 最佳时间：4月 – 9月。
  • 梅州山谷 (富寿（原和平）) – 稻田山谷中宁静的泰族村寨. 最佳时间：3月 – 5月、9月 – 10月。
  • 平辽 (广宁) – “恐龙脊背”与边境芦花. 最佳时间：3月 – 4月、9月 – 11月。
  • 三岛 (富寿（原永福）) – 河内近郊云雾缭绕的山城. 最佳时间：4月 – 9月。

越南中部

  • 归仁 (嘉莱（原平定）) – 武术之乡的宁静海滨城市. 最佳时间：3 月 – 9 月。
  • 普隆 (清化) – 梯田、水车与宁静的泰族村寨. 最佳时间：5 月 – 6 月，9 月 – 10 月。

越南南部

  • 昆岛 (胡志明市（原巴地头顿）) – 原始自然与动人历史. 最佳时间：3 月 – 9 月。
  • 芹苴 – 湄公河三角洲 (芹苴) – 水上市场、果园与河上生活. 最佳时间：9 月 – 11 月（洪水季）。
  • 朱笃 – 山姆山 (安江) – 灵地与洪水季的白千层林. 最佳时间：9 月 – 11 月（洪水季）。

9月的节日与注意事项

  • Full-moon night in the old town · 会安古镇 每月 · 农历

    Every 14th lunar evening the old town switches to lantern light only.

    Check the lunar calendar to catch the full-moon night.

  • Highland market days · 河江, 沙坝 每月

    Rotating markets: Dong Van and Meo Vac (Ha Giang), Bac Ha and Can Cau (near Sa Pa) – colourful costumes, livestock trading, brocade and thang co stew.

    Most run on Sunday mornings (Can Cau: Saturday) and wind down by noon – plan to arrive by 7–8 am.

  • Sea turtle nesting season · 昆岛 5月 – 9月

    Watch turtles nest and hatchlings released on Bay Canh island.

    Must be booked in advance with Con Dao National Park.

  • Southern rainy season · 胡志明市, 芹苴 – 湄公河三角洲, 富国岛, 南游群岛, 河仙, 金瓯角 5月 – 10月

    Short afternoon downpours that pass quickly; some Saigon streets flood in heavy rain and high tides; seas around the southern islands get rough.

    Plan outdoor activities in the morning and always carry a raincoat; boats to Nam Du and Phu Quoc may be cancelled in rough seas.

  • Rough-sea season · 富国岛, 昆岛, 河仙, 吉婆岛 – 兰夏湾 6月 – 9月

    Monsoon winds bring rough seas; island tours and ferries may be cancelled.

    Keep a spare day in case boats are cancelled.

  • National Day holiday (2 Sep) 30/08 – 03/09

    An end-of-summer break with celebrations in Hanoi and other cities.

    Room prices rise – book at least 2–3 weeks ahead.

  • Vu Lan – Buddhist filial piety festival (15th day of the 7th lunar month) 8月 – 9月 · 农历

    Big pagodas hold rose-pinning ceremonies, lantern releases and prayers; the mood is solemn yet warm.

    Dress modestly at pagodas; they are very busy on the full-moon evening.

  • Ban Gioc Falls at full flow · 板约瀑布 8月 – 10月

    The falls are at their fullest and whitest, and in late September golden rice fields frame the base.

    After heavy rain the water turns muddy and rafts near the falls may stop – check the weather first.

  • Mid-Autumn Festival · 河内, 会安古镇 9月 – 10月 · 农历

    15th of the 8th lunar month: lantern-filled Hang Ma street and Hoi An, lion dances.

    Evenings are packed – park further out and walk in.

  • Golden rice terraces · 木江界, 沙坝, 河江, 普隆 05/09 – 15/10

    Terraces turn gold – best from late September to early October.

    Weekends are crowded and homestays fill up fast.

  • Mekong flood season · 芹苴 – 湄公河三角洲, 朱笃 – 山姆山 9月 – 11月

    Rising water brings linh fish, sesbania flowers and lush Tra Su forest.

    Try linh fish hotpot – only available this season.

  • Central Vietnam storm season · 顺化皇城, 岘港, 会安古镇, 风牙 – 者榜, 归仁, 富安 – 盘石滩, 李山岛, 芽庄 9月 – 11月

    Heavy rain, possible floods and typhoons; Hoi An and Hue may flood.

    Watch forecasts and book flexible tickets/rooms; island boats may be suspended.

  • Cham Kate Festival · 宁顺 – 永熙 9月 – 10月 · 农历

    The seventh month of the Cham calendar (usually September–October) – costume processions, music and dance at the Po Klong Garai and Po Rome towers.

    Check the official dates first; respect the rites and don't cut into the procession.

  • Hanoi autumn · 河内 15/09 – 15/11

    Dry air and soft golden light, milk-flower scent on Nguyen Du and Quang Trung streets; green rice flakes from Vong village and street bicycles of daisies (late October–November).

    Best in the morning around Hoan Kiem and West Lake; buy com (green rice flakes) from street vendors or Hang Than street.

  • "Flying over the golden season" paragliding festival · 木江界 9月

    When the rice ripens, paragliders launch from the top of Khau Pha Pass over the terraced valleys; visitors can fly tandem with a pilot.

    Dates change each year and depend on the weather – check local announcements; book tandem flights ahead and prefer calm mornings.

  • Nghinh Ong whale festival at Can Gio and Thang Tam · 胡志明市, 头顿 9月 – 10月 · 农历

    Mid-8th lunar month: hundreds of decorated boats escort the whale god at sea, with hat boi opera and lion dances – the biggest fishermen's festival in the south.

    The road to Can Gio jams on the main day – leave early; ask the organisers beforehand if you want to join a boat.

  • Dieu Tri Palace Banquet – Cao Dai Holy See · 西宁 – 黑婆山 9月 – 10月 · 农历

    On the 15th day of the 8th lunar month: a major Cao Dai rite drawing hundreds of thousands of white-clad followers to the Tay Ninh Holy See, with processions and ceremonies.

    Visitors watch from designated areas – dress modestly and stay quiet during the rites.

  • Bay Nui bull racing · 朱笃 – 山姆山 9月 – 10月 · 农历

    During the Khmer Sen Dolta festival: pairs of bulls dragging harrows race across muddy fields in Tri Ton and Tinh Bien – a wild, unique spectacle.

    Stay behind the safety barriers and expect mud – wear old clothes and protect your camera.
