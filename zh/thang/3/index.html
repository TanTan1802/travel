3月去越南哪里玩？25 个最佳目的地

The north warms up and bauhinia blooms in the northwest; central Vietnam turns sunny; the south is hot and dry.

越南北部

  • 木州高原 (山罗) – 翠绿茶山与白色芥菜花. 最佳时间：10 月 – 3 月。
  • 梅州山谷 (富寿（原和平）) – 稻田山谷中宁静的泰族村寨. 最佳时间：3月 – 5月、9月 – 10月。
  • 巴贝湖 (太原（原北件）) – 东北山林中越南最大的天然淡水湖. 最佳时间：3 月 – 6 月、9 月 – 10 月。
  • 平辽 (广宁) – “恐龙脊背”与边境芦花. 最佳时间：3月 – 4月、9月 – 11月。

越南中部

  • 风牙 – 者榜 (广治（原广平）) – 洞穴王国. 最佳时间：3 月 – 8 月。
  • 会安古镇 (岘港（原广南）) – 灯笼闪耀的古老商港. 最佳时间：2 月 – 5 月。
  • 岘港 (岘港) – 美溪海滩边的宜居城市. 最佳时间：3 月 – 8 月。
  • 顺化皇城 (顺化市) – 梦幻香江畔的阮朝皇城. 最佳时间：1 月 – 4 月。
  • 大叻 (林同) – 朗边高原上的万花之城. 最佳时间：11 月 – 3 月（花季）。
  • 芽庄 (庆和) – 东海明珠. 最佳时间：1 月 – 8 月。
  • 归仁 (嘉莱（原平定）) – 武术之乡的宁静海滨城市. 最佳时间：3 月 – 9 月。
  • 富安 – 盘石滩 (多乐（原富安）) – 拥有独特玄武岩海岸的“绿草黄花之地”. 最佳时间：2 月 – 8 月。
  • 李山岛 (广义) – 大海中的大蒜王国. 最佳时间：3 月 – 8 月。
  • 美奈 (林同（原平顺）) – 越南的“小撒哈拉”. 最佳时间：11 月 – 4 月。
  • 邦美蜀 (多乐) – 越南咖啡之都与西原. 最佳时间：11 月 – 3 月。
  • 宁顺 – 永熙 (庆和（原宁顺）) – 阳光、海风、葡萄园与古占塔. 最佳时间：1月 – 8月。
  • 芒登 (广义（原昆嵩）) – 西原林海中的“第二个大叻”. 最佳时间：11月 – 4月。

越南南部

  • 富国岛 (安江（原坚江）) – 拥有越南最美日落的珍珠岛. 最佳时间：11 月 – 4 月。
  • 昆岛 (胡志明市（原巴地头顿）) – 原始自然与动人历史. 最佳时间：3 月 – 9 月。
  • 胡志明市 (西贡) – 不夜的活力之城. 最佳时间：12 月 – 4 月（旱季）。
  • 头顿 (胡志明市（原巴地头顿）) – 离西贡最近的海滨城市. 最佳时间：11 月 – 4 月。
  • 河仙 (安江（原坚江）) – 泰国湾畔温柔的边境小城. 最佳时间：11 月 – 4 月。
  • 西宁 – 黑婆山 (西宁) – 南部屋脊与高台教圣座. 最佳时间：12月 – 4月。
  • 金瓯角 (金瓯) – 红树林伸向大海的最南端. 最佳时间：12月 – 4月。
  • 南游群岛 (安江（原坚江）) – 泰国湾中的原始海岛. 最佳时间：11月 – 4月。

3月的节日与注意事项

  • Full-moon night in the old town · 会安古镇 每月 · 农历

    Every 14th lunar evening the old town switches to lantern light only.

    Check the lunar calendar to catch the full-moon night.

  • Ba Den Mountain Spring Festival · 西宁 – 黑婆山 1月 – 3月 · 农历

    From Tet to the end of the first lunar month – the busiest pilgrimage season of the year.

    Buy cable car tickets ahead, go very early and avoid the full-moon day.

  • Highland market days · 河江, 沙坝 每月

    Rotating markets: Dong Van and Meo Vac (Ha Giang), Bac Ha and Can Cau (near Sa Pa) – colourful costumes, livestock trading, brocade and thang co stew.

    Most run on Sunday mornings (Can Cau: Saturday) and wind down by noon – plan to arrive by 7–8 am.

  • Perfume Pagoda Festival · 河内 2月 – 4月 · 农历

    From the 6th of the first lunar month to the end of the third – huge crowds on Yen stream.

    Avoid weekends in the first lunar month; set off by 6am.

  • Northern 'nồm' humidity · 河内, 长安 – 宁平, 下龙湾, 三岛 2月 – 4月

    Humidity near 100%: floors and walls sweat, the sky is hazy and laundry won't dry; visibility on the bay and mountains can be poor.

    Bring grippy shoes, pick rooms with a dehumidifier or air-con, and check the forecast before a scenic cruise.

  • Bai Dinh – Trang An pilgrimage season · 长安 – 宁平 2月 – 4月 · 农历

    From the 6th day of the lunar new year to the end of the 3rd lunar month Bai Dinh pagoda holds its festival; Trang An and Hoa Lu festivals bring palanquin processions and folk performances.

    Weekends and the first weeks of spring are packed – go on a weekday, early in the morning; expect queues for Trang An boats.

  • Peach and plum blossom on the stone plateau · 河江 2月 – 3月

    Pink peach and white plum blossom against stone walls and rammed-earth houses in Lung Cu, Sung La, Pho Cao and Lao Xa.

    It's still cold with drizzle – pack warm layers; ride a motorbike only if you're used to mountain passes.

  • Fishing villages' Cau Ngu (whale worship) festival · 归仁, 富安 – 盘石滩, 李山岛, 头顿 2月 – 6月 · 农历

    Central-coast fishing villages honour the whale god for a safe season at sea: boat processions, ba trao rowing songs and basket-boat races.

    Each village has its own date – ask your host or the commune office to get it right.

  • Coffee blossom season · 邦美蜀 2月 – 3月

    After the first dry-season watering, coffee farms burst into white, fragrant blossom for a few days.

    Each bloom lasts only 3–5 days – ask a farmer or your homestay to time it.

  • Cave expedition season · 风牙 – 者榜 2月 – 8月

    In the dry season, expeditions to Son Doong, Hang En and Tu Lan run, and Phong Nha and Paradise caves are fully open.

    Son Doong and Hang En tours must be booked months ahead with a fitness check; many pause from September for the flood season.

  • Buon Ma Thuot Coffee Festival · 邦美蜀 3月

    Usually every other March: coffee streets, elephant races and gong music.

    Check whether it runs that year before planning.

  • Rhododendrons on Fansipan · 沙坝 3月 – 4月

    Red, pink and white rhododendrons bloom along the trails and around Fansipan's summit; clear, dry weather makes it great trekking season.

    Trekking requires a local guide; if you take the cable car, go up early before the clouds roll in.

  • Quan The Am Festival at the Marble Mountains · 岘港 3月 – 4月 · 农历

    On the 17th–19th days of the 2nd lunar month: Bodhisattva processions, lantern displays, martial arts and human chess at the foot of the Marble Mountains.

    Climb Thuy Son early in the morning in grippy shoes – the stone steps are slippery.

  • Purple jacaranda in Da Lat · 大叻 3月 – 5月

    Jacarandas bloom along Yersin, Tran Phu and Hung Vuong streets and around Xuan Huong Lake – sunny dry-season weather.

    Best on clear mornings; bring a jacket, as mornings and evenings are still cold.

  • Kitesurfing season · 美奈 11月 – 3月

    Steady north-east monsoon winds make Mui Ne one of Asia's best-known kitesurfing spots; the beach fills with colourful kites.

    Beginners should learn at a certified school (about 3 sessions); the sea is choppy and not ideal for small children.
