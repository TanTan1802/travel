12月去越南哪里玩？12 个最佳目的地

The north is cold with possible frost on high peaks; the south and southern islands are dry and sunny – beach season in the south.

越南北部

  • 木州高原 (山罗) – 翠绿茶山与白色芥菜花. 最佳时间：10 月 – 3 月。

越南中部

  • 大叻 (林同) – 朗边高原上的万花之城. 最佳时间：11 月 – 3 月（花季）。
  • 美奈 (林同（原平顺）) – 越南的“小撒哈拉”. 最佳时间：11 月 – 4 月。
  • 邦美蜀 (多乐) – 越南咖啡之都与西原. 最佳时间：11 月 – 3 月。
  • 芒登 (广义（原昆嵩）) – 西原林海中的“第二个大叻”. 最佳时间：11月 – 4月。

越南南部

  • 富国岛 (安江（原坚江）) – 拥有越南最美日落的珍珠岛. 最佳时间：11 月 – 4 月。
  • 胡志明市 (西贡) – 不夜的活力之城. 最佳时间：12 月 – 4 月（旱季）。
  • 头顿 (胡志明市（原巴地头顿）) – 离西贡最近的海滨城市. 最佳时间：11 月 – 4 月。
  • 河仙 (安江（原坚江）) – 泰国湾畔温柔的边境小城. 最佳时间：11 月 – 4 月。
  • 西宁 – 黑婆山 (西宁) – 南部屋脊与高台教圣座. 最佳时间：12月 – 4月。
  • 金瓯角 (金瓯) – 红树林伸向大海的最南端. 最佳时间：12月 – 4月。
  • 南游群岛 (安江（原坚江）) – 泰国湾中的原始海岛. 最佳时间：11月 – 4月。

12月的节日与注意事项

  • Full-moon night in the old town · 会安古镇 每月 · 农历

    Every 14th lunar evening the old town switches to lantern light only.

    Check the lunar calendar to catch the full-moon night.

  • Highland market days · 河江, 沙坝 每月

    Rotating markets: Dong Van and Meo Vac (Ha Giang), Bac Ha and Can Cau (near Sa Pa) – colourful costumes, livestock trading, brocade and thang co stew.

    Most run on Sunday mornings (Can Cau: Saturday) and wind down by noon – plan to arrive by 7–8 am.

  • White mustard flower season · 木州高原 11月 – 12月

    Fields of white mustard flowers across Chieng Di and Na Ka.

    Some fields charge 20,000–30,000đ for photos.

  • Tea-oil camellia blossom in Binh Lieu · 平辽 11月 – 12月

    White tea-oil camellias cover the hills and reeds line the border-marker road – Binh Lieu often holds its camellia festival at this time.

    Cool, misty mornings – bring a jacket; the border-marker road is steep, so you need a solid vehicle and steady hands.

  • Pink grass season in Da Lat · 大叻 11月 – 12月

    Grassy hills at Trai Mat, Langbiang and Tuyen Lam Lake turn soft pink as the dry season begins.

    Shoot at sunrise or sunset; many hills are private land – ask first and don't trample the grass.

  • Kitesurfing season · 美奈 11月 – 3月

    Steady north-east monsoon winds make Mui Ne one of Asia's best-known kitesurfing spots; the beach fills with colourful kites.

    Beginners should learn at a certified school (about 3 sessions); the sea is choppy and not ideal for small children.

  • Dry season – peak time on Phu Quoc · 富国岛, 河仙 12月 – 2月

    Calm seas and clear skies – the most beautiful and busiest time.

    Book resorts, island tours and the cable car ahead.

  • Da Lat Flower Festival · 大叻 12月

    Usually every other December: flower parades and displays around Xuan Huong Lake.

    Coincides with year-end crowds – book early.

  • Frost season – possible snow · 沙坝 12月 – 1月

    Fansipan and O Quy Ho may see frost or snow during strong cold spells.

    Temperatures can drop below 0°C – pack a down jacket, gloves and a beanie.

  • New Year's Eve countdown · 河内, 胡志明市, 岘港 31/12

    On 31 December: open-air concerts and midnight fireworks at Hoan Kiem Lake, Nguyen Hue walking street and along the Han River.

    Arrive before 9 pm for a good spot; park well outside the centre and walk in, as many streets close to traffic.

  • Christmas season · 胡志明市, 河内, 大叻 20/12 – 25/12

    Notre-Dame and Tan Dinh churches in Saigon, St Joseph's Cathedral in Hanoi and Da Lat's Rooster Church put up nativity scenes and star lights; the streets are buzzing on the night of the 24th.

    Traffic jams around the big churches on the 24th – walk or go early, and keep quiet during mass.
