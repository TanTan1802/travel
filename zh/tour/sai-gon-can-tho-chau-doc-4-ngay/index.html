Saigon – Can Tho – Chau Doc in 4 days: the Mekong Delta

概览

A day in Saigon, Cai Rang floating market at dawn, then Tra Su cajuput forest and Sam mountain in Chau Doc.

每日行程

  1. 第 1 天: Saigon

    Independence Palace, Notre-Dame cathedral, Ben Thanh market, then a District 4 motorbike food tour.

    • Independence Palace 65k 08:00–16:30 135 Nam Kỳ Khởi Nghĩa, Quận 1
    • Notre-Dame Cathedral 免费 全天开放 01 Công xã Paris, Quận 1
    • Ben Thanh Market 免费 07:00–18:00 Lê Lợi, Quận 1
    • District 4 motorbike food tour 700k–1.2M 17:30–21:30 Đón tại khách sạn Quận 1

  2. 胡志明市 → 芹苴 – 湄公河三角洲 · 大巴 / 汽车 ~3 小时 · 160k–390k

    第 2 天: To Can Tho

    About 3.5 hours by road to Can Tho, the Binh Thuy ancient house in the afternoon, a Ninh Kieu wharf cruise at night.

    • Binh Thuy ancient house 20k 08:00–17:00 144 Bùi Hữu Nghĩa, Bình Thủy
    • Ninh Kieu wharf – river cruise 100k–200k 19:00–21:00 Hai Bà Trưng, Ninh Kiều

  3. 第 3 天: Cai Rang floating market – Phong Dien

    Boat to the floating market at 5 – 6 am, a noodle workshop, lunch in the Phong Dien orchards.

    • Cai Rang floating market 150k–250k 5–9am Bến Ninh Kiều / cầu Cái Răng
    • Sau Hoai noodle workshop 免费 07:00–17:00 Hẻm 18 Lộ Vòng Cung, Cái Răng
    • Phong Dien fruit orchards 30k–60k 07:00–18:00 Phong Điền, Cần Thơ

  4. 芹苴 – 湄公河三角洲 → 朱笃 – 山姆山 · 大巴 / 汽车 ~2 小时 · 120k–280k

    第 4 天: Chau Doc

    Drive to Chau Doc, row through the Tra Su cajuput forest, then the Lady Xu temple on Sam mountain; back to Saigon takes about 6 hours, or stay another night.

    • Tra Su cajuput forest 150k 06:30–17:00 Văn Giáo, Tịnh Biên
    • Ba Chua Xu temple 免费 05:00–22:00 Núi Sam, Châu Đốc
