Hue – Da Nang – Hoi An in 5 days: the heritage road

顺化皇城 → 岘港 → 会安古镇 · Multi-stop · 5天4夜 · 3.3M–7M / 人

更新：11/2026

概览

Hue citadel and royal tombs, over the Hai Van pass to Da Nang, then Hoi An old town and the My Son sanctuary.

行程路线
顺化皇城 (2 天) → 岘港 (1 天) → 会安古镇 (2 天)
风格
Multi-stop
时长
5天4夜
人均估算费用
3.3M–7M
住宿
Hotel, Homestay

每日行程

  1. 第 1 天: Hue citadel

    The Imperial City in the morning, Thien Mu pagoda in the afternoon, Hue folk songs on the Perfume River at night.

    • Hue Imperial City – Noon Gate, Forbidden City 200k 07:00–17:30 23 Tháng 8, Thuận Hòa, Huế
    • Thien Mu Pagoda 免费 07:00–18:00 Kim Long, Huế
    • Hue folk music on a dragon boat 100k–150k 19:00–21:00 Bến Tòa Khâm, Lê Lợi, Huế

  2. 第 2 天: Royal tombs

    Khai Dinh and Minh Mang tombs, then the Thuy Xuan incense village.

    • Khai Dinh Tomb 150k 07:00–17:30 Thủy Bằng, Hương Thủy
    • Minh Mang Tomb 150k 07:00–17:30 Hương Thọ, Hương Trà
    • Thuy Xuan incense village 免费 07:00–18:00 Thủy Xuân, Huế

  3. 顺化皇城 → 岘港 · 大巴 / 汽车 ~2 小时 · 120k–210k

    第 3 天: Hai Van pass – Da Nang

    Drive over the Hai Van pass into Da Nang, swim at My Khe in the afternoon, Han River cruise at night.

    • Hai Van pass 免费 全天开放 QL1A, Liên Chiểu
    • My Khe beach 免费 全天开放 Võ Nguyên Giáp, Sơn Trà
    • Han river cruise 150k–200k 18:00–22:00 Bến du thuyền Bạch Đằng, Hải Châu

  4. 岘港 → 会安古镇 · 大巴 / 汽车 ~1 小时 · 120k–120k

    第 4 天: Hoi An old town

    Down to Hoi An (about 45 minutes), the old town and Japanese Bridge, lanterns on the Hoai River at night.

    • Hoi An Old Town ticket 120k 07:00–21:30 Quầy vé Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai, Hội An
    • Japanese Covered Bridge 免费 08:00–21:00 Trần Phú, Hội An
    • Lanterns on the Hoai river 20k–50k 17:30–22:00 Bến sông Hoài, Hội An

  5. 第 5 天: My Son – coconut forest

    My Son sanctuary early, basket boats in the Bay Mau coconut forest, then Da Nang airport.

    • My Son Sanctuary 150k 06:00–17:00 Duy Phú, Duy Xuyên
    • Bay Mau coconut forest – basket boat 100k–150k 07:00–17:30 Cẩm Thanh, Hội An

小贴士

  • Sep – Dec is storm season and Hue and Hoi An flood easily – go Feb – Aug.
  • Fly into Hue (Phu Bai) and out of Da Nang.
