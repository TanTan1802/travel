Hue – Da Nang – Hoi An in 5 days: the heritage road · 顺化皇城 → 岘港 → 会安古镇 · 5天4夜

  1. 第 1 天: Hue citadel

    The Imperial City in the morning, Thien Mu pagoda in the afternoon, Hue folk songs on the Perfume River at night.

    • Hue Imperial City – Noon Gate, Forbidden City 200k 07:00–17:30 23 Tháng 8, Thuận Hòa, Huế
    • Thien Mu Pagoda 免费 07:00–18:00 Kim Long, Huế
    • Hue folk music on a dragon boat 100k–150k 19:00–21:00 Bến Tòa Khâm, Lê Lợi, Huế
    + 添加顺化皇城的地点（11）
    • Tu Duc Tomb 150k 07:00–17:30 Thủy Xuân, Huế
    • Dong Ba Market 免费 06:00–19:00 2 Trần Hưng Đạo, Huế
    • Chuon lagoon – Tam Giang 免费 全天开放 Phú An, Phú Vang
    • Phuoc Tich ancient village 免费 全天开放 Phong Hòa, Phong Điền
    • Thuan An beach 免费 全天开放 Thuận An, Huế
    • Bach Ma National Park – Do Quyen falls 60k 07:00–17:00 Thị trấn Phú Lộc, Huế
    • Lang Co bay 免费 全天开放 Lăng Cô, Phú Lộc
    • Hai Van pass – Hai Van Gate 免费 全天开放 Đỉnh đèo Hải Vân
    • My Khe beach 免费 全天开放 Võ Nguyên Giáp, Đà Nẵng
    • Linh Ung Pagoda, Son Tra 免费 06:00–21:30 Bãi Bụt, Sơn Trà, Đà Nẵng
    • Dragon Bridge 免费 Fire show 9pm Sat & Sun Nguyễn Văn Linh, Đà Nẵng

  2. 第 2 天: Royal tombs

    Khai Dinh and Minh Mang tombs, then the Thuy Xuan incense village.

    • Khai Dinh Tomb 150k 07:00–17:30 Thủy Bằng, Hương Thủy
    • Minh Mang Tomb 150k 07:00–17:30 Hương Thọ, Hương Trà
    • Thuy Xuan incense village 免费 07:00–18:00 Thủy Xuân, Huế

  3. 顺化皇城 → 岘港 · 大巴 / 汽车 ~2 小时 · 120k–210k

    第 3 天: Hai Van pass – Da Nang

    Drive over the Hai Van pass into Da Nang, swim at My Khe in the afternoon, Han River cruise at night.

    • Hai Van pass 免费 全天开放 QL1A, Liên Chiểu
    • My Khe beach 免费 全天开放 Võ Nguyên Giáp, Sơn Trà
    • Han river cruise 150k–200k 18:00–22:00 Bến du thuyền Bạch Đằng, Hải Châu
    + 添加岘港的地点（14）
    • Ba Na Hills – Golden Bridge 950k 07:00–22:00 Hòa Ninh, Hòa Vang 在线找票
    • French Village – Le Jardin D'Amour 免费 08:00–21:00 Đỉnh Bà Nà
    • Linh Ung Pagoda, Son Tra 免费 06:00–21:30 Bãi Bụt, Sơn Trà
    • Marble Mountains 40k 07:00–17:30 81 Huyền Trân Công Chúa, Ngũ Hành Sơn
    • Non Nuoc stone-carving village 免费 07:00–18:00 Huyền Trân Công Chúa, Ngũ Hành Sơn
    • Dragon Bridge 免费 Fire show 9pm Sat & Sun Nguyễn Văn Linh, Đà Nẵng
    • Son Tra night market 免费 17:00–23:30 Mai Hắc Đế, Sơn Trà
    • Lang Co bay 免费 全天开放 Lăng Cô, Phú Lộc, Huế
    • Tra Que vegetable village 免费 07:00–17:00 Cẩm Hà, Hội An
    • Bay Mau coconut forest – basket boat 100k–150k 07:00–17:30 Cẩm Thanh, Hội An
    • Hoi An Old Town ticket – Japanese Bridge 120k 07:00–21:30 Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai, Hội An
    • Lanterns on the Hoai river 20k–50k 17:30–22:00 Bến sông Hoài, Hội An
    • Cu Lao Cham speedboat & snorkelling 650k–800k Boats leave ~8am Cảng Cửa Đại, Hội An 在线找票
    • Bai Chong beach – Bai Lang village 免费 全天开放 Cù Lao Chàm

  4. 岘港 → 会安古镇 · 大巴 / 汽车 ~1 小时 · 120k

    第 4 天: Hoi An old town

    Down to Hoi An (about 45 minutes), the old town and Japanese Bridge, lanterns on the Hoai River at night.

    • Hoi An Old Town ticket 120k 07:00–21:30 Quầy vé Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai, Hội An
    • Japanese Covered Bridge 免费 08:00–21:00 Trần Phú, Hội An
    • Lanterns on the Hoai river 20k–50k 17:30–22:00 Bến sông Hoài, Hội An
    + 添加会安古镇的地点（13）
    • Phuc Kien Assembly Hall 免费 07:00–17:30 46 Trần Phú, Hội An
    • Tan Ky Old House 免费 08:00–18:00 101 Nguyễn Thái Học, Hội An
    • Nguyen Hoang night market 免费 17:00–22:30 Nguyễn Hoàng, An Hội
    • Cooking class – Tra Que village 500k–800k 08:00–13:00 Cẩm Hà, Hội An 在线找票
    • An Bang beach 免费 全天开放 Cẩm An, Hội An
    • Hoi An Memories show 600k–1.2M 7:30–8:30pm, closed Mon Công viên Ấn tượng Hội An, Cẩm Nam 在线找票
    • Thanh Ha pottery village 35k 08:00–17:30 Phạm Phán, Thanh Hà, Hội An
    • Kim Bong carpentry village 免费 07:00–17:00 Cẩm Kim, Hội An
    • Cu Lao Cham speedboat & snorkelling 650k–800k Boats leave ~8am Cảng Cửa Đại, Hội An 在线找票
    • Bai Chong beach – Hai Tang pagoda 免费 全天开放 Cù Lao Chàm
    • Ba Na Hills – Golden Bridge 950k 07:00–22:00 Hòa Ninh, Hòa Vang, Đà Nẵng 在线找票
    • French Village – Le Jardin 免费 08:00–21:00 Đỉnh Bà Nà
    • Dragon Bridge 免费 Fire show 9pm Sat & Sun Nguyễn Văn Linh, Đà Nẵng

  5. 第 5 天: My Son – coconut forest

    My Son sanctuary early, basket boats in the Bay Mau coconut forest, then Da Nang airport.

    • My Son Sanctuary 150k 06:00–17:00 Duy Phú, Duy Xuyên
    • Bay Mau coconut forest – basket boat 100k–150k 07:00–17:30 Cẩm Thanh, Hội An

  • Fly into Hue (Phu Bai) and out of Da Nang.
