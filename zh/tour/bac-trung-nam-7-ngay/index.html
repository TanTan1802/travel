小贴士

  • Book two one-way flights, Hanoi → Da Nang and Da Nang → Ho Chi Minh City, instead of returns; Da Nang airport is about 30 km from Hoi An.
  • The three regions have different climates: Hanoi has a cool winter, central Vietnam is wettest around October – December and the south's rainy season runs roughly May – November (mostly afternoon showers) – pack a light jacket and a rain poncho.
  • The pace is brisk – to slow down, swap Ba Na Hills for an extra day in Hoi An, or use the customise option to drop some sights.
