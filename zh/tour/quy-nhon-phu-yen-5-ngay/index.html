Quy Nhon – Phu Yen in 5 days: blue sea and rock reefs · 归仁 → 富安 – 盘石滩 · 5天4夜

  1. 第 1 天: Quy Nhon – Eo Gio

    Ghenh Rang Tien Sa, Eo Gio for sunset, then seafood at the night market.

    • Ghenh Rang – Han Mac Tu's tomb 22k 06:00–18:00 Ghềnh Ráng, Quy Nhơn
    • Eo Gio cliffs 20k 06:00–18:00 Nhơn Lý, Quy Nhơn
    • Quy Nhon night market 免费 17:00–23:00 Ngô Văn Sở, Quy Nhơn
    + 添加归仁的地点（8）
    • Xuan Dieu beachfront 免费 全天开放 Đường Xuân Diệu, Quy Nhơn
    • Twin Towers (Thap Doi) 15k 07:00–17:00 Trần Hưng Đạo, Quy Nhơn
    • Long Khanh Pagoda 免费 06:00–18:00 62 Trần Cao Vân, Quy Nhơn
    • Quang Trung Museum 20k 07:00–17:00 Phú Phong, Tây Sơn
    • Banh It Cham Towers 免费 07:00–17:00 Phước Hiệp, Tuy Phước
    • Nguyen Tat Thanh square 免费 全天开放 Nguyễn Tất Thành, Quy Nhơn
    • Cu Lao Xanh island – lighthouse 300k–500k 07:00–16:00 Cảng Quy Nhơn 在线找票
    • Nhon Ly village – basket boat to Hon Kho 100k–200k 06:00–16:00 Nhơn Hải / Nhơn Lý, Quy Nhơn

  2. 第 2 天: Ky Co – Hon Kho

    Speedboat to Ky Co and Bai Dua, then snorkelling at Hon Kho.

    • Ky Co – Bai Dua speedboat tour 350k–600k 07:00–15:00 Nhơn Lý, Quy Nhơn 在线找票
    • Hon Kho – snorkelling 50k–100k 07:00–16:00 Hòn Khô, Nhơn Hải

  3. 归仁 → 富安 – 盘石滩 · 大巴 / 汽车 ~1 小时 30 分钟 · 120k–180k

    第 3 天: To Phu Yen – Ganh Da Dia

    Drive south to Tuy An: Ganh Da Dia, Mang Lang church and sunset over O Loan lagoon.

    • Ganh Da Dia basalt columns 20k 06:00–18:00 An Ninh Đông, Tuy An
    • Mang Lang church 免费 06:00–17:00 An Thạch, Tuy An
    • Bai Mon beach – O Loan lagoon 免费 全天开放 Đông Hòa / Tuy An
    + 添加富安 – 盘石滩的地点（4）
    • Hon Yen 免费 全天开放 An Hòa Hải, Tuy An
    • Cu Lao Mai Nha island 250k–400k 07:00–15:00 Bến An Hải, Tuy An 在线找票
    • Dong Cam dam – Tuy An rice fields 免费 全天开放 Phú Hòa, Phú Yên
    • Hoa Da station – Chop Chai mountain 免费 全天开放 An Mỹ, Tuy An

  4. 第 4 天: Mui Dien – Xuan Dai bay

    Sunrise at Mui Dien, then Xuan Dai bay and Ganh Ong in the afternoon.

    • Mui Dien – Dai Lanh lighthouse 20k 05:00–17:00 Hòa Tâm, Đông Hòa
    • Xuan Dai bay – Do Do village 150k–300k 07:00–17:00 Xuân Phương, Sông Cầu 在线找票
    • Ganh Ong – Bai Xep 0k–10k 全天开放 An Chấn, Tuy An

  5. 第 5 天: Tuy Hoa

    Nhan tower and Tuy Hoa beach, then fly home from Tuy Hoa airport.

    • Nhan Tower 免费 07:00–21:00 Núi Nhạn, Tuy Hòa
    • Tuy Hoa beach 免费 全天开放 Độc Lập, Tuy Hòa
