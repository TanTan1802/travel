Resort stay

Phu Quoc resort stay + theme parks

富国岛

Two resort nights in the north of the island, a full day at VinWonders and Vinpearl Safari, and the Hon Thom sea cable car before flying home.

3天2夜 4M–10.2M
Islands & sea

Phu Quoc: An Thoi four-island tour

富国岛

A day by speedboat around the An Thoi islands: snorkelling, swimming off Hon May Rut, then the night market.

一日 840k–1.5M
Family

Nha Trang in 2 days: VinWonders + the bay islands

芽庄

Day one on Hon Tre island with the cable car and VinWonders; day two snorkelling at Hon Mun and a mineral mud bath.

2天1夜 2.1M–6.3M
Family

Da Nang – Hoi An family trip, 4 days

岘港

My Khe beach, Ba Na Hills and the Golden Bridge, Hoi An old town with basket boats and lanterns – an easy pace from one base.

4天3夜 3M–12.5M
Islands & sea

Ha Long in 2 days: bay, caves and cable car

下龙湾

A day on the bay with Sung Sot cave, kayaking and Ti Top island; next day the Queen cable car and the Sun Wheel.

2天1夜 1.9M–3.8M
Mountains & nature

Sa Pa in 3 days: Fansipan and the villages

沙坝

Cat Cat village, the cable car to the roof of Indochina, Silver Falls and a walk through the Lao Chai – Ta Van terraces.

3天2夜 2.2M–4.1M
Culture & heritage

Ninh Binh in a day: Trang An – Mua Cave – Hoa Lu

长安 – 宁平

Rowing boats through Trang An's caves and karsts, the Mua Cave climb over Tam Coc and the Hoa Lu ancient capital – a day trip from Hanoi.

一日 640k–900k
Mountains & nature

Da Lat in 3 days: lakes, tea hills and waterfalls

大叻

Cool highland air: Xuan Huong lake, a cable car over pine forest to Tuyen Lam lake, the Datanla falls coaster and cloud-hunting at Cau Dat tea hills.

3天2夜 1.6M–6.8M
Culture & heritage

Can Tho – Mekong Delta in 2 days: floating market and orchards

芹苴 – 湄公河三角洲

Ninh Kieu wharf by night, Cai Rang floating market at dawn, a noodle workshop and the Phong Dien orchards.

2天1夜 910k–2.2M
Multi-stop

Da Lat – Nha Trang in 5 days: highlands then sea

大叻 → 芽庄

Two cool days in the highlands, then down the pass to Nha Trang for Po Nagar, VinWonders and snorkelling at Hon Mun.

5天4夜 4M–8.7M
Multi-stop

Hue – Da Nang – Hoi An in 5 days: the heritage road

顺化皇城 → 岘港 → 会安古镇

Hue citadel and royal tombs, over the Hai Van pass to Da Nang, then Hoi An old town and the My Son sanctuary.

5天4夜 3.3M–7M
Multi-stop

Hanoi – Ninh Binh – Ha Long in 4 days

河内 → 长安 – 宁平 → 下龙湾

Hanoi old quarter, Trang An boats and Mua Cave, then a day on Ha Long Bay – the classic northern loop.

4天3夜 3.4M–6.4M
Multi-stop

Saigon – Can Tho – Chau Doc in 4 days: the Mekong Delta

胡志明市 → 芹苴 – 湄公河三角洲 → 朱笃 – 山姆山

A day in Saigon, Cai Rang floating market at dawn, then Tra Su cajuput forest and Sam mountain in Chau Doc.

4天3夜 3M–7.3M
Multi-stop

Quy Nhon – Phu Yen in 5 days: blue sea and rock reefs

归仁 → 富安 – 盘石滩

Eo Gio, the Ky Co speedboat and Hon Kho snorkelling in Quy Nhon, then down to Phu Yen for Ganh Da Dia, O Loan lagoon and sunrise at Mui Dien.

5天4夜 3M–6.5M
Multi-stop

Mui Ne – Da Lat in 4 days: sand dunes then pine forests

美奈 → 大叻

Red dunes, White Sand Dunes at sunrise and Fairy Stream in Mui Ne; then up to cool Da Lat for Tuyen Lam lake and Datanla falls.

4天3夜 2.1M–9.3M
Mountains & nature

The Ha Giang loop in 4 days: Quan Ba – Dong Van – Ma Pi Leng

河江

The north’s finest mountain loop: Quan Ba gate, the Vuong palace, Lung Cu flag tower, Ma Pi Leng pass and a boat on the Nho Que river.

4天3夜 1.6M–3.3M
Multi-stop

Ha Tien – Phu Quoc in 4 days: the Pirate Islands then the pearl island

河仙 → 富国岛

Seaside Ha Tien, a boat to the unspoilt Pirate Islands, then the fast ferry to Phu Quoc for the Hon Thom cable car and Sunset Town.

4天3夜 3.2M–6.8M

费用如何估算

  • 价格为每人预估，按各目的地页面的门票和房价（标有核实月份）计算：低价为最便宜的房间加平价餐饮，高价为同类最贵的房间加餐厅。
  • 不含前往目的地的机票、火车或大巴——请查看路线页面或行程规划器。
  • 度假村、乐园的套餐（住宿 + 门票）往往比单买便宜——预订前请比价。
