The Ha Giang loop in 4 days: Quan Ba – Dong Van – Ma Pi Leng · 河江 · 4天3夜

  1. 第 1 天: Ha Giang – Quan Ba – Yen Minh

    Photo at the Km0 marker, up to Quan Ba gate and the twin mountains, through the Yen Minh pine forest.

    • Ha Giang Km0 marker 免费 全天开放 Trước UBND tỉnh, TP Hà Giang
    • Quan Ba Heaven Gate – Fairy Bosom hills 免费 全天开放 Tam Sơn, Quản Bạ
    • Yen Minh pine forest 免费 全天开放 QL4C, Yên Minh
    + 添加河江的地点（2）
    • Hoang Su Phi rice terraces 免费 全天开放 Hoàng Su Phì, Hà Giang
    • Nam Hong tea hills 免费 全天开放 Nậm Hồng, Hoàng Su Phì

  2. 第 2 天: Vuong palace – Lung Cu – Dong Van

    The Vuong palace, Tham Ma slope, Lung Cu flag tower at the northern tip, then Dong Van old quarter at night.

    • Vuong family mansion 20k 07:00–17:30 Sà Phìn, Đồng Văn
    • Tham Ma slope 免费 全天开放 QL4C, Phố Cáo
    • Lung Cu flag tower 25k 07:00–17:30 Lũng Cú, Đồng Văn
    • Dong Van old quarter 免费 全天开放 Thị trấn Đồng Văn

  3. 第 3 天: Ma Pi Leng – Nho Que river

    Over Ma Pi Leng pass, a boat through Tu San canyon on the Nho Que, then the Meo Vac – Du Gia road.

    • Ma Pi Leng pass – Tu San canyon 免费 全天开放 QL4C, Đồng Văn – Mèo Vạc
    • Nho Que river boat 150k–200k 07:00–17:00 Bến thuyền Tà Làng, Mèo Vạc
    • Meo Vac – Du Gia road 免费 全天开放 Mèo Vạc – Yên Minh

  4. 第 4 天: Du Gia – back to Ha Giang

    Swim at Du Gia waterfall in the morning, then back to Ha Giang town.

    • Du Gia waterfall 免费 全天开放 Du Già, Yên Minh
