越南节庆与活动日历：按月份整理的 62 个节日、花季和丰收季

传统节日、花季、稻谷成熟季、人多的假期和需要注意的天气——按月份整理，帮你选对出游时间。

1月 7

  • Ba Den Mountain Spring Festival · 西宁 – 黑婆山 1月 – 3月 · 农历

    From Tet to the end of the first lunar month – the busiest pilgrimage season of the year.

    Buy cable car tickets ahead, go very early and avoid the full-moon day.

  • Nguyen Hue Tet flower street · 胡志明市 1月 – 2月 · 农历

    For about a week around Tết, Nguyen Hue walking street becomes a flower avenue with the zodiac mascot of the year and dazzling lights at night.

    Go early in the morning (6–8 am) for photos without crowds; evenings are packed.

  • H'Mong Gau Tao festival · 沙坝, 河江 1月 – 2月 · 农历

    In early spring: a ceremonial pole, khen pipes, pao ball throwing, top spinning and horse racing – the biggest blessing festival of the H'Mong.

    Ask your homestay host which village holds it and when; ask before taking close-up photos of people.

  • Plum and peach blossom season · 木州高原 1月 – 2月

    Na Ka valley turns white with plum blossom, dotted with pink peach.

    It is cold and misty – bring warm layers.

  • Cherry blossom season · 大叻 1月 – 2月

    Pink cherry blossoms around Xuan Huong Lake and Cau Dat tea hills.

    Often overlaps with Tết – book early.

  • Cherry blossom in Mang Den · 芒登 1月 – 2月

    Cherry blossoms paint the town and pine-forest edges pink, often with a blossom festival and fair at the start of the year.

    Timing varies with the weather and lasts 1–2 weeks – follow local news before booking.

  • Lunar New Year (Tết) · 全国 1月 – 2月 · 农历

    The biggest holiday of the year: flower markets, busy temples and a festive mood.

    Many shops close for 3–5 days; transport and rooms get much pricier – book 1–2 months ahead.

1月最佳目的地

2月 8

  • Perfume Pagoda Festival · 河内 2月 – 4月 · 农历

    From the 6th of the first lunar month to the end of the third – huge crowds on Yen stream.

    Avoid weekends in the first lunar month; set off by 6am.

  • Giong Festival at Soc Temple · 河内 2月 · 农历

    On the 6th day of the lunar new year in Soc Son: bamboo-elephant processions and bamboo-flower offerings – a UNESCO-listed intangible heritage festival.

    About 40 km from central Hanoi; go early and mind your belongings in the scramble for lucky bamboo flowers.

  • Bai Dinh – Trang An pilgrimage season · 长安 – 宁平 2月 – 4月 · 农历

    From the 6th day of the lunar new year to the end of the 3rd lunar month Bai Dinh pagoda holds its festival; Trang An and Hoa Lu festivals bring palanquin processions and folk performances.

    Weekends and the first weeks of spring are packed – go on a weekday, early in the morning; expect queues for Trang An boats.

  • Fishing villages' Cau Ngu (whale worship) festival · 归仁, 富安 – 盘石滩, 李山岛, 头顿 2月 – 6月 · 农历

    Central-coast fishing villages honour the whale god for a safe season at sea: boat processions, ba trao rowing songs and basket-boat races.

    Each village has its own date – ask your host or the commune office to get it right.

  • Peach and plum blossom on the stone plateau · 河江 2月 – 3月

    Pink peach and white plum blossom against stone walls and rammed-earth houses in Lung Cu, Sung La, Pho Cao and Lao Xa.

    It's still cold with drizzle – pack warm layers; ride a motorbike only if you're used to mountain passes.

  • Coffee blossom season · 邦美蜀 2月 – 3月

    After the first dry-season watering, coffee farms burst into white, fragrant blossom for a few days.

    Each bloom lasts only 3–5 days – ask a farmer or your homestay to time it.

  • Cave expedition season · 风牙 – 者榜 2月 – 8月

    In the dry season, expeditions to Son Doong, Hang En and Tu Lan run, and Phong Nha and Paradise caves are fully open.

    Son Doong and Hang En tours must be booked months ahead with a fitness check; many pause from September for the flood season.

  • Northern 'nồm' humidity · 河内, 长安 – 宁平, 下龙湾, 三岛 2月 – 4月

    Humidity near 100%: floors and walls sweat, the sky is hazy and laundry won't dry; visibility on the bay and mountains can be poor.

    Bring grippy shoes, pick rooms with a dehumidifier or air-con, and check the forecast before a scenic cruise.

2月最佳目的地

3月 4

  • Buon Ma Thuot Coffee Festival · 邦美蜀 3月

    Usually every other March: coffee streets, elephant races and gong music.

    Check whether it runs that year before planning.

  • Quan The Am Festival at the Marble Mountains · 岘港 3月 – 4月 · 农历

    On the 17th–19th days of the 2nd lunar month: Bodhisattva processions, lantern displays, martial arts and human chess at the foot of the Marble Mountains.

    Climb Thuy Son early in the morning in grippy shoes – the stone steps are slippery.

  • Rhododendrons on Fansipan · 沙坝 3月 – 4月

    Red, pink and white rhododendrons bloom along the trails and around Fansipan's summit; clear, dry weather makes it great trekking season.

    Trekking requires a local guide; if you take the cable car, go up early before the clouds roll in.

  • Purple jacaranda in Da Lat · 大叻 3月 – 5月

    Jacarandas bloom along Yersin, Tran Phu and Hung Vuong streets and around Xuan Huong Lake – sunny dry-season weather.

    Best on clear mornings; bring a jacket, as mornings and evenings are still cold.

3月最佳目的地

4月 7

  • Hue Festival · 顺化皇城 4月 – 6月

    Royal arts and culture events – the main edition is usually held in even years.

    Check the official schedule; central hotels sell out early.

  • Khau Vai Love Market · 河江 4月 – 5月 · 农历

    On the 26th–27th of the third lunar month in Meo Vac – a unique highland gathering.

    Book Meo Vac homestays early; mountain roads get busy.

  • Ly Son Feast for the Hoang Sa soldiers · 李山岛 4月 – 5月 · 农历

    In mid-3rd lunar month: a ceremony honouring the historic Hoang Sa flotilla, with paper boats released to the sea – a national intangible heritage.

    Ferries fill up during the festival – book ahead and watch for cancellations in rough seas.

  • Po Nagar Tower Festival · 芽庄 4月 – 5月 · 农历

    On the 20th–23rd days of the 3rd lunar month: statue bathing and offering dances by Viet and Cham communities at the ancient Cham towers by the Cai River.

    Wear clothes covering shoulders and knees; go in the morning to avoid the harsh sun.

  • Easter lily season · 河内 01/04 – 30/04

    Throughout April, white Easter lilies ride on flower-sellers' bicycles across the Old Quarter – Hanoi's signature sign of the changing season.

    Buy bunches at Quang An flower market or from bicycle vendors – cheaper and fresher than shops.

  • Reunification Day & Labour Day holiday · 全国 28/04 – 03/05

    A long early-summer break; beaches and places near big cities get crowded.

    Book early and avoid road travel on the first and last day of the break.

  • Hung Kings' Commemoration (10th day of the 3rd lunar month) · 全国 4月 · 农历

    A national public holiday; when it falls next to a weekend or the 30 April break it becomes a long weekend and places near Hanoi get crowded.

    Check the official holiday dates each year and book early if it falls next to a weekend.

4月最佳目的地

5月 7

  • Ba Chua Xu Festival at Sam Mountain · 朱笃 – 山姆山 5月 – 6月 · 农历

    The 23rd–27th of the fourth lunar month – one of the biggest festivals in the south.

    Very crowded – stay in Chau Doc, go early and watch your belongings.

  • Water-pouring season · 木江界, 普隆, 沙坝 10/05 – 15/06

    Flooded terraces mirror the sky as planting begins.

    Go early morning or late afternoon for the best light.

  • Sea turtle nesting season · 昆岛 5月 – 9月

    Watch turtles nest and hatchlings released on Bay Canh island.

    Must be booked in advance with Con Dao National Park.

  • Golden rice at Tam Coc · 长安 – 宁平 15/05 – 10/06

    Golden rice lines the Ngo Dong River between limestone peaks – the year's prettiest moment for the Tam Coc boat ride.

    Take the boat early or after 3 pm to avoid the heat; climb Hang Mua for the panorama.

  • Moc Chau plum harvest · 木州高原 5月 – 6月

    Orchards hang heavy with purple-red plums; visitors can pick their own and buy straight from the trees.

    Agree on the price before picking; go in the cool morning and bring a hat.

  • Mekong orchard fruit season · 芹苴 – 湄公河三角洲, 朱笃 – 山姆山 5月 – 8月

    Durian, rambutan, mangosteen and mango are all in season – many orchards in Phong Dien and Cai Rang let you eat straight from the trees.

    Ask the entry price (usually covers eating on site; take-away is extra) and bring mosquito repellent.

  • Southern rainy season · 胡志明市, 芹苴 – 湄公河三角洲, 富国岛, 南游群岛, 河仙, 金瓯角 5月 – 10月

    Short afternoon downpours that pass quickly; some Saigon streets flood in heavy rain and high tides; seas around the southern islands get rough.

    Plan outdoor activities in the morning and always carry a raincoat; boats to Nam Du and Phu Quoc may be cancelled in rough seas.

5月最佳目的地

6月 3

  • Da Nang International Fireworks Festival · 岘港, 会安古镇 6月 – 7月

    International teams put on shows over the Han River on weekend nights.

    Buy stand tickets or grab a riverside spot early; rooms get pricey.

  • Summer peak season · 下龙湾, 吉婆岛 – 兰夏湾, 岘港, 芽庄, 归仁, 富安 – 盘石滩, 头顿, 富国岛, 李山岛, 昆岛, 美奈, 宁顺 – 永熙, 姑苏岛 6月 – 8月

    School holidays make beaches their busiest of the year.

    Travel midweek and book early for better prices.

  • Rough-sea season · 富国岛, 昆岛, 河仙, 吉婆岛 – 兰夏湾 6月 – 9月

    Monsoon winds bring rough seas; island tours and ferries may be cancelled.

    Keep a spare day in case boats are cancelled.

7月最佳目的地

8月 3

  • Vu Lan – Buddhist filial piety festival (15th day of the 7th lunar month) · 全国 8月 – 9月 · 农历

    Big pagodas hold rose-pinning ceremonies, lantern releases and prayers; the mood is solemn yet warm.

    Dress modestly at pagodas; they are very busy on the full-moon evening.

  • Ban Gioc Falls at full flow · 板约瀑布 8月 – 10月

    The falls are at their fullest and whitest, and in late September golden rice fields frame the base.

    After heavy rain the water turns muddy and rafts near the falls may stop – check the weather first.

  • National Day holiday (2 Sep) · 全国 30/08 – 03/09

    An end-of-summer break with celebrations in Hanoi and other cities.

    Room prices rise – book at least 2–3 weeks ahead.

8月最佳目的地

9月 10

  • Mid-Autumn Festival · 河内, 会安古镇 9月 – 10月 · 农历

    15th of the 8th lunar month: lantern-filled Hang Ma street and Hoi An, lion dances.

    Evenings are packed – park further out and walk in.

  • Cham Kate Festival · 宁顺 – 永熙 9月 – 10月 · 农历

    The seventh month of the Cham calendar (usually September–October) – costume processions, music and dance at the Po Klong Garai and Po Rome towers.

    Check the official dates first; respect the rites and don't cut into the procession.

  • "Flying over the golden season" paragliding festival · 木江界 9月

    When the rice ripens, paragliders launch from the top of Khau Pha Pass over the terraced valleys; visitors can fly tandem with a pilot.

    Dates change each year and depend on the weather – check local announcements; book tandem flights ahead and prefer calm mornings.

  • Nghinh Ong whale festival at Can Gio and Thang Tam · 胡志明市, 头顿 9月 – 10月 · 农历

    Mid-8th lunar month: hundreds of decorated boats escort the whale god at sea, with hat boi opera and lion dances – the biggest fishermen's festival in the south.

    The road to Can Gio jams on the main day – leave early; ask the organisers beforehand if you want to join a boat.

  • Dieu Tri Palace Banquet – Cao Dai Holy See · 西宁 – 黑婆山 9月 – 10月 · 农历

    On the 15th day of the 8th lunar month: a major Cao Dai rite drawing hundreds of thousands of white-clad followers to the Tay Ninh Holy See, with processions and ceremonies.

    Visitors watch from designated areas – dress modestly and stay quiet during the rites.

  • Bay Nui bull racing · 朱笃 – 山姆山 9月 – 10月 · 农历

    During the Khmer Sen Dolta festival: pairs of bulls dragging harrows race across muddy fields in Tri Ton and Tinh Bien – a wild, unique spectacle.

    Stay behind the safety barriers and expect mud – wear old clothes and protect your camera.

  • Golden rice terraces · 木江界, 沙坝, 河江, 普隆 05/09 – 15/10

    Terraces turn gold – best from late September to early October.

    Weekends are crowded and homestays fill up fast.

  • Mekong flood season · 芹苴 – 湄公河三角洲, 朱笃 – 山姆山 9月 – 11月

    Rising water brings linh fish, sesbania flowers and lush Tra Su forest.

    Try linh fish hotpot – only available this season.

  • Hanoi autumn · 河内 15/09 – 15/11

    Dry air and soft golden light, milk-flower scent on Nguyen Du and Quang Trung streets; green rice flakes from Vong village and street bicycles of daisies (late October–November).

    Best in the morning around Hoan Kiem and West Lake; buy com (green rice flakes) from street vendors or Hang Than street.

  • Central Vietnam storm season · 顺化皇城, 岘港, 会安古镇, 风牙 – 者榜, 归仁, 富安 – 盘石滩, 李山岛, 芽庄 9月 – 11月

    Heavy rain, possible floods and typhoons; Hoi An and Hue may flood.

    Watch forecasts and book flexible tickets/rooms; island boats may be suspended.

9月最佳目的地

10月 1

  • Buckwheat flower season · 河江 10月 – 11月

    Pink-purple buckwheat flowers cover the hills around Dong Van and Sung La.

    Weekends peak – go midweek if you can.

10月最佳目的地

11月 5

  • White mustard flower season · 木州高原 11月 – 12月

    Fields of white mustard flowers across Chieng Di and Na Ka.

    Some fields charge 20,000–30,000đ for photos.

  • Wild sunflower season · 大叻, 邦美蜀 11月

    Wild sunflowers line the Highlands roads, Prenn pass and Langbiang.

    Sunny and cool – ideal for a motorbike ride.

  • Tea-oil camellia blossom in Binh Lieu · 平辽 11月 – 12月

    White tea-oil camellias cover the hills and reeds line the border-marker road – Binh Lieu often holds its camellia festival at this time.

    Cool, misty mornings – bring a jacket; the border-marker road is steep, so you need a solid vehicle and steady hands.

  • Pink grass season in Da Lat · 大叻 11月 – 12月

    Grassy hills at Trai Mat, Langbiang and Tuyen Lam Lake turn soft pink as the dry season begins.

    Shoot at sunrise or sunset; many hills are private land – ask first and don't trample the grass.

  • Kitesurfing season · 美奈 11月 – 3月

    Steady north-east monsoon winds make Mui Ne one of Asia's best-known kitesurfing spots; the beach fills with colourful kites.

    Beginners should learn at a certified school (about 3 sessions); the sea is choppy and not ideal for small children.

11月最佳目的地

12月 5

  • Da Lat Flower Festival · 大叻 12月

    Usually every other December: flower parades and displays around Xuan Huong Lake.

    Coincides with year-end crowds – book early.

  • New Year's Eve countdown · 河内, 胡志明市, 岘港 31/12

    On 31 December: open-air concerts and midnight fireworks at Hoan Kiem Lake, Nguyen Hue walking street and along the Han River.

    Arrive before 9 pm for a good spot; park well outside the centre and walk in, as many streets close to traffic.

  • Christmas season · 胡志明市, 河内, 大叻 20/12 – 25/12

    Notre-Dame and Tan Dinh churches in Saigon, St Joseph's Cathedral in Hanoi and Da Lat's Rooster Church put up nativity scenes and star lights; the streets are buzzing on the night of the 24th.

    Traffic jams around the big churches on the 24th – walk or go early, and keep quiet during mass.

  • Frost season – possible snow · 沙坝 12月 – 1月

    Fansipan and O Quy Ho may see frost or snow during strong cold spells.

    Temperatures can drop below 0°C – pack a down jacket, gloves and a beanie.

  • Dry season – peak time on Phu Quoc · 富国岛, 河仙 12月 – 2月

    Calm seas and clear skies – the most beautiful and busiest time.

    Book resorts, island tours and the cable car ahead.

12月最佳目的地

全年 2

  • Full-moon night in the old town · 会安古镇 每月 · 农历

    Every 14th lunar evening the old town switches to lantern light only.

    Check the lunar calendar to catch the full-moon night.

  • Highland market days · 河江, 沙坝 每月

    Rotating markets: Dong Van and Meo Vac (Ha Giang), Bac Ha and Can Cau (near Sa Pa) – colourful costumes, livestock trading, brocade and thang co stew.

    Most run on Sunday mornings (Can Cau: Saturday) and wind down by noon – plan to arrive by 7–8 am.
