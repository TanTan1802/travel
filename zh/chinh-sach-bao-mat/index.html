隐私政策

更新：11/2026

Summary

  • Viet Travel has no user accounts, uses no advertising cookies and never sells or shares your data.
  • Most features (favourites, trip plans, packing lists) run in your browser and are stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

  • Your browser’s storage (localStorage) keeps: favourite destinations, trip plans, light / dark theme, ticked packing items and itineraries saved for offline use.
  • A cache (service worker) keeps some pages so the site works offline; weather forecasts are remembered for the session.
  • None of this is sent to a server. To delete it, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Visitor statistics

  • The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits: pages viewed, referring site, country, device and browser type – in aggregate.
  • It sets no cookies, does no browser fingerprinting and does not track you across other sites.

Your location

  • The "Near me" button asks for your location to sort destinations by distance. The calculation happens on your device; your location is never sent or stored. You can decline and still use the site.

Third-party services

  • When a page uses these, your browser connects to the service directly (they see your IP address, as with any website): Open-Meteo (weather – sends the destination’s coordinates, not yours), OpenStreetMap and cdnjs (maps), Wikimedia Commons (some photos).
  • Buttons for Booking.com, Airbnb, Klook, Google Maps, Google Calendar and Vexere just open their sites; their own policies apply from there.

Information you send us

  • "Report an error" opens a pre-filled GitHub Issue – its content and your GitHub account are public on GitHub.
  • Community photos are also submitted through a public GitHub Issue; they are published only after review, with location data (EXIF / GPS) removed and credited as you ask.

Contact and deletion requests

  • For questions or to have something you sent removed, open an Issue at https://github.com/TanTan1802/travel/issues.
