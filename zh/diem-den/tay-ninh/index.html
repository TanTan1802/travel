为什么要去西宁 – 黑婆山？

西宁距胡志明市约 100 公里，拥有海拔 986 米、被称为“南部屋脊”的黑婆山：乘缆车登顶，可参拜灵验的婆寺建筑群，欣赏云海之上的西补陀山佛像。造型独特、仪式庄严的高台教圣座是独一无二的体验，当地的露晒米纸和虾盐也很有名。

节日与季节亮点

  • Ba Den Mountain Spring Festival 1月 – 3月 · 农历

    From Tet to the end of the first lunar month – the busiest pilgrimage season of the year.

    Buy cable car tickets ahead, go very early and avoid the full-moon day.

  • Dieu Tri Palace Banquet – Cao Dai Holy See 9月 – 10月 · 农历

    On the 15th day of the 8th lunar month: a major Cao Dai rite drawing hundreds of thousands of white-clad followers to the Tay Ninh Holy See, with processions and ceremonies.

    Visitors watch from designated areas – dress modestly and stay quiet during the rites.

农历节日每年日期不同——出发前请确认官方日期。

如何前往西宁 – 黑婆山

From Saigon: bus or limousine van about 2–2.5 h (~100 km) on Highway 22, many departures daily; or hire a car for a day trip.
