为什么要去沙坝？

海拔 1600 米的沙坝四季凉爽，层层梯田如通往天空的阶梯，赫蒙族和红瑶族文化丰富多彩。这里也是通往“印度支那屋脊”番西邦峰的门户。

最佳季节 本月 所选月份

节日与季节亮点

  • H'Mong Gau Tao festival 1月 – 2月 · 农历

    In early spring: a ceremonial pole, khen pipes, pao ball throwing, top spinning and horse racing – the biggest blessing festival of the H'Mong.

    Ask your homestay host which village holds it and when; ask before taking close-up photos of people.

  • Highland market days 每月

    Rotating markets: Dong Van and Meo Vac (Ha Giang), Bac Ha and Can Cau (near Sa Pa) – colourful costumes, livestock trading, brocade and thang co stew.

    Most run on Sunday mornings (Can Cau: Saturday) and wind down by noon – plan to arrive by 7–8 am.

  • Rhododendrons on Fansipan 3月 – 4月

    Red, pink and white rhododendrons bloom along the trails and around Fansipan's summit; clear, dry weather makes it great trekking season.

    Trekking requires a local guide; if you take the cable car, go up early before the clouds roll in.

  • Water-pouring season 10/05 – 15/06

    Flooded terraces mirror the sky as planting begins.

    Go early morning or late afternoon for the best light.

  • Golden rice terraces 05/09 – 15/10

    Terraces turn gold – best from late September to early October.

    Weekends are crowded and homestays fill up fast.

  • Frost season – possible snow 12月 – 1月

    Fansipan and O Quy Ho may see frost or snow during strong cold spells.

    Temperatures can drop below 0°C – pack a down jacket, gloves and a beanie.

农历节日每年日期不同——出发前请确认官方日期。
