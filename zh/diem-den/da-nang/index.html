为什么要去岘港？

岘港集海、山与现代都市于一身。美溪海滩被评为全球最具吸引力的海滩之一，巴拿山的金桥闻名世界，龙桥每个周末都会喷火。

节日与季节亮点

  • Quan The Am Festival at the Marble Mountains 3月 – 4月 · 农历

    On the 17th–19th days of the 2nd lunar month: Bodhisattva processions, lantern displays, martial arts and human chess at the foot of the Marble Mountains.

    Climb Thuy Son early in the morning in grippy shoes – the stone steps are slippery.

  • Summer peak season 6月 – 8月

    School holidays make beaches their busiest of the year.

    Travel midweek and book early for better prices.

  • Da Nang International Fireworks Festival 6月 – 7月

    International teams put on shows over the Han River on weekend nights.

    Buy stand tickets or grab a riverside spot early; rooms get pricey.

  • Central Vietnam storm season 9月 – 11月

    Heavy rain, possible floods and typhoons; Hoi An and Hue may flood.

    Watch forecasts and book flexible tickets/rooms; island boats may be suspended.

  • New Year's Eve countdown 31/12

    On 31 December: open-air concerts and midnight fireworks at Hoan Kiem Lake, Nguyen Hue walking street and along the Han River.

    Arrive before 9 pm for a good spot; park well outside the centre and walk in, as many streets close to traffic.

农历节日每年日期不同——出发前请确认官方日期。

如何前往岘港

Da Nang Airport (DAD) is just ~3 km from the centre, taxi/Grab ~80–120k VND. Da Nang Station is on the North–South line.
