为什么要去邦美蜀？

坐落在红色玄武岩高原上的城市，被誉为越南咖啡之都。从这里出发，可探访壮观的德雷努瀑布、勒湖、埃地族和墨侬族村落，以及被列为联合国教科文组织非物质文化遗产的西原锣钲文化。

节日与季节亮点

  • Coffee blossom season 2月 – 3月

    After the first dry-season watering, coffee farms burst into white, fragrant blossom for a few days.

    Each bloom lasts only 3–5 days – ask a farmer or your homestay to time it.

  • Buon Ma Thuot Coffee Festival 3月

    Usually every other March: coffee streets, elephant races and gong music.

    Check whether it runs that year before planning.

  • Wild sunflower season 11月

    Wild sunflowers line the Highlands roads, Prenn pass and Langbiang.

    Sunny and cool – ideal for a motorbike ride.

农历节日每年日期不同——出发前请确认官方日期。

如何前往邦美蜀

Buon Ma Thuot Airport (BMV) is ~8 km from the centre. Sleeper buses take ~8 h from Saigon and ~4–5 h from Da Lat or Nha Trang.
