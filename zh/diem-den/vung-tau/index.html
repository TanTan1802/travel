概览

为什么要去头顿？

头顿距胡志明市只有约 2 小时车程，是人气周末度假地，有后滩、前滩、小山上的耶稣像和古老的灯塔。这里以酥脆的小米饼（bánh khọt）和新鲜海鲜闻名。

节日与季节亮点

  • Fishing villages' Cau Ngu (whale worship) festival 2月 – 6月 · 农历

    Central-coast fishing villages honour the whale god for a safe season at sea: boat processions, ba trao rowing songs and basket-boat races.

    Each village has its own date – ask your host or the commune office to get it right.

  • Summer peak season 6月 – 8月

    School holidays make beaches their busiest of the year.

    Travel midweek and book early for better prices.

  • Nghinh Ong whale festival at Can Gio and Thang Tam 9月 – 10月 · 农历

    Mid-8th lunar month: hundreds of decorated boats escort the whale god at sea, with hat boi opera and lion dances – the biggest fishermen's festival in the south.

    The road to Can Gio jams on the main day – leave early; ask the organisers beforehand if you want to join a boat.

农历节日每年日期不同——出发前请确认官方日期。
