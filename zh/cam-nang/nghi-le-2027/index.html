Vietnam public holidays 2027 and where to go

Seven days off for Tet, four for 30 April and National Day – every 2027 holiday, where the weather is good and how to book in peak season.

更新于：2026/10

目录
  1. The 2027 holidays
  2. Where to go for each holiday
  3. Booking tips for peak holidays

The 2027 holidays

The Tet and National Day dates below are the schedules approved for civil servants; private companies may choose different days under the Labour Code. Updated October 2026 – check the official announcement before booking.

  • New Year: Friday 1 – Sunday 3 January 2027 (3 days).
  • Lunar New Year (Tet, year of the Goat): Thursday 4 – Wednesday 10 February 2027 (7 days); the first day of Tet is Saturday 6 February.
  • Hung Kings Commemoration Day (10th day of the 3rd lunar month): Friday 16 – Sunday 18 April 2027 (3 days).
  • 30 April – 1 May: Friday 30 April – Monday 3 May 2027 (4 days – 1 May falls on a Saturday so Monday is a day in lieu; awaiting official confirmation).
  • National Day: Thursday 2 – Sunday 5 September 2027 (4 days for civil servants; businesses add either 1 or 3 September).
  • Vietnamese Culture Day, 24 November (Wednesday): one day off expected.

Where to go for each holiday

  • New Year (early January): the South is in its dry season – Phu Quoc, Con Dao, Can Tho; for cool weather try Sa Pa or Da Lat.
  • Tet (February): plum and peach blossom in Moc Chau and Ha Giang, but it is cold; the Centre and South are sunny – Hoi An, Quy Nhon, Phu Quoc. Many eateries close for the first three days.
  • Hung Kings Day (mid-April): pleasant weather in the North and fewer crowds than 30 April – Ninh Binh, Mai Chau, Ba Be Lake, Ha Long.
  • 30 April – 1 May (4 days): the busiest time of year at beaches near Hanoi and Saigon (Vung Tau, Cat Ba, Sam Son). Go a little further for space: Quy Nhon, Phu Yen, Ly Son, Con Dao.
  • National Day (early September): rice harvest season in Mu Cang Chai, Sa Pa and Ha Giang; Central Vietnam enters the typhoon season – watch the forecast.

Booking tips for peak holidays

  • Book Tet flights and trains as early as possible – fares rise fast and routes home sell out first.
  • Leaving a day or two before (or returning after) the holiday is much cheaper and avoids traffic jams out of the cities.
  • Choose rooms with free cancellation – book now and change once your plans are fixed.
  • Beaches and mountains near the big cities fill up early for 30 April and 2 September; highland homestays usually ask for a bank-transfer deposit.
