What to eat in Vietnam: a food guide by region

Must-try dishes in each region, how to eat street food safely and a few table manners.

更新于：2026/10

目录
  1. The North: clean, subtle flavours
  2. Central: bold, spicy, lots of small dishes
  3. The South: sweeter, generous, lots of herbs
  4. Eating street food safely

The North: clean, subtle flavours

  • Beef pho, bun cha, turmeric fish, rice rolls and egg coffee in Hanoi.
  • Ha Long squid cakes, Hai Phong crab noodles, thang co and free-range pork in the highlands.

Central: bold, spicy, lots of small dishes

  • Hue beef noodle soup, banh beo, nam and loc, and clam rice in Hue.
  • Mi Quang and pork rice-paper rolls in Da Nang; cao lau, chicken rice and banh mi in Hoi An.
  • Fish-cake noodles and grilled pork rolls in Nha Trang; banh can in Da Lat and Phan Thiet.

The South: sweeter, generous, lots of herbs

  • Broken rice, hu tieu, banh mi and banh xeo in Saigon.
  • Fermented-fish hotpot, shrimp fritters and orchard fruit in Can Tho and Chau Doc.
  • Bun quay, herring salad and Ham Ninh crab in Phu Quoc.

Eating street food safely

  • Pick stalls busy with locals and food cooked hot in front of you.
  • Drink bottled water; ice in cities is usually factory-made.
  • Vegetarians can look for quán chay – especially busy on the 1st and 15th of the lunar month.
  • At the table: never stand chopsticks upright in rice; invite elders to start first.
