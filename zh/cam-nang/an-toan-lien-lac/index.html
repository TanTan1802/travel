Safety, health and staying connected

Emergency numbers, SIM/eSIM, apps to install, avoiding scams and staying healthy on the road.

更新于：2026/10

目录
  1. Emergency numbers
  2. SIM, eSIM and useful apps
  3. Avoiding scams
  4. Staying healthy

Emergency numbers

  • 113 – Police
  • 114 – Fire and rescue
  • 115 – Ambulance

SIM, eSIM and useful apps

The main networks are Viettel (best coverage in mountains and islands), VinaPhone and MobiFone. Buy a tourist SIM/eSIM at the airport or an official store; you need your passport/ID to register.

  • Google Maps – directions, opening hours and reviews.
  • Grab / Be / Xanh SM – rides and food delivery.
  • Zalo – the most popular messaging app; many homestays and bus companies use it.

Avoiding scams

  • Use ride-hailing apps or metered taxis from major companies; agree prices before cyclo or motorbike taxi rides.
  • Check bills and count your change.
  • Book tours with clearly reviewed companies; be wary of cheap tours offered on the street.

Staying healthy

  • The sun in the Centre and South is strong: stay hydrated, use sunscreen and avoid 11 am – 2 pm.
  • Use mosquito repellent, especially in the rainy season (dengue).
  • Bring personal medicine and motion-sickness pills for mountain passes and island boats.
  • Watch typhoon forecasts for islands and Central Vietnam from Sep – Dec.
